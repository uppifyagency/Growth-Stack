## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

**The thesis.** Copywriting = "words on a page that convert better" — the highest-impact skill, the difference between rejection and a sale. Same envelope, only the words change the result. Beat **sitting-down-at-the-computer-itis** (going stiff/formal at a keyboard): write **casual copy** — to one friend at lunch, suit jacket on, never the goofy jackass. Editing test for any wacky line: **"Does it add to the message?"** No → cut. If you're bored reading it, cut it.

**The two non-negotiable mindsets.** (1) **No one cares about you** — open with the reader's problem, not the founder story; sell the **benefit, not the feature**. (2) **Give-give-give, not ask-ask-ask** — lead outreach with usable value (free report, vetted vendors); remove every ask from first contact to flip reply rates from near-zero to >50%. Write to **Bob** — one composite-average buyer profiled from real data; "sell to everyone and you've sold to no one."

**AIDA + the Slippery Slide (the spine of everything).** **Attention → Interest → Desire → Action.** Type the four labels down the page, write one sentence under each, then expand: Attention (headline/question/name), Interest (3–7 facts/benefits — cap ~7), **Desire** (logical ROI math AND emotional status/relief/fear), Action (ONE instruction). Each line's only job is to get the next line read — the **slippery slide** — fueled by a story or genuine education. End on **one** call to action; readers execute one action well, so kill button-soup. Make the action a **BFB** (obvious from 10 steps back in 1 second) and add a bolded P.S.

**Headlines & subject lines.** 8/10 read the headline, 2/10 read on — it decides if anything else is read. Generate variants with the **CBI Method** (Competitive / Benefit-driven / Inspirational, one lens per buyer psychology) or mine **socially-proven headlines** (upvoted Reddit/Quora/Digg/Twitter titles + Google "searches related to") into a swipe file — relevant, never clickbait. **Authentic > bombastic**: email subjects short, lowercase, casual, mobile-safe; never a coupon-in-subject. Canon: Gary Halbert (Boron Letters) → Ogilvy → Sugarman. **Sugarman's length rule**: cheap/known item → short copy; expensive/complex → long, but only if every section earns it.

**Cold sales emails.** The **4 Steps**: personalize (real name = +50–100% readership) · give something OR keep it short · use the **Cold Email Outline** (= AIDA: intro / what it does for them / what happens if they get it / when can we talk) · end with one bolded ask. Be a **Giving Gary** (lead with the reader's quantified benefit — "$24k/year saved", backed by a custom proof artifact) not a **Leeching Larry** (droning company history). Conversion math: 200 sends/mo at 3-in-10 vs 1-in-10 = 3× sales, same effort. When you're the product, lead with "what I'll do for you," demote credentials to bullets.

**Sales pages & eCommerce.** Default skeleton = **Modified AIDA (AIDA + T)** — AIDA with Testimonials dropped in (work by mass, not individually). **Match length to buyer knowledge**: branded/Googled product → buy button; unfamiliar → full sequence. Build long pages by **stacking** short per-block sections. **Steal the Amazon field set** incl. the boring mandatory block (dimensions, weight, power, box contents, shipping, compatible products) — answering every question on-page kills ~80% of support calls; **each product page IS a landing page**. **Work backwards from the one goal**: confirm the CTA repeats per section and closes; show don't tell (varied case studies, reason-why); selling is often done beforehand — fix the email sequence first.

**Content marketing.** Run the **70/30 Rule** (70% useful content, 30% sales; help first, sell last) and pair every **feature with a benefit** for first-time buyers. Win the **long tail**, never fight incumbents head-on; treat each post as a **timeless, appreciating asset** (edit it live forever) and beat **the dip**. Salvage dead posts with the **4-Step Content Revamp**: angle → headline (write 4, keep slug) → image → **Blog Content Modules** (TOC, custom score, map, calculator, generator, original-data tables, shareable templates, gated PDF, CTA). Every post ends with ONE next action; own your website, social is borrowed traffic.

**Email & autoresponders.** Engineer every email into Gary Halbert's **A-pile** (plain/minimal look, useful, recognized sender) not the thumb-deleted **B-pile** — the sender's reputation outranks the subject line. **Follow-ups** = the evergreen interval-timed sequence (the money-maker; 0, +1, +2, +3 days); **broadcasts** = one-time sends. Use the email AS the content, **drop hints** sparingly, end each on a **cliffhanger**, and route feedback via a **whitelisting PS**. **Constant Launch**: strip dates from launch broadcasts → load into a follow-up → every signup relives the launch forever ("sell while you sleep").

**Ads, website tuneups & images.** Search ads: the **3 AdWords Rules** — know the intent (fuse competitor angles into a **super ad**), keep it to the point (no cleverness), buyers only (every click costs money); match the landing page (**ad scent**). The **Bleary-Eyes Read** (5-sec test): is "what is this / what do I do" instantly clear? One page = one goal; a landing page = one CTA top and bottom. Explain confusing offers with a literal **1-2-3 how-it-works** block. Use **heat maps** (Crazy Egg) to find the **deep-blue-of-death** where readers quit; fix that section. Transparent watermarked **PNGs**, built vertical/mobile-first.

**Getting clients (becoming a copywriter).** The **$100 Goal** + **cheap, fast, dirty** — first $100 removes fear as the real obstacle (no website, no LLC). Use the **Lazy Google Doc Portfolio**, post your offer to **friends & family first**, then Craigslist/Gumtree/Thumbtack. **Three-Pronged Pricing** ($100 floor / ~$500 mid / $2k+ top); when leads flood, **double the price** instead of removing the post, and **restrict the scope**. Cold email in 1–2 sentences then **CALL**. Document before/after proof; go broad first and **let the market niche you down**.

**Products, courses & promotions.** Every promo = **The 3-Email Black Friday Sequence**: Introduce (offer + discount + deadline + CTA) → **Cool Angle** (proof: testimonials/numbers/story) → close-the-door scarcity (1–2 sentences). Lean on **reusability** (write once from existing assets, reuse everywhere) and pre-schedule it. **Digital product leverage**: build a course in 3–4 weeks, sell it forever (Terminator 2 model); ship on the simplest platform, charge enough, judge by **buyer ROI not completion rate**. Too cheap to discount? **Reverse Black Friday** (price going up). No list? Sell to friends/family first.

**Interview wisdom (Ch 12–13).** **One Call To Action** and content built from **writing modules** make posts **timeless** and defensible vs **the dip**; **Make Content, Get Links** reduces SEO to link-worthy content + personalized outreach (~10-year horizon). **Own the asset** (blog + email list compound; Amazon owns *your* customer); **validate before producing** (Kickstarter pre-sell). Bigger picture: **email + YouTube are the only two channels that matter** (FB organic reach 90%→~4%); pick content by **FFR** (Fast, Fun, Repeatable, already-proven) inside the **Sex-Cash** Venn; build the audience first, then find what to sell. Escape the **Sea of Sameness** with a **content moat**; make every line **earn its pixels**; **B2B is human-to-human** — kill buzzwords; decide by data + experienced gut, not **HIPPO**.

Tools, platform features (AWeber, Crazy Egg, Gumroad, AppSumo, Kickstarter, etc.) and pricing date fast — verify current names and numbers before relying on a specific one. For applying this to real copy, pair it with hands-on practice and live teardowns of your own pages.
