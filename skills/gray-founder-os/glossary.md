# Glossary — Founder OS (Matt Gray)

**4 E's of Copywriting** — Educate, Entertain, Engage, Emotion; the pillars of online copy (Ch 4).
**90-Day Plan** — the nearest objective horizon on the Vision Board, broken across People/Product/Process (Ch 3).
**AIDA funnel** — Awareness → Interest → Desire → Action; the marketing engine's path (Ch 4).
**Automate, Eliminate, Delegate** — operating discipline: automate repeatable work, cut non-core tasks, delegate anything someone can do 80% as well as you (Ch 3, Ch 5).
**Best-in-class examples** — reference pieces that set the quality bar for each content format, used to brief/onboard writers (Ch 4).
**Brand values** — 3–5 values that are the brand's personality and content filter; deliberately distinct from personal values (Ch 4).
**Build in public** — sharing wins, failures, tools, and lessons to compound community, mentors, and luck (Ch 4).
**Category King** — owning (or inventing) a category and being its pioneer/leader (Ch 2).
**Client Health Survey** — Typeform survey to gauge PMF and mine customer language for copy (Ch 5).
**Company Wiki** — Notion knowledge base of all company systems; new hires read it line by line (Ch 5).
**Compounding** — small daily content/outreach reps that snowball over years ("ninth wonder of the world") (Ch 4).
**Content Categories** — the ~4 topic pillars all content ladders up to (Ch 4).
**Content Formats** — the ~3 content types (how-tos, guides, lists, stories…) (Ch 4).
**Content Franchise** — recurring content at a fixed cadence/time people know and love (e.g. Saturday newsletter) (Ch 4).
**Content Funnel** — Rented → Owned → Monetized audience (Ch 4).
**Content Playbook** — the two-page content strategy artifact (Ch 4).
**Cost of a bad hire** — ~$70K+ plus culture damage; rationale for the scorecard system (Ch 5).
**Done is better than perfect** — bias to action; "experimenting is better than perfection" (all modules).
**Edutainment** — 50% educational / 50% entertaining content (Ch 4).
**Embrace the suck** — accept being bad early; commit to volume, not the first attempts (Ch 4).
**Emotion dial** — emotions to engineer into content: LOL, WTF, aww, NSFW, aha, yay, "finally" (Ch 4).
**Every day is day one** — daily tenacity as a core value (Ch 1, Ch 5).
**Flow** — ~4 hrs/day of protected deep work; mornings are sacred (Ch 4, Ch 5).
**Founder OS Framework** — the 4-module formula: Operating System → Vision & Niche → Community → Scale (Ch 1).
**Founder coaching** — "entrepreneurs are athletes"; get a bi-weekly coach (Ch 5).
**Give-give-get** — provide value first (e.g. a Loom audit), then ask; leverages reciprocity (Ch 5).
**Hell yes / full-body yes** — opportunity filter: if not a head+heart+gut yes, it's a no (Ch 2).
**The Hook** — the first sentence; "80% of content success"; engineered to be scroll-stopping (Ch 4).
**Ikigai** — Japanese "reason for being"; intersection of love / world-needs / paid-for / good-at (Ch 2).
**Indigestion not starvation** — startups fail from saying yes to too much, not too little (Ch 2, Ch 4).
**Leverage Triangle** — People, Product, Process — the three axes of scale (Ch 3, Ch 5).
**Make something people want** — Y Combinator product principle (Ch 5).
**Mental clarity system** — Gray's routine: walks, sunlight, weekly/quarterly/annual reflection, soul trips (Ch 4).
**Mission & culture fit** — required competency for every hire (Ch 5).
**Monetization Stack** — multiple revenue models layered on a niche (newsletter + community + program + SaaS) (Ch 2).
**Monthly Personal Board Meeting** — month-start personal+business goals; month-end reflection (well/not/learned) (Ch 3).
**Net worth = your network** — outreach-driven relationship building as a growth lever (Ch 5).
**Owned audience** — email/SMS subscribers (usually a newsletter) you control (Ch 4).
**Personal Board of Advisors** — curated mentors (~80% of success = 5 people around you) (Ch 3, Ch 5).
**PMF (Sean Ellis) test** — ≥60% of users "very disappointed" to lose the product = strong product-market fit (Ch 5).
**Profitable Niche Formula** — Skills + Interests + Monetization = profitable niche of one (Ch 2).
**Profitability is beauty** — preference for a profitable niche business over raise-big-burn-big (Ch 2).
**Proven systems** — distilled, reusable processes; the program's core value (Ch 1).
**Rented audience** — followers on platforms you don't own (Twitter/LinkedIn/YouTube) (Ch 4).
**Revenue Matrix** — checklist to drive each platform → newsletter → product (Ch 5).
**Role Scorecard** — role + mission + outcomes + competencies; internal hiring filter (Ch 5).
**Soul trip / life resume** — a bucket-list trip every ~6 weeks (from Jesse Itzler) (Ch 4, Ch 5).
**Stand on the shoulders of giants** — study/remix proven content from top creators (Ch 4).
**Super Bowl is won in the preseason** — plan tomorrow's 9 tasks tonight (Ch 5).
**Surface area of luck** — output (content/outreach) expands serendipity, connections, customers (Ch 1).
**SMART goals** — Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound; all goals must be objective (Ch 3).
**Systems Legend** — Founder OS identity: "smarter, leaner, faster, free" (Ch 1).
**TWEMEX** — Chrome extension to find a creator's top tweets for inspo/remix (Ch 4).
**Two platforms then domino** — master 2 platforms, then repurpose winners outward (Ch 4).
**Vision Board (Founder OS)** — one-page artifact: values, niche/vision/mission, marketing system, 10/3/1-yr + 90-day goals, opportunities (Ch 3).
**You're only as strong as your systems** — the program's central thesis (Ch 1, Ch 5).
