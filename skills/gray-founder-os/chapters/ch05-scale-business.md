# Chapter 5: Module 4 — Scale Your Business

## Core Idea
Scale by building **leverage** across **People, Product, Process** — hire legends with a rigorous system, build a product people would be devastated to lose, and systemize everything so your brain transfers into the company.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **The Leverage Triangle — People / Product / Process.**
- **PEOPLE — Hiring system:**
  - **Role Scorecard** (internal): role · mission · **outcomes** (measurable) · **competencies**. Acts as an instant filter.
  - **Competencies by changeability**: easy to change / harder-but-doable / very-difficult — don't hire missing the hard-to-change ones; coach the easy ones.
  - **Job Description** (external A+ template): mission · problem · solution · traction · role overview · 30/60/90-day expectations · who should apply · team. **Jedi tip: steal/mimic billion-dollar companies' careers pages.**
  - **Interview question bank** (signature ones): "What motivates/frustrates you?" · "Are you a storyteller?" · "Would you ever want to be a CEO?" (ownership test) · "**What separates a *great* from a *good* [role]?**" · "What book would you recommend & why?" · Peter Thiel's "**What important truth do very few people agree with you on?**"
  - **Mission & culture fit** is required for every role; **cost of a bad hire ≈ $70K+** plus culture damage. Raise the bar with every hire.
- **PRODUCT:**
  - **"Make something people want"** (Y Combinator).
  - **Sean Ellis PMF test**: survey customers — "How would you feel if you could no longer use [product]?" **≥60% "very disappointed" = strong product-market fit**; ~20% = keep refining.
  - **Client Health Survey** (Typeform): mine customer *language* for landing-page/email copy; capture main benefit + NPS-style "how likely to recommend."
  - **Revenue Matrix**: systematically drive each social platform → newsletter → product (featured links, first-comment links, lead magnets, testimonials, high-purchase-intent content).
- **PROCESS:**
  - **Automate, Eliminate, Delegate** — automation 1000×'s the business; be ruthless cutting non-core tasks.
  - **Company Wiki** (Notion): the company's knowledge base; new hires go through it **line by line** to "put your brain in their brain." Build ~a couple systems per week.
  - **Personal Board of Advisors + Outreach system** (see Patterns).
  - **Founder coaching**: "entrepreneurs are athletes — every great athlete has a coach." Find a bi-weekly coach (formal or informal).

## Key Concepts
- **"You are only as strong as your systems"** — every business area (tech, design, sales, HR, ops, finance…) needs a scalable, documented system.
- **Referrals / viral loop**: best growth channel; aim for each customer to refer ~3 others; use testimonials + case studies as social proof.
- **Net worth = your network**; ~80% of success = the 5 people you surround yourself with.
- **Give-give-get / reciprocity**: provide value first (e.g. a Loom audit) before asking — reciprocity is a powerful bias.
- **Outreach tooling**: TextExpander (`;int` snippet), iPhone text replacement, Yesware (Gmail templates). Gray does ~50 outreaches/day.

## Mental Models
- **"The Super Bowl is won in the preseason"**: plan the next day's nine tasks the night before; mornings are sacred (flow, no meetings, phone off).
- **Mental health is wealth**: investing in soul/health/spirituality grows cash flow; audit your daily/weekly schedule.
- **Creation process**: curate inspiration the night before → wake with energy after 8 hrs sleep → produce → walk/break → edit.

## Anti-patterns
- **Bad/rushed hires**: skipping the scorecard → ~$70K+ waste + culture rot.
- **Building in a vacuum / no customer surveys**: you won't know if you have PMF or what copy resonates.
- **Hoarding tasks instead of systemizing**: blocks leverage; if you do it repeatedly, document it into the wiki and delegate.

## Key Takeaways
1. Build scorecards + JDs before hiring; filter on hard-to-change competencies and culture fit.
2. Survey customers; push "very disappointed" past 60%; harvest their language for copy.
3. Systemize relentlessly into a company wiki; automate/eliminate/delegate.
4. Run a daily ~50-outreach, value-first network engine; get a coach and a board of advisors.
5. Consistency is a daily activity — win task by task; refer/affiliate to grow the community.
