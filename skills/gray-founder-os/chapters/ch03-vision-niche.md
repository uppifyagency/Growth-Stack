# Chapter 3: Module 2 — Create a Clear Vision & Own Your Niche

## Core Idea
A clear, written vision aligns customers, community, team, and investors — and it's built by **reverse-engineering** from a 10-year dream back down to what you do this month. The artifact is the **Founder OS Vision Board**, kept alive by a **Monthly Personal Board Meeting**.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Founder OS Vision Board** (one page you reverse-engineer top-down):
  1. **Company values** (used to hire, set culture, and steer content)
  2. **Niche / Vision / Mission**
  3. **Marketing system**: target market · unique value proposition · unique/proven process · guarantee
  4. **10-year goals → 3-year → 1-year → 90-day plan**
  5. **Opportunities**, broken out across People / Product / Process
  - How: state the 10-yr dream, then back into each shorter horizon. Print it (mirror, desk, bedside) to reinforce daily.
- **People / Product / Process — the Leverage Triangle**: the three axes on which to build leverage; used to translate 90-day goals into concrete moves.
- **SMART goals**: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound. Every goal must be objective ("audience to 200K," not "great audience") — red light / green light.
- **Monthly Personal Board Meeting**: at month start, set ~5 personal + ~5 business 30-day goals (SMART, laddering to 90-day). At month end, reflect: **what went well / what didn't / what did I learn.** Keep your Ikigai at the top.
- **Personal Board of Advisors**: list the mentors/people (from Twitter, LinkedIn, books) you need around you; enlist them once you have a clear vision to show.

## Key Concepts
- **Automate, eliminate, delegate**: a core company value and operating discipline (expanded in Ch 5).
- **Make magic possible / invent and wander / optimize for flow**: example company values driving culture and content.
- **5 pillars for 90-day goals**: marketing, community, revenue, team, product (not exhaustive — finance/HR exist too).
- **Vision as a recruiting & alignment tool**: people follow a leader with a clear vision; only hire those who fit your values and can deliver the vision.

## Mental Models
- Goals are a **ladder**: every daily task → 30-day → 90-day → 1-yr → 3-yr → 10-yr vision. No task should feel purposeless.
- **Reverse-engineer, don't forecast**: decide the destination, then derive the steps.
- **Reflection is a tool**: monthly + quarterly reviews create a tight accountability loop so the vision board doesn't die on paper.

## Anti-patterns
- **Subjective goals** ("be amazing") — can't tell if you hit them. Always attach a number + date.
- **Set-and-forget vision board**: without the monthly board meeting, nothing executes.
- **Perfectionism**: "experimenting is better than perfection" — V1 now, refine every 90 days.

## Key Takeaways
1. Fill the Vision Board: values, vision/mission/niche, marketing system, 10/3/1-yr + 90-day goals, opportunities.
2. Make every goal SMART and objective.
3. Run a Monthly Personal Board Meeting (personal + business goals + end-of-month reflection).
4. Draft your Personal Board of Advisors.
5. Print the vision board and keep it in sight; ~80% of success comes from the mentors around you.
