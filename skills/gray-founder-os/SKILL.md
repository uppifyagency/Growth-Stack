## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

**The thesis**: *"You're only as strong as your systems."* Build proven, reusable systems instead of relying on hustle — they manufacture luck, leverage, and freedom.

**The Founder OS Framework (4 modules):**
1. **Operating System / Calling** — find your why, then a profitable niche.
2. **Vision & Niche** — a written, reverse-engineered vision.
3. **Grow Your Community** — a consistent content engine + audience funnel.
4. **Scale** — leverage across People, Product, Process.

**Ikigai** — your calling sits where four circles overlap: what you **love** · what the **world needs** · what you can be **paid for** · what you're **good at**. Journal 2–3 bullets each, revisit, find recurring words.

**Profitable Niche Formula** — `Skills + Interests + Monetization Strategy`. Then become the **Category King** (own a category, or invent one). Layer a **monetization stack** (e.g. newsletter + community + program + SaaS).

**Values as a filter** — say **no to 90%** so you can say "**hell yes**" (full-body yes) to what matters. *"Startups die of indigestion, not starvation."*

**Founder OS Vision Board** — reverse-engineer from a 10-yr dream → 3-yr → 1-yr → **90-day** → opportunities across **People/Product/Process**. Every goal is **SMART** (objective: a number + a date). Keep it alive with a **Monthly Personal Board Meeting** (personal + business goals; month-end reflection: went well / didn't / learned).

**Content engine** — a two-page **Content Playbook**: brand values (3–5, distinct from personal), 4 categories, 3 formats, **content franchises** (recurring, known cadence), best-in-class examples. Drive the **Content Funnel: Rented → Owned (newsletter) → Monetized**. Copy via the **4 E's** (Educate, Entertain, Engage, Emotion); the **hook is 80%** of success; **edutainment** 50/50; **curate → remix**; nail **2 platforms then domino**; build a 30–60-day queue; **embrace the suck** and compound.

**Scale via the Leverage Triangle (People/Product/Process):**
- **People** — hire with a **Role Scorecard** (mission/outcomes/competencies) → **JD** (mimic billion-$ careers pages) → interview bank; require mission+culture fit; a bad hire ≈ $70K+.
- **Product** — *"make something people want"* (YC); validate with the **Sean Ellis PMF test** (≥60% "very disappointed" to lose it); mine customer language for copy; build a referral/viral loop.
- **Process** — **Automate, Eliminate, Delegate** (delegate the 80%-as-good); a **Company Wiki** (Notion) onboards hires line-by-line; **value-first outreach** (give-give-get) builds your network ("net worth = network"; ~80% of success = your 5 people); get a **coach** (entrepreneurs are athletes).

**Daily operating rhythm** — plan tomorrow's 9 tasks tonight ("Super Bowl won in the preseason"); ~4 hrs of protected **flow**, sacred mornings; weekly/quarterly/annual reflection; a **soul trip** every ~6 weeks. *Done > perfect; consistency is a daily activity.*
