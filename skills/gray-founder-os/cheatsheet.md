# Founder OS — Cheatsheet

## The 4-Module Framework
1. **Operating System** → Ikigai + profitable niche
2. **Vision & Niche** → Vision Board + SMART goals
3. **Grow Community** → Content Playbook + funnel
4. **Scale** → People / Product / Process leverage

## Ikigai (4 circles)
Love · World needs · Paid for · Good at → overlap = your calling.
**Niche = Skills + Interests + Monetization.** Be the **category king**.

## Vision Board (reverse-engineer)
Values → Niche/Vision/Mission → Marketing system (target · UVP · process · guarantee) → **10yr → 3yr → 1yr → 90-day** → Opportunities (People/Product/Process).
Goals must be **SMART** (objective: a number + a date).

## Content Funnel
**Rented** (Twitter/LinkedIn/YouTube) → **Owned** (newsletter/email) → **Monetized** (paying customers).

## Copywriting
- **4 E's**: Educate · Entertain · Engage · Emotion.
- **Hook = 80%** of success (1st sentence sells the 2nd).
- **Emotion dial**: LOL · WTF · aww · NSFW · aha · yay · "finally."
- **Edutainment** = 50/50. **Curate → remix.** Nail **2 platforms**, then domino.

## PMF Test (Sean Ellis)
"How would you feel if you could no longer use it?" → **≥60% "very disappointed" = PMF.** ~20% = refine.

## Hiring
**Scorecard** (role · mission · outcomes · competencies) → **JD** (mimic billion-$ careers pages) → **interview bank**.
Competencies: easy / harder / very-hard to change. Require **mission + culture fit**. Bad hire ≈ **$70K+**.

## Operating Principles
- You're only as strong as your **systems**.
- **Automate, eliminate, delegate** (delegate the 80%-as-good).
- **Done > perfect**; consistency is a daily activity.
- **Net worth = network**; ~80% of success = your 5 people.
- **Give-give-get** (value first → ask). ~1–50 outreaches/day.
- **Super Bowl won in the preseason**: plan 9 tasks the night before; ~4 hrs flow, sacred mornings.
- **Embrace the suck**; compounding wins the 5–10 yr game.
