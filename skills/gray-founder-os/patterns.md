# Patterns & Techniques — Founder OS

## Ikigai Discovery
**When to use**: choosing a calling / what business to commit to.
**How**: journal 2–3 bullets in each circle — what you **love**, what the **world needs**, what you can be **paid for**, what you're **good at**. Revisit over several days; circle recurring words; the overlap is your Ikigai.
**Trade-offs**: introspective and fuzzy; treat as a living doc, not a one-time answer.

## Profitable Niche Formula
**When to use**: turning a calling into income.
**How**: `Skills + Interests + Monetization Strategy`. Pick something you're amazing at + lose track of time doing, then attach 1–2 monetization models (SaaS, community, course, 1:1, newsletter, book…).
**Trade-offs**: may take experiments to find the right monetization pairing.

## Founder OS Vision Board (reverse-engineering)
**When to use**: setting direction and aligning team/community.
**How**: write the 10-year dream → back into 3-year → 1-year → 90-day → opportunities (People/Product/Process). Make every goal SMART. Print and keep visible.
**Trade-offs**: revisit every 90 days; not stone.

## Monthly Personal Board Meeting
**When to use**: monthly execution cadence.
**How**: month start — set ~5 personal + ~5 business SMART goals laddering to the 90-day plan (Ikigai at top). Month end — reflect: what went well / what didn't / what I learned. Quarterly review every 90 days.
**Trade-offs**: requires discipline; this is what keeps the vision board alive.

## Two-Page Content Playbook
**When to use**: standing up a content/marketing engine.
**How**: Page 1 — mission/vision/niche, target market, brand values (3–5), 4 categories, 3 formats, curation sources, franchises, best-in-class examples, creator inspo, tech stack. Page 2 — content funnel (rented → owned → monetized).
**Trade-offs**: a living doc; prune categories/formats that underperform after ~30 days of data.

## Hook Engineering + Curate-and-Remix
**When to use**: writing any post/thread/email/video.
**How**: save content that stops *your* scroll (bookmarks/saves/collections/TWEMEX); study the hook pattern; remix into your voice. First sentence's only job is the second sentence. Optimize for ≥1 emotion (LOL/WTF/aww/aha/yay/"finally").
**Trade-offs**: remix ≠ copy; always reframe in your brand voice.

## Nail-2-Platforms-Then-Domino
**When to use**: choosing distribution.
**How**: focus all energy on 2 platforms; once winning, repurpose top content outward (e.g. Twitter → LinkedIn → YouTube Shorts → Reels/TikTok). Batch-produce a 30–60-day queue.
**Trade-offs**: resist spreading thin early ("indigestion").

## Hiring System (Scorecard → JD → Interview)
**When to use**: any new hire.
**How**: (1) Scorecard — role, mission, measurable outcomes, competencies; tag competencies easy/harder/very-hard to change. (2) JD — mimic billion-dollar careers pages; include mission, problem, solution, traction, 30/60/90-day expectations. (3) Interview from the question bank; require mission+culture fit.
**Trade-offs**: slower upfront, but a bad hire costs ~$70K+ and culture.

## Sean Ellis PMF Survey
**When to use**: validating/refining a product.
**How**: ask customers "How would you feel if you could no longer use [product]?" Target ≥60% "very disappointed." Mine the verbatim main-benefit language for landing-page/email copy.
**Trade-offs**: needs a real user base (start with ~8–10 customers).

## Company Wiki Onboarding
**When to use**: scaling the team / preserving knowledge.
**How**: document repeatable work in Notion (~a couple systems/week). New hires read it line by line in month one — "put your brain in their brain."
**Trade-offs**: builds over years; start now, expect it to be thin on day one.

## Value-First Outreach Engine (Give-Give-Get)
**When to use**: building a network / board of advisors / partnerships.
**How**: list target legends → find a way to provide value first (Loom audit, doc, product feedback) → then ask for a short call. Use templates: TextExpander `;int`, iPhone text replacement, Yesware for Gmail. Aim ~1/day (up to ~50/day at scale).
**Trade-offs**: reciprocity works only if the value is genuine and customized.

## Daily Operating Rhythm
**When to use**: sustaining output without burnout.
**How**: plan tomorrow's 9 tasks tonight ("Super Bowl won in preseason"); 8 hrs sleep; sacred mornings of ~4 hrs flow (phone off); curate → create → walk/break → edit; weekly/quarterly/annual reflection; a soul trip every ~6 weeks.
**Trade-offs**: rigid mornings; adapt the hours to your life (3 hrs > 0).
