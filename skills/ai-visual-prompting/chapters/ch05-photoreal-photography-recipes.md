# Chapter 5: Photoreal Recipes — Cats, Product & Lifestyle

## Core Idea
Three reusable photoreal categories, each anchored by a **Master Prompt** (general creative direction) and refined by specific example prompts. Adapt by swapping subject, lighting, lens, or styling.

## The Master-Prompt Pattern
Every category opens with a Master Prompt you customize, not a fixed prompt. Structure of a Master Prompt: subject + range of camera/lens techniques + range of lighting setups + physical-detail emphasis + background guidance + target mood.

## Category 1 — Captivating Cat Portraits
**Master Prompt:** *Generate a photorealistic photograph capturing a cat in an unusual, character-rich, or dynamically posed situation. Employ specific camera techniques (extreme close-up, fisheye distortion, shallow depth of field) and lighting setups (dramatic studio light, soft window light, harsh flash) to evoke a distinct mood. Emphasize fur texture, eye expression, subtle movement, and the cat's interaction with its environment. Background complements the scene (detailed interior to minimalist). Final image: cinematic, expressive, potentially humorous or surreal.*

**Exemplar (Siamese Typing):** *A photorealistic 1x1 square-format image captured from the perspective of a silver MacBook's built-in webcam, showing a Siamese cat with cream-colored fur and bright green eyes aggressively typing on the keyboard. The cat's face is extremely close to the camera, heavily distorted by a super-exaggerated fisheye effect — nose and eyes oversized, head curving outward toward the lens. One paw is blurred mid-strike. The keyboard and silver MacBook frame appear bent at the bottom due to the lens. Motion blur trails from paw and head. Dust flecks, fingerprint smudges, subtle screen reflections give a tactile analog texture. Slight RGB fringing and light scratches simulate a worn webcam lens under artificial indoor lighting. Photorealistic Style. Resolution 1024x1024.*

What makes it work: specific POV (webcam) + extreme lens effect + dynamic action + sensory analog detail. Levers to vary: breed, action, perspective, mood.

## Category 2 — Modern Product Photography
**Master Prompt:** *Create a high-end product photograph for a contemporary ad/editorial. Central product, key features/textures/form shown through meticulous lighting (crisp studio, soft gradients, dramatic highlights) and composition (minimalist flat lay, dynamic close-up, floating objects). Complementary props/surfaces/backgrounds enhance the story. Specific lens effects (sharp focus + creamy bokeh, macro). Optionally integrate branding/taglines. Final image: polished, premium, aspirational, audience-targeted.*

**Exemplar (MILK-inspired flat lay):** *high-end beauty product flat lay featuring a metallic-blue cosmetic tube with holographic block-letter logo reading "MILK," soft matte body with subtle gloss edges, diagonally placed on deep teal satin fabric, shimmer gel oozing from the open tube, silver disco balls, transparent holographic confetti, tipped frosted martini glass, chrome high heel in background reflecting cool blue tones, crisp studio lighting with icy blue gels, soft shadows, ultra-sharp cinematic focus using a 50mm lens, creamy bokeh, top text "GRIP. GLOW. HOLIDAY.", bottom "STAYS ON. TURNS HEADS." in modern editorial font, premium winter campaign for a Gen Z beauty brand, minimal but bold, photorealistic.*

What makes it work: named material + composition + props + studio lighting + lens + on-image text layout + audience. Levers: product, fabric, props, lighting tone, brand style (Glossier, Fenty).

## Category 3 — Cool Lifestyle Shots
**Master Prompt:** *Generate a dynamic, atmospheric lifestyle photo of a person in an authentic/stylized everyday or leisure context. Compelling perspectives (low-angle fisheye, top-down, intimate close-up) and evocative lighting (harsh desert sun, golden hour, neon, poolside reflections) convey energy and narrative. Capture motion, candid expression, subject–environment interaction. Emphasize clothing, tattoos, accessories, textures. Final image tells a visual story — raw, editorial, cinematic, slightly gritty.*

Exemplar levers (e.g., fisheye desert portrait, poolside dive, top-down fall): peak action + specific styling (pink durag, retro tattoos, Oakley shades) + harsh flash/disposable-flash + analog artifacts.

## Recurring Techniques (cross-category)
- **Fisheye editorial** — exaggerated distortion + close subject for surreal intimacy.
- **Analog imperfection layer** — lens dirt, scratches, dust, RGB fringing, film grain → "less sterile."
- **Flat lay** — top-down product on a textured surface with props.
- **Motion-blur vs sharp-focus contrast** — blur the moving part, keep the hero detail crisp.

## Anti-patterns
- Naming a vibe without naming materials/lighting → generic.
- Over-stacking props until the hero subject is lost.

## Key Takeaways
1. Start from the Master Prompt, then swap one lever at a time.
2. The analog-imperfection layer is what reads as "real."
3. On-image text and brand references steer commercial polish.
