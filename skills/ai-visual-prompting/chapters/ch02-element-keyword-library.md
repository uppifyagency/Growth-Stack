# Chapter 2: The Element Keyword Library

## Core Idea
Each of the 9 prompt layers has a reusable vocabulary bank. Strong prompting is mostly pulling the right concrete keyword from the right bank — not inventing prose.

## How to use
For each layer in your prompt skeleton (Ch 1), grab 1–3 keywords from the matching bank below. Mix specificity levels: one technical term + one evocative adjective per layer.

## Subject & Core Concept
- **People**: elderly botanist, cybernetic athlete, freckled child, tattooed bartender
- **Objects**: antique brass compass, holographic smartphone, dilapidated victorian dollhouse
- **Creatures**: spectral fox, bioluminescent jellyfish, mechanical hummingbird, scaled dragon

## Action & Interaction
leaping across · meticulously crafting · dissolving into · erupting from · intertwining with · hovering above · peering through · cascading down · emerging from shadow · colliding with · transforming into · reflecting upon · disintegrating slowly

## Setting & Environment
- **Locations**: abandoned space station, vibrant coral reef, brutalist architecture, verdant rainforest
- **Weather**: thick fog, electrical storm, gentle snowfall, scorching heat wave, monsoon rain
- **Time**: twilight, blue hour, golden morning, midnight, magic hour, harsh noon
- **Scale**: microscopic world, vast landscape, intimate interior, cosmic scale

## Materials & Textures
- **Natural**: weathered driftwood, rough granite, smooth river stones, feathered texture
- **Synthetic**: carbon fiber weave, holographic vinyl, brushed titanium, patinated copper
- **Fabrics**: crushed velvet, fine silk chiffon, distressed leather, coarse burlap
- **States**: viscous liquid, crystalline structure, gelatinous mass, vapor cloud

## Composition & Framing
- **Angles**: bird's-eye view, worm's-eye view, canted angle, straight-on
- **Framing**: extreme close-up, wide establishing shot, over-the-shoulder, split frame
- **Composition**: rule of thirds, leading lines, symmetrical composition, golden ratio
- **Distance**: telephoto compression, wide angle distortion, normal perspective

## Lighting
- **Sources**: dappled sunlight, neon signs, bioluminescent glow, campfire
- **Qualities**: harsh contrast, soft diffusion, volumetric rays, specular highlights
- **Techniques**: Rembrandt lighting, split lighting, butterfly lighting, silhouette
- **Moods**: sinister shadows, ethereal glow, dramatic chiaroscuro, flat even lighting

## Style & Artistic Medium
- **Traditional Media**: charcoal sketch, gouache illustration, ukiyo-e woodblock print
- **Digital Styles**: low-poly 3D render, voxel art, pixel art, procedural generation
- **Art Movements**: art nouveau, constructivist, dadaist, pop art, baroque
- **Artists**: in the style of Moebius, inspired by Hilma af Klint, reminiscent of Caravaggio

## Color Palette & Mood
- **Palettes**: monochromatic blue, complementary orange and teal, pastel palette
- **Schemes**: analogous colors, triadic harmony, split complementary, tetradic
- **Moods**: melancholic, whimsical, foreboding, serene, chaotic, harmonious
- **Saturation**: vivid saturated colors, desaturated palette, selective color

## Technical Camera/Lens Effects
- **Lenses**: 85mm portrait lens, 16mm fisheye, tilt-shift lens, anamorphic lens
- **Effects**: lens flare, chromatic aberration, barrel distortion, bokeh balls
- **Film**: 35mm film grain, Kodachrome colors, Polaroid effect, cross-processed look
- **Focus**: focus stacking, selective focus, deep focus, soft focus glow

## Photorealism baseline keywords
photorealistic, hyperrealistic, 8k resolution, sharp focus, detailed texture, cinematic lighting, natural light, studio lighting, DSLR photo, shot on [Camera Model/Lens Type], editorial photography, high detail, analog imperfections.

## Key Takeaways
1. Keep this as a pick-list, not a read-through.
2. One technical + one evocative keyword per layer is the sweet spot.
3. "Analog imperfections" is the single most reused realism multiplier in these packs.
