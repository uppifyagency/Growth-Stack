# Glossary

**Analog imperfection layer** — deliberate lens dirt, scratches, dust, film grain, light leaks, and RGB fringing added to make an AI image read as a real photo (Ch 2, Ch 5).

**Anchor (prompt anchor)** — a reusable prompt fragment naming a fixed look, dropped into many prompts (Ch 4).

**Aspect ratio (`--ar`)** — frame proportions, e.g. `16:9`, `1:1`, `4:5` (Ch 3).

**Blue hour** — the soft twilight period before sunrise / after sunset (Ch 2).

**Bokeh** — the aesthetic blur of out-of-focus areas; "creamy bokeh," "bokeh balls" (Ch 2, Ch 5).

**Butterfly / Rembrandt / split lighting** — named portrait lighting setups defined by shadow shape (Ch 2).

**Catchlight** — the reflected highlight in an eye or glossy surface; "catchlight glow" (Ch 5).

**Chaos (`--c`)** — parameter raising variation/unpredictability across generations (Ch 3).

**Chiaroscuro** — strong light/dark contrast for drama (Ch 2).

**Chromatic aberration / RGB fringing** — color fringing at edges, used as an analog texture cue (Ch 2, Ch 5).

**Companion prompt (Sora companion)** — a video prompt built from a finished still prompt, preserving styling and adding minimal motion (Ch 6, Ch 7).

**Fisheye** — ultra-wide lens producing exaggerated barrel distortion; a signature editorial look here (Ch 2, Ch 5).

**Flat lay** — top-down product composition on a textured surface with props (Ch 5).

**Golden hour / magic hour** — warm, low-sun light shortly after sunrise / before sunset (Ch 2).

**Keyword weighting** — earlier words carry more influence; some platforms allow explicit weights (Ch 1).

**Master Prompt** — a foundational paragraph defining a category's creative direction, customized per shot (Ch 5, Ch 6).

**Motion blur** — streaking of moving elements; paired with sharp focus on the hero detail (Ch 5).

**Negative prompt (`--no`)** — explicit exclusions appended last, e.g. `--no text, watermarks` (Ch 1, Ch 3).

**Panning** — slow-shutter technique (~1/40s) keeping a moving subject sharp while the background blurs (Ch 6).

**Peak action / peak moment** — the apex of motion (top of a jump, instant of impact) chosen as the frame (Ch 6).

**Rear-curtain sync** — flash firing at the end of exposure so motion streaks trail behind a frozen subject (Ch 6).

**Seed (`--seed`)** — fixed value that reproduces composition across iterations; the control variable (Ch 3).

**Selective focus** — sharp hero subject, blurred surroundings (or vice versa) (Ch 2, Ch 6).

**Stylize (`--s` / `--stylize`)** — dial; lower = more photorealistic, higher = more stylized (Ch 3).

**`--style raw`** — reduces the model's artistic interpretation toward literal prompt adherence (Ch 3).

**Volumetric light / light shafts** — visible beams of light through atmosphere (fog, mist) (Ch 1, Ch 2).
