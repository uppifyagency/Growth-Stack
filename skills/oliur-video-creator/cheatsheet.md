# Video Creator Course — Cheatsheet

## The Pipeline
`Idea → Script → Scene & Light → Camera → A-Roll → B-Roll → Edit → Package → Upload → Monetise`
80% of a video = **scene + lighting + audio**. Camera body is the *least* important variable.

## iPhone Video Settings
HDR Video **OFF** · 4K 30fps (or 24) · Grid **ON** · Preserve Settings **ON** · A-roll on **1×** · ProRes off · B-roll mix 1×/2×/3× at 60fps.

## Scene & Framing Rules
- Tidy, color-coordinated background; nothing behind the head.
- **Shoot into a corner** for depth/separation.
- Head in the **top third** of frame.
- Kill natural light (blinds + door); control all lighting.
- A-roll: **3 lights** (key near face + warm + bluish accents). B-roll: **2 lights** (key + tube).

## Audio (more important than video)
| Mic | Use case |
|---|---|
| **DJI Mic kit** | recommended default — wireless, records onboard, works on phone |
| **Pencil mic + XLR** | static treated studio |
| **Dynamic podcast mic** | close-to-mouth, room-rejecting |
| **Shotgun mic** | directional on-camera (windshield/dead-cat outdoors) |
| **Lavalier** | interview / close voice |
Rule: **get the mic close + treat the room** (carpet, panels). Avoid the on-camera mic. XLR > 3.5mm (less interference).

## Lens Decision (the 5 questions)
Budget · Content · Aperture · Prime vs. Zoom · Travel.
| Need | Lens |
|---|---|
| One-lens / 90% B-roll | Tamron 28–75 / Sigma 28–70 f/2.8 |
| Intimate A-roll | Sony 24mm f/1.4 GM |
| Tight spaces / interiors | Tamron 17–28 |
| Macro / close-ups | Sony 90mm f/2.8 (often unnecessary) |
| Far / sports / portraits | 70–200 |
Shoot **faces at 50–100mm** (avoid warping). **Avoid variable-aperture zooms** for video. Lower f = more blur.

## Editing (Final Cut Pro)
- Folders: **A-Roll / B-Roll / cache / render**.
- **Compound clip** the A-roll → grade + level audio once.
- Audio level: **-6 to -12 dB** (dual mono).
- Grade preset: **Color Wheels + Hue/Sat Curves + Color Curves + Custom LUT (~25%)**.
- Luma waveform: face brightness **60–75**.
- **Automatic Speed → F5** for one-tap slow-mo (60→24/30fps).
- Lay **B-roll over the cut** to hide edits; cut tight, no gaps.
- Export: **H.264**, video+audio.
- Edit on **wired** headphones only (no Bluetooth — latency).

## Packaging
- **Title**: engaging *and* accurate — never clickbait. Think of it early.
- **Thumbnail**: simple + a **consistent, recognizable** style; borrow, then add your own style.

## Monetisation
- Order: **AdSense → Sponsorships → diversified (digital/physical products, affiliates, client work)**.
- **Let sponsors come to you** = leverage. "Everything is a negotiation."
- Pricing rule: **~$1,500 per 100k subs** for a 60s integration. Niche audience value can raise it.
- Prerequisites: audience + catalog + clear niche + clean persona + easy contact.

## Operating Maxims
- "80% is scene, lighting, audio."
- "Audio > video sharpness."
- "Buy the mic before the camera."
- "Fix the scene before you record — not in post."
- "Replicate, don't invent" (shots).
- "Practice makes perfect" — confidence/speed come from reps.
- "Money follows audience."
