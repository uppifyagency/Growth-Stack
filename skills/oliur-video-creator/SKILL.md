## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

**The thesis.** A great creator video is built, not bought. **80% of a video is your scene, your lighting, and your audio** — not the camera body. Viewers tolerate average video; they abandon bad audio. So spend money/effort on scene → light → mic *before* a fancy camera. "Practice makes perfect": confidence and editing speed are earned over 100–200 videos, not unlocked by gear.

**The end-to-end pipeline** (the spine of the whole course):
`Idea → Script → Scene & Lighting → Camera Setup → Shoot A-Roll → Shoot B-Roll → Edit (cut + color + audio) → Package (thumbnail + title) → Upload → Monetise`
Pre-production (script, title) feeds shooting; editing the A-roll reveals the B-roll shot list.

**A-Roll + B-Roll.** **A-Roll** = the talking-head spine (you to camera). **B-Roll** = product/demo footage laid *over* it — to illustrate, and to hide cuts. Record the spine, then dress it. Shoot B-roll *after* editing the A-roll, working from a list of shots it needs.

**The Notion idea + script system.** Capture ideas in a Kanban (**Ideas → In Progress → Uploaded**). Script structure: **Intro → Main → Outro**; one sentence per line, H3 headings per topic, **bold** the to-camera lines (read the rest off the iPad). Write "as if talking to a friend."

**The manual camera baseline (Sony a7 IV).** 4K · Manual · **24fps** (60fps for B-roll) · **S-Log3** (Picture Profile 8) · 4:2:2 10-bit @ 100Mbps · **shutter ≈ 2× frame rate** · lowest aperture · **base ISO (800)** · WB custom **5600K** to match lights.

**The clean-scene method.** Tidy, color-coordinated background; nothing behind the head; **shoot into a corner** for depth; kill natural light; **head in the top third**. A-roll = **3-light** setup (key near face + warm + bluish accents); B-roll = **2-light** (key + tube). Fix the scene *before* recording — you can't fix it in post. Use a low-aperture lens for the shallow-DoF "look."

**The edit-once grade (Final Cut Pro).** Wrap A-roll in a **compound clip**, then grade + level audio once. Audio to **-6 to -12 dB**. Grade with a saved preset: **Color Wheels + Hue/Sat Curves + Color Curves + Custom LUT (~25%)**; push highlights so face brightness reads **60–75** on the Luma waveform. **Automatic Speed → F5** for one-tap slow-mo. Lay **B-roll over the cut** to hide edits; cut tight, no gaps. Export **H.264**. Wired headphones only.

**Packaging.** Title = engaging *and* accurate (never clickbait), thought of early. Thumbnail = simple + a **consistent, recognizable** style (borrow, then make it yours).

**Lens selection — the 5 questions**: budget · content · aperture · prime vs. zoom · travel. One-lens starter = **Tamron 28–75 / Sigma 28–70 f/2.8** (≈90% of shots); intimate A-roll = **24mm f/1.4**; tight spaces = **17–28**; macro = **90mm** (often unneeded); far/portraits = **70–200**. Shoot faces at **50–100mm** (avoid warping). **Avoid variable-aperture zooms** for video. Lower f-number = more light + more blur.

**Audio-first stack.** Get the mic **close** + **treat the room** (carpet, panels). Default = **DJI Mic** (wireless, onboard recording, works on a phone). Step up = **XLR + pencil mic** (treated studio) or **dynamic podcast mic** (close, room-rejecting). XLR > 3.5mm (less interference). Never use the on-camera mic.

**iPhone budget workflow.** Proof the camera isn't the bottleneck: 4K 30/24fps, **HDR off**, grid on, Preserve Settings on, 1× for A-roll. Same scene/light/top-third rules + softbox + DJI Mic into the phone. B-roll: mix 1×/2×/3×, 60fps, slow to 50%, lock AE/AF + pull exposure. Edit identically; LUT ~30%.

**Monetisation — let sponsors come to you.** Money follows audience. Order: **AdSense → Sponsorships → diversified** (digital/physical products, affiliates, client work — these build an owned email list). Build audience + catalog + clear **niche** + clean persona + easy contact first; inbound sponsors = leverage. "**Everything is a negotiation**." Pricing rule: **~$1,500 per 100k subscribers** for a 60s integration.

## Scope & Limits

The "What's Next / More To Come" module is filler (a thank-you promising future updates) and contributes no method. Gear models, prices, and app UIs change over time — verify current specs before relying on a specific setting or product. For applying the method, pair this with hands-on shooting and editing in your own tools.
