# Glossary — Video Creator Course (Oliur)

**4:2:2 10-bit** — high-data recording format; more color/brightness data = more grading leeway (Ch 3).
**A-Roll** — the talking-head footage, you facing camera; the spoken backbone of the video (Ch 1, 3).
**AdSense** — YouTube ad revenue; the basic, low-yield monetisation on-ramp (Ch 7).
**Aperture** — lens f-number; lower = more light + shallower depth of field, higher = more in focus (Ch 3, 5).
**Apple Log / Apple Log LUTs** — flat iPhone profile + LUTs to grade it; reset FCP's auto-LUT to None first (Ch 7).
**Automatic Speed** — Final Cut Pro command that conforms a 60fps clip to the timeline rate; bind to a key (F5) (Ch 4, 6).
**B-Roll** — supplementary footage (product/demo) laid over A-roll to illustrate and hide cuts (Ch 1, 3, 6).
**Base ISO** — the camera's native ISO (800 on the Sony a7 IV); the value with no lines under the number (Ch 3).
**Color Wheels** — FCP tool for shadows/mid-tones/highlights; place *above* the LUT in the stack (Ch 4, 7).
**Compound clip** — wrap A-roll so one color/audio correction applies to every cut (Ch 4).
**Dead cat** — furry windshield for outdoor/windy mic use (Ch 5).
**DJI Mic (kit)** — wireless mic with onboard recording; works on camera or phone; Oliur's recommended default (Ch 5, 6).
**Dual mono** — audio setting used to flatten/level the dialogue track in FCP (Ch 4).
**Dynamic microphone** — podcast mic (~$250–350) held close; rejects room noise; natural sound (Ch 5).
**Field recorder** — battery, SD, multi-XLR mobile recorder for pro field use (Ch 5).
**Final Cut Pro** — Oliur's editor of choice; principles transfer to Premiere/DaVinci Resolve (Ch 4, 6).
**Frictionless setup** — an always-ready dedicated space; sit, mount, record (Ch 3).
**FX3** — Sony cinema camera used for B-roll; ships with the XLR-K3M module (Ch 3, 5).
**G Master (GM)** — Sony's top-tier lenses (sharpest, best AF); overkill for sub-4K online video (Ch 5).
**H.264** — the export codec used in FCP (File → Share → Export File) (Ch 4).
**HDR Video** — iPhone setting Oliur turns OFF; flat-off looks better and grades better (Ch 6).
**Key light** — the main light on the subject; kept close to the face with grid + dome (Ch 3).
**Lavalier mic** — small clip-on mic; ideal for interviews/voice, close to the mouth (Ch 5).
**Log / S-Log3** — flat picture profile (Picture Profile 8 on a7 IV) for easier color grading (Ch 3, 4).
**Luma waveform** — brightness readout; keep skin/face ~60–75 (Ch 4).
**LUT (Custom LUT / Oliur O1)** — color look applied last in the stack; kept ~25% (pro) / ~30% (iPhone) (Ch 4, 6, 7).
**Manual mode** — full manual camera control so nothing auto-adjusts (Ch 3).
**Microfiber cloth** — wipe products before shots for a clean, dust-free look (Ch 3).
**Niche** — a hyper-focused channel topic; tells sponsors exactly who they reach (Ch 7).
**Notion Kanban** — idea pipeline with Ideas / In Progress / Uploaded columns (Ch 2).
**Pencil microphone** — directional studio mic for controlled, treated rooms; Oliur's main mic (Ch 5).
**Picture Profile 8** — the a7 IV slot holding S-Log3 (Ch 3).
**Prime lens** — single focal length; smaller, lighter, often cheaper, lower aperture (Ch 5).
**Room treatment** — carpet, foam panels, soft furnishings to deaden echo for clean audio (Ch 3, 5).
**Shallow depth of field** — subject sharp, background blurred; from low-aperture lenses (Ch 3, 5).
**Shotgun mic** — directional on-camera mic with windshield; picks up what it points at (Ch 5).
**Shutter speed rule** — set shutter to ~2× the frame rate (24fps→50; 60fps→125) (Ch 3).
**Softbox** — light modifier that softens shadows; key for clean lighting on a budget (Ch 6).
**Sony a7 IV** — Oliur's main A-roll camera; shoots 4K, 60fps, S-Log3 (Ch 3).
**Sponsorship** — paid brand integration; lucrative once you have audience + catalog (Ch 7).
**Three-light setup** — key light near face + two accent/back lights (warm + bluish) for separation (Ch 3).
**Top-third framing** — head sits in the top third of frame, not centered (Ch 3, 6).
**32-bit float** — high-data audio recording; lets you fix clipping/levels after recording (Ch 5).
**Tube light** — small accent light for moody B-roll product shots (Ch 3).
**Variable aperture** — a zoom whose brightness shifts mid-zoom; avoid for video (Ch 5).
**Windshield / windscreen** — foam cover guarding the mic from wind noise (Ch 5).
**XLR / XLR-K3M** — pro audio cable/standard + Sony module into the camera; cleaner, interference-resistant vs. 3.5mm (Ch 3, 5).
**Zoom lens** — variable focal length; heavier, possibly higher/variable aperture vs. a prime (Ch 5).
