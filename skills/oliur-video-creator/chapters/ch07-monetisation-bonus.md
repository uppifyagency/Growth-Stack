# Chapter 7: Monetisation & Bonus (Apple LOG LUTs)

## Core Idea
Money follows audience, not the other way around: grow a niche channel with a catalog and a clean image, then **let sponsors come to you** — which puts you in the negotiating seat. Diversify beyond sponsorships. The bonus covers applying Apple Log LUTs in Final Cut Pro.

*(Scope note: the "What's Next / More To Come" module is filler — a thank-you promising future updates; no method content.)*

## Frameworks Introduced
- **The monetisation ladder** — in order of leverage:
  1. **AdSense** (ads on videos) — the basic on-ramp; low income-per-view.
  2. **Sponsorships** — bigger lump sums, recurring, lucrative — but only after you have an audience + catalog.
  3. **Diversified streams**: **digital products, physical products, affiliates, client work** — as valuable or more, because they build an owned **customer base / email list** that trusts and re-buys.
- **"Let the sponsors come to you"** — don't chase brands early; once you upload regularly and build an audience, sponsors fly into your inbox, and you hold the leverage to set terms/integrations.
- **The sponsor-readiness prerequisites**: (1) a **grown audience**, (2) a **catalog of videos** (signals you're serious), (3) a **clear niche** (tells sponsors exactly who they're reaching), (4) a **clean/professional public persona** (no heavy cursing/controversy — brands avoid it), (5) **easy contact** (form/email on profile).
- **Pricing guideline**: ~**$1,500 per 100k subscribers** for a basic **60-second integration** (general rule). Oliur charges **$4,000–6,000** per 60s on his tech channel — and the *same* on his smaller finance channel (richer audience supports higher rates).
- **"Everything is a negotiation"** — nothing is off the table; always negotiate rates and integration terms. Talk to similarly-sized creators about their rates (they're usually open) to hold pricing up across the field.
- **The Apple Log LUT workflow (Bonus, Final Cut Pro)**:
  1. Drag in the Apple Log clip — FCP **auto-applies a LUT** (Apple's processing).
  2. Info icon → set to **Extended** → **Camera LUT: None** to get the flat profile back.
  3. Add the **Custom LUT** (your downloaded Apple Log LUT) in the Color section; choose among the four LUTs.
  4. Add **Color Wheels** and **reorder them ABOVE the custom LUT** — so corrections happen *before* the LUT.
  5. Tweak highlights/shadows/saturation down to taste, then enable/disable to compare.

## Key Concepts
- **Audience-first**: if you're new, you simply won't make money yet — focus on growing and shipping.
- **Niche leverage**: hyper-focused niches let brands target precisely; the odd off-niche video is fine if the majority stays focused.
- **Owned audience**: an email list of past buyers is the compounding asset — future launches sell more easily.
- **Grade-before-LUT ordering**: corrections must sit above the LUT in the FCP stack to behave correctly (mirrors Ch 4).

## Mental Models
- Think of monetisation as a **lagging indicator of audience** — build the asset, the money arrives.
- Treat inbound sponsors as **negotiating leverage**: they already want you, so you set terms.
- Diversify so no single revenue stream (or platform) owns you.

## Anti-patterns
- **Chasing sponsors / money before an audience**: backwards and ineffective.
- **A controversial or heavy-cursing persona**: scares off brand deals.
- **Relying only on AdSense or only on sponsorships**: under-monetised and fragile.
- **Making contact difficult**: a hidden email loses inbound deals.
- **Leaving Apple's auto-LUT on / color-wheels below the LUT**: wrong look and wrong order.

## Key Takeaways
1. Grow audience + catalog + a clear niche + clean persona first; money follows.
2. Let sponsors come to you for negotiating leverage; "everything is a negotiation."
3. Pricing rule of thumb: ~$1,500 per 100k subs for a 60s integration; richer niches command more.
4. Diversify into digital/physical products, affiliates, and client work to build an owned, re-buying customer base.
5. Bonus: reset Apple's auto-LUT to None, add your Custom LUT, put Color Wheels *above* it, then grade.
