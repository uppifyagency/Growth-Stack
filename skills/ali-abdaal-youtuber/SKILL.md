## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

**The thesis.** YouTube is a *probabilistic platform* — you improve odds, you never secure results. So **Stack The Deck** with the three levers you control: **Time** (volume of videos), **Money** (gear/outsourcing/team), and **Systems** (repeatable production). It's a marathon, not a sprint; **reps beat secret sauce** and **consuming ≠ doing**.

**Foundations / mindset.** The single beginner commitment is **One Video A Week** — content barely matters early; shipping weekly builds the habit everything rests on. Assume your first ~100 videos are bad and make them fast (the avg 1M-sub channel has **~3,873 videos**). Growth is **compounding** (flat then vertical). Engineer **Manufactured Luck** (~100 reps + obsessive prep before going viral) rather than waiting. Then make the **Me-Mindset → Audience-Mindset shift** to convert a posting habit into growth. Perfectionism/overthinking is the #1 thing killing your volume.

**Ideas (niche).** Don't blueprint up front (the ~2% **architects**); be an **archaeologist** (~98%) who picks a rough site and digs. Use **The Three Levels of Finding Your Niche** (who am I & what I want → who is my audience & what they want → value = L1 + L2) via timed two-minute brain-dumps. Compress to **The Niche Formula**: "I make videos about [topic] for [people]." Build an **Ideal Viewer Avatar**; no audience yet → target **yourself, younger and more incompetent**. Write a **dual value proposition** (explicit line + implicit thought + the "how"). *Riches are in the niches* — be the only, not the best.

**Algorithm & packaging.** The whole job = **The Million Dollar Formula**: *earn the click* (title + thumbnail) and *keep them watching* (hook + retention). The algorithm follows people, not vice versa — stop hacking tags/keywords. The **title decides ~70–80%** of performance: brainstorm **20 titles per video** (~10 min, highest-ROI habit), obeying the **5 title rules** (sell the result · intrigue not agreement · don't describe · optimize for humans · A/B test). Pick **Browse vs Search** before titling (browse ≈ 90% of traffic and the only real path to viral). Thumbnail (~20%): **3 rules** — make it pop (real photo, heavily edited, not a screen-grab) · match the vibe (*vibe attracts tribe*) · emotion only if it fits. Win the first 10–30s with **The Hook**.

**Retention & branding.** Run the **A.B.C.D.E. of Personalization** (Aesthetic, Branding, Confidence, Delivery, Editing) to turn a "good video" channel into a loved one. Brand with **Values, Rituals & Icons** (*Primal Branding*). Structure every video with **HIVES** (Hook · Introduction · Value · End-screen Sales pitch). Optimize for **super-fans**, not subscriber count. Kill the **British Airways voice** (pitch down, go conversational, smile); default to **bullet points** over scripts. Hold attention with the **10-second pattern-interrupt** rule, tight cuts, B-roll, and sound design. Creators sit on the **Camera Talker ↔ Editing Jedi Master** spectrum.

**Audience / build.** Once you ship one *good* video a week, **repurpose, don't recreate** via **The Artery Method** — the weekly video is the *aorta* feeding every platform (it's already 95% of the work; distribution is ~95% of the impact). Climb the **9 Levels of Repurposing** (beginners: only L1 Twitter threads + L2 Shorts). **Outsource editing** after ~5 videos via a paid **audition** of ~10 freelancers; pay per hour, give a mood board + relentless feedback; scale freelancer → part-time → full-time. Grow on **public** (rented) platforms, funnel toward **private** (owned) ones — the **email list** converts best. Build **super fans** via the **Inverted Funnel** (casual → active → community → super fans); *one inch wide, one mile deep*.

**Monetise.** Model AdSense on **RPM, not CPM** (income ≈ (views ÷ 1,000) × RPM; standard ~$1–3, finance/business ~$20–40); raise it via 3 levers (high-CPM niche, longer videos, US/UK geo). Eligibility: **1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours / 12 mo**. Climb the **5 Ways to Monetise** worst→best: AdSense < affiliates < sponsors < **personal products** ("the real money is in selling your own products"). Sequence launches with **Jab, Jab, Jab, Right Hook** — give value for years, then ask. Match the model to the audience's wallet; diversify pillars as a hedge.

**Guest-workshop wisdom.** The **Big Domino** (Gannon): one pillar asset makes the rest grow as byproducts; cross-platform pollinate one titled, subtitled clip. **MILES** unfair advantages (Money/Intelligence/Location/Education/Status on a Mindset base) + the **Niche Checklist** (painful, urgent problem actively being solved with few options; **painkiller > vitamin**). The **5 P's of community** (Pact, People, Platform, Promotion, Profit). Writing: **Tell, Title, Tension** intro + **C.L.E.A.R.** sentences (write music, not words). Storytelling: a **Five-Second Moment** of change, beginning = its opposite, told in scenes (**Homework for Life**). Speaking: reframe nerves as excitement, **stay in character**, the **Four Delivery Tactics**, and silence (**the snow globe**).

**Gear.** "No such thing as need" — gear just stacks odds. **Upgrade order: AUDIO > LIGHTING > camera/lens.** Get audio to **Level 2** (cheap shotgun mic off-camera, a hand's-breadth from the face); one big soft **key light at 45°**; buy budget/mid tier, not the executive ceiling. Phone is OK to start, not to grow.
