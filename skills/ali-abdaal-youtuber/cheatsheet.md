# Part-Time YouTuber Academy — Cheatsheet

## The Course Arc
`Foundations → Ideas (niche) → Algorithm (packaging) → Retention → Build (repurpose/team) → Monetise → Guest Workshops → Q&A → Gear → Bonus`

## The Two Jobs (everything reduces to this)
**The Million Dollar Formula = earn the click + keep them watching.** Tags, keywords, likes/dislikes don't move the needle. Ignore all of it until you can ship one *good* video a week.

## Key Numbers & Rules
| Thing | Rule |
|---|---|
| Beginner commitment | **One video a week** — the only thing that matters early |
| First ~100 videos | Assume they're bad; make them fast |
| Avg 1M-sub channel | **~3,873 videos** (TubeBuddy) — volume wins |
| Avg to first 1,000 subs | ~152 videos — don't expect money early |
| Niche commitment | Judge after **20–40–50 videos**, not before |
| Titles per video | Brainstorm **20** (~10 min) — highest-ROI habit |
| Title vs thumbnail weight | **~70–80%** title · **~20%** thumbnail |
| Browse vs search traffic | **~90%** browse (viral) · ~10% search (long-tail) |
| Outsource editing | After **~5 videos** |
| Early retention | **~20–25%** drop-off is normal, not personal |

## Packaging (title + thumbnail tell ONE story)
- **5 title rules**: sell the result · intrigue not agreement · don't describe · optimize for humans · A/B test.
- Not clickbait if you **deliver** the promise — reframe "clickbait" → "intrigue".
- **3 thumbnail rules**: make it pop (real photo + heavy edit, not a screen-grab) · match the vibe · emotion only if it fits.
- **Vibe attracts tribe** — for non-kid audiences, classy "Instagram-feed" beats the shock-face.
- Title + thumbnail complement, never duplicate text; keep a per-channel colour palette.
- Tools: TubeBuddy (A/B + Click Magnet), thumbsup.tv (preview), vidIQ (tags).

## Retention Levers
- **HIVES** = Hook · Introduction (who + value prop) · Value · End-screen Sales pitch.
- **A.B.C.D.E.** = Aesthetic · Branding · Confidence · Delivery · Editing.
- Branding = **Values, Rituals, Icons** (Primal Branding); optimize for super-fans.
- Win the first **10–30s** with a hook (question · fact · transformation · tip · B-roll · flash-forward).
- **10-second pattern-interrupt** (1-second for high energy); cut dead pauses; B-roll; zoom cuts; sound design.
- Default to **bullet points**; full scripts + teleprompter only for expert/presentational channels.

## Speaking & Storytelling
- Audience cares about itself, not you — the reframe behind all 9 tips.
- **Pitch down + smile + talking points**; volume 5/10 → 7/10 for video/stage.
- **Pause = snow globe** — feels longer to you than them. Silence is the most underused tool.
- Reframe **nervous → excited**; never tell anyone you're nervous; stay in character.
- Intro = **Tell + Title + Tension** (<50 words). Edit with **C.L.E.A.R.** Write music, not words.
- Every story = a **5-second moment** of change; beginning = the opposite of the ending; tell in scenes.

## Gear Priorities
- **Upgrade order: AUDIO > LIGHTING > camera/lens.** "No such thing as need" — gear just stacks odds.
- Minimum viable kit (~$1,000): Canon M50 + Sigma 16mm f/1.4 + Rode VideoMicro + ~$20 stand.
- **Audio Level 2**: shotgun mic off-camera on a stand, a hand's-breadth from your face via a $10 extension cable. Mic proximity beats mic price.
- **One big soft key light at 45°** and slightly above. "45 is the magic number." Budget pick: Godox SL60 (~$100).
- Lower f-number = blurrier background; sit far from the back wall.
- Avoid: lavalier mics, ring lights, three-point/hair/fill lights, blank-wall framing, Level 0 audio.

## Build & Monetise
- **Repurpose, don't recreate** — the video is 95% of the work. Beginners: only L1 threads + L2 Shorts.
- **Artery Method**: one aorta video feeds every platform; publish micro ~1 min after the pillar.
- Outsource via paid audition of ~10 freelancers; pay per hour; give a mood board + relentless feedback.
- Grow on **public** (rented), funnel to **private** (owned) — the email list converts best.
- AdSense eligibility: **1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours / 12 mo.** Income ≈ (views ÷ 1,000) × RPM (not CPM).
- Standard RPM ~$1–3; finance/business ~$20–40. Longer videos (>20 min) earn more.
- **Monetise stack (worst→best)**: AdSense < affiliates < sponsors < **your own products** (the real money).
- **Jab, jab, jab, right hook** — give value for years, then ask. "If you don't ask, it's a default no."

## Operating Maxims
- It's a **marathon, not a sprint** — enjoy the journey; let compounding work.
- **Reps beat secret sauce** — consuming ≠ doing.
- **A deadline ships videos, not inspiration** — fix a weekly slot; never break the streak.
- **People add value, not equipment** — don't buy gear as a beginner unless it's blocking growth.
- **Distribution = 95% of impact** even though the video is 95% of the effort.
- Don't depend on one platform OR one person (you) — diversify both.
- Comparison to bigger creators is the game — compete on personality/jokes/editing, not spend.
