# Chapter 10: Step 7 — Determine Who Cares a Lot

## Core Idea
Not all customers value your value equally. Find the segment that cares *a lot* — your best-fit prospects — and target as narrowly as you can to hit your near-term goals.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Actionable segmentation**: "a list of a person's or company's easily identifiable characteristics that make them really care about what you do." Goes well beyond demographics/firmographics (e.g. "businesses that send a high volume of invoices," not just "small businesses").
- **Two criteria for a worthy segment**: (1) big enough to meet your business goals, and (2) has important, specific, *unmet* needs common to the segment.

## Key Concepts
- **Best-fit prospects**: buy quickly, rarely ask for discounts, tell their friends, don't churn, may say you should charge more — they're "buying" as much as you're "selling."
- **Other customers**: think you're "OK for the price," slow to decide, ask for discounts, jump ship for cheaper/better. Harder to sell and keep.
- **Segmentation ≠ demographics**: for businesses use how they sell, what they've invested in, skills they have/lack; for consumers use brands they like, stores, job, preferences.

## Mental Models
- "Who cares and why?" — repeat this question against each value theme to find the segment with the highest affinity (e.g. multi-location appointment value → financial-services/tax-prep firms, *not* drugstores).
- Narrow focus *increases* odds of connecting; broad focus makes it harder to convince anyone you're the best.

## Anti-patterns
- **Targeting as broadly as possible** to "increase chances" — the opposite is true.
- **Getting positioning too far ahead of business objectives**: if best-fit alone can't hit this year's sales target, broaden the definition; if it can, stay focused.

## Key Takeaways
1. Target as narrowly as you can to meet near-term sales objectives; broaden later.
2. Best-fit customers are easiest to sell to *and* retain.
3. Positioning and target customers evolve over time — resonate with best-fit *now*.
