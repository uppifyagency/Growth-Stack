# Glossary — Internet Pipes (Steph Smith)

**Affirmation Station** — generation method: find ideas already widely upvoted/pre-vetted online (Ch 8).
**Also Asked** — tool surfacing the real questions people ask about a topic (Ch 5).
**Answer the Public** — tool visualizing real search questions/comparisons; great for direct + indirect competitors (Ch 5, 12, 13).
**anvaka "say it"** — subreddit knowledge-graph tool (by a dev named Anvaka) mapping connected/adjacent communities (Ch 5, 7).
**Aspirational Self** — audience whose searches spike every New Year for who they want to become (Ch 10).
**Automatic Win** — design goal of Preparation Station: value even if you do nothing else (Ch 4, 5).
**BuiltWith** — reveals the tech a website is built with (Ch 12).
**Charter** — data-viz newsletter (sold to Robinhood); brand budget from $25K (Ch 14, 15).
**Cross-platform arbitrage** — reposting top-voted content to another platform with overlapping audience (Ch 8).
**Differentiation Station** — figure out how to stand out via audience understanding (Ch 13).
**Douglas Adams' three reactions** — tech feels normal/exciting/unnatural by your age at its invention (Ch 7).
**Dynamic search** — advanced search (Twitter/X, Google) filtered by like-thresholds or keywords (Ch 12, bonus).
**eRank** — Etsy tag/seasonality and revenue-estimate tool (Ch 7, 10).
**Evaluation Station** — magnifying-glass competitor analysis (Ch 12).
**Exploration Station** — generation method based on technological progress/hype cycles (Ch 7).
**Facebook Ad Library** — see competitors' ads and how long they've run (Ch 12).
**Flourish** — viz tool for moving infographics; acquired by Canva 2022 (Ch 5, 14).
**Gartner Hype Cycle** — Peak → Trough of Disillusionment → Plateau of Productivity (Ch 7).
**Gastrodiplomacy** — governments spreading culture/food as a trend leading indicator (Ch 9).
**Generation Station / "The Generator"** — idea engine of 12 substation methods (Ch 6).
**GigaBrain** — ask-a-question tool synthesizing answers from real Reddit threads (Ch 13).
**Ghostery** — privacy extension; reveals trackers/pixels on a site (Ch 5, 12).
**Google Dataset Search** — search specifically for datasets (Ch 5).
**Google Trends** — search-interest over time and by subregion (Ch 5, 9, 10).
**Gummy Search** — deep subreddit analysis: themes, pain, solution requests, AI-pattern synthesis (Ch 5, 13).
**Hierarchy of Information** — survey < search < sales data, by reliability (Ch 11, 12).
**Import Yeti** — free tool showing companies' suppliers/shipments (Ch 12).
**Internet Glasses** — learned posture of seeing the web as human-intent signal (Ch 1).
**Jungle Scout** — Amazon product sales/revenue estimator (Ch 8, 11, 12).
**Keywords Everywhere** — paid extension: inline search volume/CPC/trend on Google/Amazon/Etsy (Ch 5).
**Koala Inspector** — Shopify store inspector (revenue/products) (Ch 12).
**Konbini rankings** — Japanese convenience-store product charts (auto-translated) (Ch 9).
**Local Year in Search** — Google's "top trending near me" by city (Ch 9).
**Location Station** — generation method: geo-arbitrage of trends across regions (Ch 9).
**Moving infographic** — animated data viz; a new shareable storytelling art form (Ch 14).
**Navigation Station** — the map/overview of the whole Station journey (Ch 4).
**No bad ideas (yet)** — collect freely in Generation; filter later (Ch 6).
**Our World in Data** — large downloadable dataset of long-term global trends (Ch 7).
**Owletter** — monitor competitors' email campaigns (Ch 12).
**Passive documentation** — the web recording desires that were once private (Ch 1).
**Prediction markets** — Polymarket/Manifold; conviction signal with money behind it (Ch 13).
**Preparation Station** — browser/tool setup for passive opportunity-noticing (Ch 5).
**Recommendation Station** — curated content-diet upgrade: creators/newsletters/subreddits (Ch 15).
**Sci-Fi Idea Bank** — catalog of ideas first imagined vs. first built; a mind-expander (Ch 7).
**SimilarWeb** — site traffic volume + source mix + competitors (Ch 5, 12).
**SparkToro** — audience-makeup analysis for a company/domain (Ch 12).
**Station model** — the GTM pipeline: Prep → Generate → Validate → Evaluate → Differentiate (+Visualize/Recommend) (Ch 4).
**Subreddit Stats** — per-subreddit submissions/subscriber history (Ch 2, 5, 8, 13).
**The Pudding** — interactive "visual essays" site (Ch 14).
**Two-and-three-star reviews** — the most nuanced, actionable customer feedback (Ch 12, 13).
**Validation Station** — filter ideas against the biggest websites; validate or invalidate demand (Ch 11).
**Variation Station** — generation method: seasonal trends and the aspirational self (Ch 10).
**Visual Capitalist** — infographic publisher with a creator program (Ch 14).
**Visualization Station** — data viz as storytelling and as a business (Ch 14).
**Wappalyzer** — reveals a site's technology stack (Ch 5, 12).
**WikiPulse** — Wikipedia seasonality by month and day-of-week (Ch 10).
**XTools** (Wikipedia gadget) — page views/revisions/editors overlay (Ch 5).
