# Chapter 15: Recommendation Station (Upgrade Your Content Diet)

## Core Idea
There's only so much one course can contain, so this Station hands you curated rabbit holes — creators, Twitter/X accounts, newsletters, subreddits — to keep learning. The premise: everyone, including the author, benefits from a deliberately upgraded content/information diet.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Curate, don't just add**: the goal isn't to pile more onto your existing content diet — it's to *purge* low-value inputs and introduce high-fidelity, high-quality sources.
  - When to use: ongoing; treat your information intake as something you actively design.
  - How: replace passive/low-signal feeds with vetted creators and sources; contribute your own finds back.

## Key Concepts
- **High-fidelity information sources**: prioritize signal density over volume — a few excellent sources beat many mediocre ones.
- **Community contribution loop**: the Discord has #tips, #tools, #trends channels — share what you find so the collective diet improves (the Tool/Trend databases grow from this).
- This Station is explicitly living — recommendations expand over time.

## Mental Models
- Your "information diet" is as designable as a food diet — quality of inputs shapes quality of ideas/output.
- Sharing your best sources is positive-sum: you get others' best in return.

## Key Takeaways
1. Audit your current inputs; cut the low-signal ones before adding new ones.
2. Adopt vetted creators/newsletters/subreddits as your baseline diet (e.g. Charter for data-viz).
3. Contribute discoveries back to the community channels to compound everyone's diet.
