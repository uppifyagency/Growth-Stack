# Chapter 6: Generation Station ("The Generator")

## Core Idea
The idea-generation engine: 12 substations, each a distinct *method* for surfacing ideas. The rule here is volume, not quality — collect freely; filtering happens in later Stations.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **The 12 generation methods** (each = a different "frame" / internet glasses):
  1. **Exploration** — learn from technological progress, hype cycles, long-term trends.
  2. **Affirmation** — find what's already widely upvoted (pre-vetted ideas).
  3. **Location** — trends in one region surface opportunity in another (geo-arbitrage).
  4. **Variation** — seasonal trends and the "aspirational self."
  5. **Authorization** — IP/patents expiring or open APIs signal opportunity (PatentDrop, Patent Expiry Today, PatentsView API, Public Domain Review).
  6. **Regulation** — new laws reshape the business landscape (GovTrack, LegiScan, congress.gov; e.g. sports betting, pay transparency, nomad visas, psychedelics).
  7–12. **Monetization, Association, Innovation, Valuation, Compensation, Reflection** — read-only substations in Notion (no video).

## Key Concepts
- **No bad ideas (at this stage)**: don't critique; just log everything in the Idea Log.
- **"API = opportunity"**: an open API (e.g. PatentsView) spawns many products built on top of it (e.g. Patent Expiry Today).
- The first six substations have videos; the rest are written-only but follow the same logic.

## Mental Models
- Idea generation is a *portfolio* activity: run many methods, accept a low hit rate, defer judgment.
- Each method retrains your attention toward a specific kind of inflection point (tech, social proof, geography, time, IP, law).

## Anti-patterns
- **Filtering during generation**: kills volume and biases you toward the obvious.
- **Skipping the Generator when you "already have ideas"**: allowed — but the frames themselves are the lasting value.

## Key Takeaways
1. Generation is meaty by design — skip substations freely, but treat each as a reusable lens.
2. Output of this Station = a long, unfiltered idea list + the Idea Log.
3. The next three Stations (Validation → Evaluation → Differentiation) exist precisely to filter this list.
