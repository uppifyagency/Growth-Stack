# Chapter 11: Validation Station (Filter Against the Biggest Websites)

## Core Idea
Now funnel the unfiltered idea list. Validate — or, just as importantly, *invalidate* — each idea using data from the world's biggest websites. Pay attention to what people *do*, not what they *say*.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **The Hierarchy of Information** (the spine of the whole methodology):
  1. **Survey data** (weakest) — what people *say* they want; small samples, bias, leading questions.
  2. **Search data** (strong) — what people look for when no one's watching; huge scale, long-tail nuance, what they're *doing*.
  3. **Sales data** (strongest) — they put their wallet forward; proven, not hypothetical.
  - When to use: weigh every signal by where it sits on this ladder; prefer search/sales over surveys.

## Key Concepts (the validation surfaces, by scale)
- **Wikipedia** — 7th most-visited site, ~60M pages maintained by volunteers.
- **Reddit** — top-20 site, 100K+ active communities; interconnected, anti-promo moderation makes it candid.
- **Google** — 6M searches/min; the most direct window into want/buy/compare/learn.
- **YouTube** — 500 hrs uploaded/min; top app for teens.
- **TikTok** — 1B+ MAU; algorithm tuned on a single action (the swipe) → fast, accurate intent detection.
- **App Store** — 1B+ iPhones; $1T+ in billings.
- **Amazon** — $450K spent/min; a permutation of nearly every product exists, queryable via **Jungle Scout**.

## Mental Models
- "Validate or invalidate" — finding *no* demand (search volume but no sales, or no volume at all) is a win; it kills a bad idea cheaply.
- A good idea in Generation can still fail Validation — that's the point of deferring judgment earlier.

## Anti-patterns
- **Trusting survey/stated-preference data over behavior**: people lie or guess; search and sales don't.
- **Validating on one platform**: triangulate across several (it's "not just SEO").

## Key Takeaways
1. Run each idea through search and sales data on the mega-platforms; weight by the hierarchy.
2. Actively look for disconfirming evidence — invalidation is as valuable as validation.
3. Output = a short list of ideas with proven demand, ready for competitor analysis.
