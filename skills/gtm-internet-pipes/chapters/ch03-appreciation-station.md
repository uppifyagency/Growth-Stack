# Chapter 3: Appreciation Station (Bonuses)

## Core Idea
A bonus hub of supporting resources designed to keep you going down the internet rabbit hole after the core Stations. Not methodology — an index of reusable assets.

## Key Concepts
- **Exercises & videos (sequenced)**: all course exercises/videos in order in one place.
- **Tool database & Trend database**: every tool and trend mentioned across the course, aggregated and tagged by which Station they came from (so you can jump back for context). Both are expected to grow over time.
- **My First Million research notes**: Steph's raw, uncleaned research docs from her 7 MFM podcast appearances.
- **Searching Smart**: dynamic-search techniques across Google and Twitter/X (find old tweets; find databases; filter by like-thresholds).
- **Keyboard Shortcuts**: browse faster/more fluidly.
- **Idea Log**: a simple template to capture tips/trends/tools as you go.
- **Idea Generation Checklist**: quick places to look when you don't have time for the full Generator.
- **Research Checklist & Template + Case Study**: Steph applies the full research process end-to-end to one industry.

## Key Takeaways
1. Treat this as your reference shelf, not a lesson — pull from it on demand.
2. The Tool/Trend databases are living, community-fed assets.
3. The Case Study is the best place to see the whole methodology applied to a single idea.
