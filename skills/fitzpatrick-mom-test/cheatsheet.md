# Cheatsheet — The Mom Test (Rob Fitzpatrick)

## The Mom Test (3 rules)
1. Talk about **their life**, not your idea.
2. Ask about **specifics in the past**, not generics/opinions/the future.
3. **Talk less, listen more.**

## Bad data → fix
| Bad data | Looks like | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Compliment** | "I love it!" "That's cool" | **Deflect** → get back to facts |
| **Fluff** | "I usually…", "I would…", "I might…" | **Anchor** → "When's the last time? Talk me through it" |
| **Idea / request** | "You should add X" | **Dig** → "What would that let you do?" |
| **Emotion** | angry / embarrassed / overjoyed | **Dig** → "Tell me more, there's a story here" |

The deadliest fluff: **"I would definitely buy that."**

## Good questions (pass the Mom Test)
- "Why do you bother?" (motivation)
- "What are the implications of that?" (does it matter + pricing)
- "Talk me through the last time that happened." (show, not tell)
- "What else have you tried? / How are you dealing with it now?" (alternatives + price anchor)
- "Where does the money come from?" (B2B budget/stakeholders)
- "Who else should I talk to?" (end every chat with this)

## Product risk vs Market risk
- **Product**: Can I build it? grow it? will they keep using it? → validate by *building*
- **Market**: Do they want it? will they pay? are there enough? → validate by *talking*

## Commitment currencies
**Time** (next meeting / wireframe feedback / real trial) · **Reputation** (intro to boss/team / testimonial) · **Cash** (LOI / pre-order / deposit). Compliment = no currency = no data.

## Good meeting vs bad meeting
✅ "What are the next steps?" · "When can we start the trial?" · "Can I buy the prototype?" · "When can you talk to the rest of the team?"
❌ "That's so cool, I love it!" · "Looks great, let me know when it launches" · "I would definitely buy that" · vague "I'll intro you when you're ready"

## Framing a meeting — V/F/W/P/A
**V**ision (don't mention the idea) → **F**raming (stage, nothing to sell) → **W**eakness (the gap) → **P**edestal (they can help) → **A**sk.
Mnemonic: **Very Few Wizards Properly Ask.**

## Segmentation
- Before serving everyone, serve **someone**.
- Good segments are a **who-where pair** — keep **slicing** until you know where to find them.
- Pick by: **profitable · reachable · rewarding.**
- Mixed/contradictory feedback = segment too broad.

## Note symbols
:) excited · :( angry · :| embarrassed · ⚡ pain · ⊓ goal · ☐ obstacle · ↝ workaround · ^ context · ☑ feature/criteria · $ money · ♀ person/company · ☆ follow-up

## The process
**Before**: focused segment · big 3 questions · ideal next steps · who to talk to · best-guesses · desk research first.
**During**: frame · casual · Mom-Test questions · deflect/anchor/dig · notes · press for commitment.
**After**: review with team · transfer notes · update beliefs · next 3 questions.
The one prep question: **"What do we want to learn from these guys?"**

## Signs you're just going through the motions
Talking more than them · getting complimented · no clear next step · no notes · didn't review with team · an unexpected answer didn't change your idea · weren't scared of any question · not sure which big question you're answering.

## Hard truths
Opinions are worthless · the future is an over-optimistic lie · some problems don't matter · they own the problem, **you own the solution** · in early sales the goal is **learning**, revenue is a side-effect · **go build your dang company already.**
