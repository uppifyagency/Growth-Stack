# Chapter 5: Filming VSLs

## Core Idea
Production value is irrelevant; presence is everything. The goal of filming is only to convey "I'm a real person, here's my voice and body language, here's why you should call me." Screen-record slides, show your face, talk fast enough to hold attention, and accept multiple takes. The technical path: record in Loom → stitch in Camtasia → upload **unlisted** to YouTube → embed on the landing page.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **The "good enough" filming standard** — when to use: every VSL. Requirements are minimal and non-negotiable: (1) **light on your face** — daylight, light source behind the camera, not artificial; (2) **look presentable** — shower, don't look like a bum; (3) **show your face** (screen + camera), built-in webcam/mic is fine; (4) **talk fast enough** to hold interest (interest is set in the first ~3 seconds) but not so fast it's frantic; (5) expect **multiple takes** (3 to 10 is normal).
- **The record → stitch → host pipeline**:
  - **Loom** (Chrome extension) — record "screen and camera," use slide **presenter view** so you can read notes and anticipate the next slide for smooth flow.
  - **Camtasia** — set project to 1920×1080, import the clips (e.g., a talking-head intro + the slide screen-recording), resize each to fit, stitch into one continuous file, export via Share → Local File.
  - **YouTube unlisted** — upload, name it descriptively (e.g., "E-commerce Email Marketing"), mark "not made for kids," set **unlisted** (only viewable via link). Embed that link on the landing page.

## Key Concepts
- **Daylight beats artificial light** — always reads better on camera.
- **First-3-seconds rule** — attention is won or lost immediately; pace and energy must be up front.
- **Unlisted, not private/public** — unlisted won't show organically but works via embed link, and lets you **retarget viewers via Google Ads** by linking the YouTube channel to a Google Ads account.
- **Thumbnail (optional)** — your face + words behind it, made in Canva.

## Mental Models
- **Convey, don't produce** — the asset being delivered is *you*, not cinematography.
- **Iteration is the norm** — re-filming isn't failure; budget for it.

## Anti-patterns
- Chasing high production value — wasted effort that doesn't move trust (unless you're literally a design/video agency).
- Talking slowly with filler ("um, um") — loses viewers in seconds.
- Backlighting / artificial light that darkens or washes out your face.

## Key Takeaways
1. Light on face + presentable + fast-enough pace = the whole filming bar.
2. Loom → Camtasia (1080p) → YouTube unlisted → embed is the standard pipeline.
3. Unlisted YouTube hosting unlocks Google Ads viewer retargeting later.
