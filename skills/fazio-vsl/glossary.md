# Glossary — Video Sales Letter Mastery (Daniel Fazio)

**Big Win follow-up** — a follow-up email sharing a fresh client result; sent repeatedly as an infinite series ("big win, big win, big win") (Ch 4).
**Body language** — on-camera nonverbal signal that, with tonality, builds the trust text can't (Ch 1).
**Bump email** — a 3-days-later follow-up ("just bumping this to the top of your inbox"); reportedly drives ~30% of calls (Ch 4).
**Camtasia** — editing tool used to stitch clips into one 1920×1080 VSL file (Ch 5).
**Carrd** — $19/yr landing-page builder for simple high-ticket service pages (Ch 6).
**Calendly** — scheduling tool embedded for call booking; its bookings can trigger pixel/Conversions-API events (Ch 6, 7).
**ClickFunnels** — funnel builder for low-price productized services with checkout; Russell Brunson's company (Ch 2, 6).
**Component breakdown** — VSL beat listing the many pieces the service requires, to make DIY feel hard (Ch 2, 3).
**Concrete compliment** — a personalized opener so specific it could be sent to no one else; must pass the say-it-out-loud test (Ch 3).
**Conversions API** — server-side Facebook event tracking (via Zapier + Calendly) that bypasses iOS 14.5 blocking (Ch 7).
**Custom audience** — Facebook audience (site visitors, message senders, or bookers) used to target or exclude in ads (Ch 7).
**Daylight rule** — film with daylight on your face, source behind the camera; beats artificial light (Ch 5).
**Disqualification** — landing-page section stating who the offer isn't for, to filter out junk calls (Ch 6).
**Done-for-you (DFY)** — service where you do all the work; the default offer in the VSL (Ch 2, 4).
**Done-with-you (DWY)** — coaching/down-sell version (e.g., weekly call for 8 weeks) priced below DFY (Ch 2).
**Down-sell** — offering DWY when a prospect declines DFY on the call (Ch 2).
**First-3-seconds rule** — viewer interest is won or lost in the opening seconds; front-load energy/pace (Ch 5).
**Live chat** — on-site chat (ManyChat) letting in-market buyers talk instantly; a primary conversion lever (Ch 1, 7).
**Logical breakdown** — VSL beat showing the numbers (e.g., the 100-emails→1-client math) (Ch 2, 3).
**Loom** — Chrome extension to screen+camera record the VSL with slide presenter view (Ch 5).
**ManyChat** — tool that adds a Facebook-connected live-chat widget and builds messenger audiences (Ch 7).
**One-secret framework** — VSL built around a single "secret"/trick; the starter structure (Ch 2, 3).
**One-sentence case study** — single line stating who you are, what you do, and a client result (Ch 3, 4).
**Over-exaggerate difficulty** — slightly inflate how hard the secret/components are so paying you wins (Ch 2).
**Pay-now option** — instant-checkout button for cheap productized offers (vs. call booking) (Ch 2, 6).
**Personalization** — researched first line that makes a cold email read as personal, not commercial (Ch 3, 4).
**Pixel (Facebook)** — tracking snippet in the page head enabling visitor retargeting and event optimization (Ch 7).
**Pre-qualification** — only emailing/booking prospects who can already afford the service (Ch 3).
**Presenter view** — slide mode showing speaker notes/next slide for smooth delivery during recording (Ch 5).
**Prospect's question-order** — the fixed sequence of silent questions a buyer asks; the VSL's spine (Ch 2).
**Retargeting** — re-advertising to prior visitors/viewers; a backend optimization, not a primary lever (Ch 1, 7).
**Secret** — the specific knowledge/result you hold that the prospect doesn't; the core of any sale (Ch 2).
**Story ad** — casual face-to-camera Instagram/Facebook Story used as the retargeting creative (Ch 7).
**The bridge** — metaphor: prospect → result is a canyon; you are the bridge they associate with the win (Ch 2).
**Three-secrets framework** — VSL with three pain→resolution beats; coined by Russell Brunson (Ch 2, 4).
**Tonality** — vocal quality on camera that conveys trust beyond words (Ch 1).
**Trust** — the emotional thing every serious buyer is detecting; the VSL's whole job is to maximize it (Ch 1).
**Two buckets** — every cold email reads as obviously commercial (ignored) or obviously personal (opened) (Ch 4).
**Unlisted (YouTube)** — visibility setting: hidden from organic discovery, viewable via embed link; enables Google Ads view-retargeting (Ch 5).
**VSL (video sales letter)** — a recorded video on the landing page that pre-sells you and books more, easier calls (Ch 1).
**Webflow** — builder used when a genuinely polished site is needed (e.g., software companies) (Ch 6).
**Whiteboard webinar** — Dan Henry's delivery style: presenting at a whiteboard, face and body on camera (Ch 2).
**Zapier** — automation connecting Calendly bookings to Facebook Conversions API and custom audiences (Ch 7).
