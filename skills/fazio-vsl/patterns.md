# Patterns & Techniques — VSL Mastery (Daniel Fazio)

## The Prospect's Question-Order (script spine)
**When to use**: writing any VSL or landing page.
**How**: answer, in this exact sequence, the questions the buyer silently asks — Who is this? What do they do? How? Is it true? Can I trust them? Do they get results? Does this fit me? What's the next step? Friction is just an unanswered question arriving early.
**Trade-offs**: rigid by design; the order is the value — don't reshuffle it.

## The One-Secret VSL
**When**: your first VSL; offers built on a single trick.
**How**: backstory → reveal the secret (what/how/why it beats the norm) → bad-vs-good examples → quantifiable results → how-to (over-exaggerate difficulty) → logical breakdown → component breakdown → CTA.
**Trade-offs**: simplest to film; slightly lower conversion than three-secrets.

## The Three-Secrets / Three-Pain-Point VSL
**When**: after the one-secret works; want a small conversion lift + shareability.
**How**: hook → who am I → pain1→resolution → pain2→resolution → pain3→resolution → results → logical breakdown → component breakdown → CTA. Coined by Russell Brunson; whiteboard delivery from Dan Henry.
**Trade-offs**: more entertaining but a recognized cliché — never say "three secrets"; say "three things I do."

## The Big Win Follow-Up Ladder
**When**: prospect doesn't reply to the first email.
**How**: day-3 **bump** ("just bumping this to the top of your inbox") → then **Big Win #1, #2, #3…** each sharing a fresh client result, indefinitely. Good work makes case studies an infinite pool.
**Trade-offs**: the bump alone drives ~30% of calls; requires real wins to cite.

## The 100-Emails → 1-Client Math
**When**: the logical-breakdown beat; justifying volume.
**How**: 4% book a meeting (4/100), close 25% of meetings → 100 emails = 1 client; at 5 min × 100 = 8.3 hrs/client. Frame as "would you spend 8.3 hrs for a client?" → send an infinite number of emails.
**Trade-offs**: ratios are illustrative — substitute your real numbers.

## The Concrete-Compliment Test
**When**: writing the personalized first line.
**How**: spend ~5 min/prospect; reference a real news feature/post/award. Say it out loud — if you wouldn't say it to their face, rewrite. It must be un-sendable to anyone else.
**Trade-offs**: time-intensive, but ~10× reply rate vs. generic.

## The Down-Sell (DFY → DWY)
**When**: a prospect declines done-for-you on the call.
**How**: offer a done-with-you coaching version (e.g., weekly call × 8 weeks) priced slightly below DFY.
**Trade-offs**: lower margin per deal, but captures buyers who won't pay for full DFY.

## Pricing-Display Decision
**When**: deciding whether price appears in the VSL/page.
**How**: complex/high-ticket → omit exact price, say "minimum project size 3k+", keep any price on its own end slide (swap in Camtasia). Simple productized → show a pay-now price (e.g., $149/mo).
**Trade-offs**: showing price deters some but pre-filters; pay-now on complex offers creates refundable unqualified buyers.

## The Filming Pipeline
**When**: producing the VSL.
**How**: Loom (screen + camera, presenter view) → Camtasia (1920×1080, stitch clips, resize-to-fit, export local file) → YouTube **unlisted** (descriptive name, not-for-kids) → embed on landing page.
**Trade-offs**: low production value is fine and intended; expect 3–10 takes.

## The Single-Scroll Landing Page
**When**: building the page.
**How**: one column, pure white/black background, no nav — Headline → VSL → client interview → results → how it works → disqualification → Calendly. Carrd for high-ticket; ClickFunnels/Kajabi for productized checkout.
**Trade-offs**: content > design; only invest in polish if you're a design agency.

## Live Chat as a Primary Lever
**When**: every landing page.
**How**: ManyChat widget (head embed) + greeting + Facebook Business Suite app on phone for instant replies. Captures and re-markets to message senders.
**Trade-offs**: requires being reachable, but converts in-market buyers who want to talk now.

## Conversions-API Retargeting (iOS-proof)
**When**: retargeting after the basics work.
**How**: Pixel in page head + Zapier zap firing a Facebook **lead** event on each Calendly booking (server-side, unblockable); add bookers to an exclusion audience; run cheap (~$1/day) Story ads to visitors, optimized for the lead event, manual placements.
**Trade-offs**: backend optimization only — "helps a little"; don't prioritize over VSL + live chat.
