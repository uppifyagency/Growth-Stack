# Chapter 4: Intensifying Desire II — The 3-Step Process & the 18 Filters

## Core Idea
Fascinations are produced, not conjured: **find a nugget → tease the value & state the benefit → apply a filter (angle)**. The 18 filters are reusable formulas that also power headlines, subheads, and subject lines.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **The 3-Step Fascination Process** (Submodule I):
  1. **Find the nugget** — scan content for excerpts containing (a) valuable info + (b) the benefit of knowing it. Hunt **benefits first** ("where there's smoke, there's fire"); transcribe audio/video first. (Highlight every instance of value, e.g. in a Google Doc.)
  2. **Tease the value & state the benefit** — validate the nugget with an **If/Then** ("*If* you know X, *then* you get Y"). Optionally rewrite for clarity if the source is archaic. *Tease* = allude, don't reveal; *state* = say plainly what they gain.
  3. **Apply a filter** — slot it into one of the 18 angles to frame it and create variance.
  - Worked example: nugget "5 ways to get distribution" (p.39) → *"Finding distribution is one of the hardest parts of bringing a new product to market. It's also one of the most important. **Discover 5 refreshingly simple ways** to do it on page 39."* (Number filter)

## Key Concepts — The 18 Filters
1. **Mechanisms** — name your value as a "technology/system/process" in quotes (TempurPedic "SmartClimate"); signals uniqueness.
2. **Statement + Interest + Benefit** — 3 sentences: agreeable statement → tease value → state benefit (a mini Slippery Slide).
3. **"What…"** — conceals an action or info ("What top reps do differently…").
4. **"What never…"** — fear-based; we sprint from pain harder than we jog to pleasure.
5. **"Right? Wrong…"** — debunking; flips a held belief, challenging identity.
6. **"Why…"** — curiosity ("Curiosity is one of the strongest human incentives" — Hopkins); best on counterintuitive info.
7. **"When…"** — reveals timing or a system; inherently prescriptive.
8. **Specific question** — the brain auto-answers a question → built-in hook.
9. **"If, then…"** — self-identification; "If you… then you'll get…".
10. **"Quickest, easiest…"** — appeals to our hard-wired laziness.
11. **Number** — bundles strategies/secrets/reasons ("3-for-1" value psychology).
12. **Big promise** — leads with a **verb** + bold benefit; punchy hook.
13. **"How…"** — most common; "how to" is an implied promise of fulfillment.
14. **"Caution…"** — fear/self-preservation (also Warning/Danger/Stop); don't overuse or it loses credibility.
15. **"Truth…"** — controversy; truth makes someone right and someone wrong.
16. **"Best…"** — ego; people want to be / be associated with the best.
17. **"Surprising…"** — promise of surprise + benefit ("Guess what?!" hijacks attention).
18. **"Are you…"** — FOMO (also Do you / Is your / Does your); makes them question their current reality.

## Mental Models
- **Filter = angle = guardrail**: the hardest part is starting; a filter gives you a frame so the words come.
- **Master fascinations → master all short copy**: same skill writes great headlines, subheads, subject lines.
- **Learn by copyworking**: there's no fully prescriptive formula for assembly — read and hand-copy many fascinations until tone/cadence become automatic.

## Anti-patterns
- **Skipping the If/Then check** — without a real benefit, the nugget won't make a fascination.
- **Overusing fear filters** (#4, #14) — diminishing credibility with repetition.
- **Trying to assemble by formula alone** — internalization comes from reading + copyworking, not rules.

## Key Takeaways
1. Process = nugget → tease value & state benefit → apply a filter.
2. Hunt benefits to find the valuable techniques behind them.
3. Validate each nugget with an If/Then before writing.
4. Keep 18 filters on hand; rotate them to create variance.
5. Develop the feel by copyworking lots of examples.
