# Cheatsheet — The $100M Scaling Roadmap

## The 10 stages (theme · headcount · role · bottom-line graduation move)
| S | Theme | Head | Role | Graduate by… |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Improvise | 0 | Researcher | make something **free** to give away |
| 1 | Monetize | 1 | Starter | make your **first sale** |
| 2 | Advertise | 1+freelancers | Doer | consistent lead flow — **Rule of 100** |
| 3 | Stabilize | 1–4 | Trainer | get your **first hires** |
| 4 | Prioritize | 5–9 | Manager | **niche down** to one best customer |
| 5 | Productize | 10–19 | Director | add a **2nd premium product** (↑LTV:CAC) |
| 6 | Optimize | 20–49 | Leader | do everything **better, not more** |
| 7 | Categorize | 50–99 | Executive | **organize/triage** + fund channel #2 |
| 8 | Specialize | 100–249 | President | **dedicated teams** per task; prune |
| 9 | Capitalize | 250–500 | Chairman | make the **big bet** (M&A/R&D) |

## The system
- **3 elements of scaling**: Functions · People · Operations (the glue).
- **8 functions**: Product · Marketing · Sales · Customer Service · IT · Recruiting · HR · Finance.
- **Per stage**: headcount · role · leadership layers · # companies · constraint per function · graduation action · bottom line.
- **Rule**: find the **constraint**, take the **one graduation action**, ignore the rest. *Die by indigestion, not starvation.*

## Named frameworks by stage
- **0 Improvise**: Core Four (warm/cold/content/ads) · 4 R's (Review, Refer, Retain, Resell) · recruit yourself.
- **1 Monetize**: Value Equation · Proof > Promise (+continuum) · "What would it take?" / Magic Question · +20% price every 5 sales.
- **2 Advertise**: Rule of 100 · **CLOSER** + Three-Pillar Pitch (+ CLO/AAA two-step) · **HERE** / Angry Boat · Epiphany Bridge · IRS 20-point test.
- **3 Stabilize**: **RICE** (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Expense) · basic pipeline · payroll.
- **4 Prioritize**: niche→V2 · qualification + friction · manual sales KPIs.
- **5 Productize**: 2nd product · **Five A's** Game Tape · LTV:CAC · Peter Principle · CAT.
- **6 Optimize**: **Five M's** of activation · cohort segmentation · ad assembly · sales training.
- **7 Categorize**: 2nd channel · **Fast Cash Fridays** · reactive→proactive CS · ATS · HRIS.
- **8 Specialize**: dedicated teams · improve-by-deletion/prune · route best leads→best closers · brand-first messaging.
- **9 Capitalize**: cash cow vs rising star · M&A/R&D · Prep for Sale (Quality of Earnings) · strategic workforce planning.

## The Value Equation (Stage 1, core)
```
        Dream Outcome  ×  Perceived Likelihood of Achievement
Value = ───────────────────────────────────────────────────
              Time Delay  ×  Effort & Sacrifice
```
Moniker: make it **good · fast · risk-free · easy**. Hidden costs = the bottom of the equation.

## CLOSER (Stage 2 sales script)
**C**larify why they came · **L**abel the problem/plan · **O**verview past pains · **S**ell the vacation (not the flight) · **E**xplain away concerns · **R**einforce. Concerns to clear: time · stall · money · preference · authority.

## HERE (Stage 2 upset-customer script)
**H**ear out · **E**mpathize (get in the Angry Boat) · **A**pologize (own the impact) · **R**esolve *with* them (offer options). Never cower.

## Company counts (US, for perspective)
30M companies (Stages 1–2) → 24% have ≥1 employee (Stage 3) → 3M (Stage 4) → 2.4M (5) → 2.1M (6) → 1.2M (7) → 900K (8) → 300K, ~0.001% of starters (9).
