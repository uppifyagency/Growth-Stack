## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

**The thesis.** Businesses scale by **functions × headcount**, not by revenue. A $2M restaurant and a $100M software company both at 35 employees organize almost identically — so the transferable pattern of scaling is how you structure **people and functions** at each headcount band. The roadmap breaks 0→500 employees into **10 stages**, each with one **constraint** to break and one **bottom-line graduation move**. The meta-rule: **companies die by indigestion, not starvation** — find the constraint, take the one action, ignore everything else. *Strategy is prioritizing limited resources against the current constraint.*

**The 3 elements of scaling.** Every business = **Functions** (what it does to make money) + **People** (the *who*) + **Operations** (the glue: meetings, communication, training). Have all three and you can go $0→$100M.

**The 8 functions** (memorize the one-line definitions — the whole roadmap hangs off them): **Product** (goods/services/results delivered) · **Marketing** (how you tell people) · **Sales** (how you get money) · **Customer Service** (buy-again, refer, rate highly) · **IT** (gather/store/analyze/display info) · **Recruiting** (find & hire) · **HR** (reduce lawsuit risk) · **Finance** (report & manage money).

**The 10 stages** (theme · headcount · role · graduation move):
0 **Improvise** (0, Researcher) → make something **free** · 1 **Monetize** (1, Starter) → **first sale** · 2 **Advertise** (1+freelancers, Doer) → consistent leads (**Rule of 100**) · 3 **Stabilize** (1–4, Trainer) → **first hires** · 4 **Prioritize** (5–9, Manager) → **niche down** · 5 **Productize** (10–19, Director) → **2nd premium product** (↑LTV:CAC) · 6 **Optimize** (20–49, Leader) → **better, not more** · 7 **Categorize** (50–99, Executive) → **organize/triage** + 2nd channel · 8 **Specialize** (100–249, President) → **dedicated teams per task** · 9 **Capitalize** (250–500, Chairman) → the **big bet** (M&A/R&D).

**The signature named frameworks** (details in the chapters):
- **Value Equation** = `(Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)` → make it good, fast, risk-free, easy. Hidden costs live in the denominator. (Ch 4)
- **Core Four** — the only ways to get attention: warm outreach, cold outreach, post content, paid ads. (Ch 3)
- **Rule of 100** — 100 primary advertising actions every day; consistency beats cleverness. (Ch 5)
- **CLOSER** — sales script: Clarify, Label, Overview, Sell (the vacation not the flight), Explain away, Reinforce; + **Three-Pillar Pitch**, + **CLO/AAA** for two-step sales. (Ch 5)
- **Proof > Promise** — belief scales with proof; collect 10+ testimonials before selling; raise price **20% every 5 sales**. (Ch 4)
- **HERE / Angry Boat** — Hear, Empathize, Apologize, Resolve; only one person can be in the angry boat. (Ch 5)
- **Epiphany Bridge** — testimonial script: internal/external struggle → skepticism → why-now → victory. (Ch 5)
- **4 R's** — Review, Refer, Retain, Resell (the customer-service objective). (Ch 3)
- **RICE** — prioritize by Reach, Impact, Confidence, Expense. (Ch 6)
- **Five A's (Game Tape)** — Arrange, Assemble, Assess, Analyze, Act — weekly cross-dept call review. (Ch 8)
- **Five M's of Activation** — Map, Measure, hypothesis, Monitor, iterate — to cut churn. (Ch 9)
- **Peter Principle** — don't promote people to their point of incompetence. (Ch 8)
- **Cash cow vs Rising Star / Prep for Sale** — protect the core, buy/build the next bet, get a Quality of Earnings. (Ch 12)

**How to use it in practice.** Diagnose your stage by headcount → read that stage's constraint per function and its single bottom-line move → work only that stage (± one). Being ahead on a function while skipping a step means that step becomes a **weak link** later.
