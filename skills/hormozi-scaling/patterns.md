# Patterns & Playbooks — The $100M Scaling Roadmap

Concrete, step-by-step methods. Each: when to use · how · trade-offs.

## Diagnose Your Stage
**When to use**: any time you're deciding what to work on.
**How**: (1) count full-time **headcount** → find your stage band (0 / 1 / 1–4 / 5–9 / 10–19 / 20–49 / 50–99 / 100–249 / 250–500); (2) confirm with your **role** (Researcher→Chairman); (3) read that stage's **constraint** per function and its **one bottom-line graduation move**; (4) work only that stage (± one).
**Trade-offs**: guidelines not laws — if you're ahead on a function but skipped a step, that step is a **weak link** to shore up.

## Free-First Launch (Stage 0)
**When**: 0 customers, no proof.
**How**: pick a free mechanism (software/media/service/physical) shaped as reveal-a-problem, free-trial, or free-first-step → run the **Core Four** (start warm outreach) → service free customers ruthlessly for the **4 R's** → collect 10+ testimonials.
**Trade-offs**: no revenue yet; the payoff is learning + proof. "Learn more than earn."

## First Sale (Stage 1)
**When**: have proof, no paid revenue.
**How**: remove **hidden costs** to reach a sellable **V1** → pitch **proof > promise** using the proof continuum → script: *"Since I'm only taking 5 people… 80% off, so long as you use it and leave a review — fair?"* → **raise price 20% every 5 sales** (free→80→60→40→20→full) → set up ≥2 payment methods and run a real dollar through.
**Trade-offs**: premium/unscalable delivery early; that's correct — value before scale.

## Consistent Lead Flow — Rule of 100 (Stage 2)
**When**: feast-famine lead flow.
**How**: pick one Core Four channel; do **100 primary actions daily** (100 dials / 100 DMs / ~100 min content / ~100 min or $100 ads). Never stop when busy.
**Trade-offs**: brute-force and time-heavy — but consistency is the point.

## CLOSER Sales Call (Stage 2+)
**When**: one-to-one selling.
**How**: **C**larify why they came → **L**abel the problem/goal → **O**verview past pains (loop "what else have you tried? how'd it work? what did you like/not?") → **S**ell via the **Three-Pillar Pitch** (3 keys, claim + analogy each, 2–3 min) → **E**xplain away concerns (time, stall, money, preference, authority) → **R**einforce. Two-step? Set with **CLO**, close with **AAA**.
**Trade-offs**: needs reps to sound natural; over-pitching (20-min monologues) kills it.

## Handle an Upset Customer — HERE / Angry Boat (Stage 2+)
**When**: an angry customer.
**How**: **H**ear them fully (vent, short acknowledgments) → **E**mpathize and escalate past their upset (get in the Angry Boat) → **A**pologize (take accountability for impact) → **R**esolve *with* them via options. Never cower.
**Trade-offs**: emotionally demanding; requires the skill of hard conversations.

## Harvest a Testimonial — Epiphany Bridge (Stage 2+)
**When**: a happy customer.
**How**: internal struggle (rock-bottom feelings) → external struggle (objective metrics) → skepticism (pre-purchase concerns) → why-they-did-it-anyway (why us/why now) → internal + external victory. Lead with pain — prospects relate to it more than the solution.

## Prioritize Fixes — RICE (Stage 3+)
**When**: can't fix everything.
**How**: score each item by **R**each × **I**mpact × **C**onfidence ÷ **E**xpense; do high-impact/low-investment first. When in doubt, fix the single thing customers complain about most.

## Niche Down (Stage 4)
**When**: too many product directions, unqualified leads.
**How**: specialize product to a **V2** for one avatar → boost content volume, then **add qualification + friction** so volume self-selects → track sales KPIs (contact/schedule/show/offer/close/cash) on a spreadsheet.
**Trade-offs**: you deliberately turn away non-fit revenue — go deep, not wide.

## Add Product #2 — Raise LTV:CAC (Stage 5)
**When**: low money per customer, "nothing else to buy."
**How**: connect **CS → Product** to learn the next need → build a **second premium product** → now that LTV is higher, afford higher CAC (lead scoring, attribution, CRO) → run weekly **Game Tape (Five A's)**.
**Trade-offs**: split attention across two products — needs owners/pipelines.

## Reduce Churn — Five M's + Cohorts (Stage 6)
**When**: churn rising with volume.
**How**: **Map** the journey → **Measure** each step → **Make a hypothesis** on activation points → **Monitor** results → iterate; segment customers by **cohort** and attack churn per type.

## Fund a Second Channel (Stage 7)
**When**: one volatile acquisition channel.
**How**: first pad with near-free wins — stronger **referral process** (**Fast Cash Fridays**) + **segmented nurture by lead score** → use the surplus to fund the **most-similar** new channel → triage/organize every overwhelmed system into buckets; install **ATS** + **HRIS**.

## Specialize & Prune (Stage 8)
**When**: no one can know everything; product is bloated.
**How**: create **dedicated teams/roles per task** → survey customers, **prune** low-value features (improve by deletion), convert cuts to cross-sells → route best leads to best closers → blend customer journeys → standardize hiring selection with founder final-fit → reinforce brand & culture messaging.

## Make the Big Bet (Stage 9)
**When**: unsure where next growth comes from; cash cow maxed.
**How**: protect the **cash cow**; find the **rising star** (buy via **M&A** or build via **R&D**, 1–3 yrs out) → go **brand-first/omnichannel** with blended ROAS → **Prep for Sale** (Quality of Earnings, banker review, capital-structure audit) to fund it → **strategic workforce planning**; founder personally recruits the stars.
**Trade-offs**: long horizons and capital risk; hedge big investments.
