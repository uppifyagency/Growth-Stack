# Chapter 7: Stage 4 — Prioritize (headcount 5–9, role: Manager)

## Core Idea
You said yes to everyone, so feedback pulls the product in too many directions. Now you have your **first team layer** and become a **Manager**. The graduation move is to **niche down**: pick one (or your best) customer and go deep, not wide. Only ~3M US companies reach this size.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Niche-down to V2 (product)**: specialize the product into a **V2** priced for a **niched-down avatar**. Most people go wide when they should go deep.
- **Volume + friction self-selection (marketing)**: too many unqualified leads → make better, more creative free content to boost volume, then **add qualification and friction** so the extra volume self-selects. (Usually you don't need a second channel yet — you're just not maximizing the first.)
- **Manual KPI tracking (sales)**: track on a spreadsheet, not a CRM yet — **contact rate, schedule rate, show rate, offer rate, close rate, cash collected**. Fix dropping speed-to-contact.
- **CRM for notes + CS metrics**: install customer-success KPIs; centralize per-customer notes in a CRM so the team can coordinate.

## Key Concepts
- **Shared platforms (IT)**: get everyone on the same tools — project management (Monday, Asana), comms (Slack), password manager (LastPass).
- **Proper interviews (recruiting)**: learn to conduct real interviews and check references.
- **Employee handbook (HR)**: install policies (PTO, sick days, dress) so employees know how to behave.
- **P&L + cash flow + insurance (finance)**: cash is lumpy from one-time expenses; set up P&L and cash-flow statements and basic business insurance.

## Mental Models
- **Riches in niches**: being everything to everyone is the constraint; one well-served avatar out-earns many poorly-served ones.
- **Self-selecting funnels**: friction is a feature — it filters volume so your team talks only to fits.

## Anti-patterns
- **Going wide** (new channels, more customer types) when the fix is going **deep** on one.
- **Buying a CRM** to track sales you could track on a Google Sheet.

## Key Takeaways
1. **Niche down** to one best customer; specialize to a **V2**.
2. Boost lead volume, then **add qualification/friction** to self-select.
3. Track **sales KPIs manually**; centralize CS notes in a CRM.
4. Install **handbook, P&L/cash-flow, insurance**.
