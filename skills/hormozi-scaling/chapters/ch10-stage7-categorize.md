# Chapter 10: Stage 7 — Categorize (headcount 50–99, role: Executive)

## Core Idea
Every system is overwhelmed and disorganized. As an **Executive** with three leadership layers, the graduation move is to **triage and organize** — sort everything into buckets — while **funding a second acquisition channel** off cheap wins (referrals + nurture). ~1.2M US companies (≤4%) reach here.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Fund the second acquisition channel (marketing)**: lead flow is volatile with one channel. Pad yourself first with two near-free wins — a **stronger referral process** and **segmented lead nurture by lead score** — then use that extra cash to fund the **most-similar** new channel. (There's a whole process for picking the next channel.)
- **Fast Cash Fridays (referral promo)**: run **mini promotional cycles** for referrals ("this month, refer this Friday for double") — if referral pushes run all the time they stop feeling important.
- **Reactive → Proactive CS**: stop firefighting; build a **proactive customer journey** with communication matched to each milestone; focus on **renewals and ascensions**.
- **Kudos culture (sales)**: better schedule coverage for throughput, a **lead-nurture checklist**, and public **kudos** for reps whose show rates match their close rates.

## Key Concepts
- **ATS — Applicant Tracking System (recruiting)**: hiring is too slow and roles too many to track; install an ATS (a "CRM/sales pipeline for candidates") + specialized recruiting firms for hard-to-fill skills.
- **HRIS — HR Information System (HR)**: employee data is scattered and unprotected; centralize it (promotions, dates, records) in an HRIS to stay compliant at ~100 people.
- **Cloud/network IT**: data/IP/access are disorganized; move to the cloud, get a data host, centralize hardware (storage closet on-site / cloud if remote).
- **Departmental finance controls**: your team now spends your money less well than you — install quarterly expense management, **budgets by department, cards with limits, an approval process**, more insurance, better projections.

## Mental Models
- **Triage everything**: this bucket of leads here, applicants there — organization *is* the deliverable.
- **Pad before you expand**: fund channel #2 from referral/nurture surplus, not from risk.

## Anti-patterns
- **Opening a second channel** before strengthening referrals/nurture to pay for it.
- **Running referral pushes constantly** until they lose all urgency.

## Key Takeaways
1. **Organize/triage** every overwhelmed system into buckets.
2. Fund a **second acquisition channel** from referral + nurture surplus (**Fast Cash Fridays**).
3. Go **proactive** in CS (journey, renewals, ascensions).
4. Install **ATS** and **HRIS**; move IT to the cloud; add departmental spend controls.
