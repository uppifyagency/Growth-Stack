# Chapter 4: Stage 1 — Monetize (headcount 1, role: Starter)

## Core Idea
Cross the threshold into being a real company by making your **first paid sale**. The product isn't good enough to charge for yet — fix it to a sellable **V1** by removing hidden costs, then sell on **proof, not promise**.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **The Value Equation** (the core of *$100M Offers*): `Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)`. Increase the top, drive the bottom toward zero. Moniker: make it **good, fast, risk-free, easy**.
- **"What would it take?" + the Magic Question**: assume the purchase and work backward into features. Magic question: *"If we worked together for $3,000 over 3 months to hit [goal], what would you need to see to be excited to move forward?"* — sets price first, makes them paint the ideal state.
- **Proof > Promise**: people believe your promise only in proportion to your proof. Collect **10+ testimonials before selling**. The **proof continuum** (stronger → weaker): in-person > virtual · live > recorded · raw > processed · show > tell · others-say > you-say · identical-to-prospect > opposite · personal > generic · big+small results > bad/none · new > old · **lots (overwhelming social proof)** > one · numbers > none · brand track record > none.
- **Price ladder — raise 20% every 5 sales**: free → 80% off → 60% → 40% → 20% → full price → keep climbing. Find the threshold that maximizes customers × LTV.
- **Result issue vs Experience issue (CS)**: people stick more when **experience is good & result bad** than the reverse. Fix results (set expectations, match goals, define workflow); fix experience (easier, faster, more pleasant).

## Key Concepts
- **V1 product**: good enough to charge for, not perfect.
- **Free ad credits**: Google, Bing, Pinterest, Snapchat, LinkedIn, TikTok give $50–$500 to start.
- **Vendor agreements (HR)**: who does what, where, by when, cost, how long, who says it's good, what if it isn't — clarify *before* starting, not to sue but to align.
- **Collect money (finance)**: set up ≥2 payment methods (cash + card/processor: Stripe, Shopify, Amazon, Skool). Graduate = a deposit hits your business account.
- **Freelancers over hires (recruiting)**: fill skill/time gaps via Upwork/Fiverr/99designs — needs change fast, so avoid full-time commitment.

## Anti-patterns
- **Selling before you have proof**; using **generic, old, text-only testimonials** and wondering why no one opts in.
- **Refusing to charge premium** because you pre-decided you "won't do X" *divorced from price* — there's usually a price at which you would.

## Key Takeaways
1. Fix the product to a sellable **V1** by removing **hidden costs**.
2. Lead every pitch with **proof**; gather 10+ testimonials first.
3. Use the free→paid script and **raise price 20% every 5 sales**.
4. Get paid: ≥2 payment methods, run a real dollar through the pipe.
