# Chapter 9: Stage 6 — Optimize (headcount 20–49, role: Leader)

## Core Idea
Everything is inefficient. With two products and two full management layers (director + manager), you now grow by doing everything **better, not more**. You're a **Leader** leading leaders. ~2.1M US companies reach here.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **The Five M's of Activation (CS / reduce churn)**: a customer-activation loop — **Map the journey** (key milestones from onboarding to ongoing delivery) → **Measure** each step → **Make a hypothesis** (identify the key activation points / behaviors, e.g. "they bring in their partner") → **Monitor results** → iterate. Activation = getting customers to the behaviors that make them stick.
- **Cohort segmentation (CS)**: segment customers by **cohort and activation points** so you can attack churn per customer type rather than in aggregate.
- **Ad assembly process (marketing)**: as the company expands, traffic gets colder and CAC rises — install a repeatable **ad-assembly line** to increase creative volume.
- **Sales training system (sales)**: closing efficiency on the core product drops → install training systems, **individual coaching**, and team cadence to make closing consistent (refunds down *and* closes up).

## Key Concepts
- **Product pipelines with owners (product)**: incremental improvement processes for *both* products, each with a separate owner.
- **Security + on/offboarding (IT)**: more people in and out → cybersecurity, malware/password protection, formal on/offboarding to protect IP, data, access.
- **Headhunting + multi-filter interviews (recruiting)**: roles now exceed your network; learn to headhunt higher-level roles with multi-stage skill filtering.
- **Full comp packages (HR)**: higher-level talent expects benefits, retirement, bonus — set up an advanced compensation plan.
- **Scale AR/AP + balance sheet (finance)**: track accounts receivable/payable granularly; add balance-sheet visibility; pay taxes quarterly.

## Mental Models
- **Better, not more**: at this stage efficiency (not volume) is the growth lever.
- **Churn is segmented, not singular**: reduce it cohort by cohort.

## Anti-patterns
- **Adding more volume** to a leaky, inefficient machine.
- **Letting churn hide in aggregate numbers** instead of segmenting.

## Key Takeaways
1. Grow by **optimizing**, not adding.
2. Fight churn with the **Five M's of activation** + **cohort segmentation**.
3. Systematize ads (assembly line) and sales (training/coaching/cadence).
4. Harden IT security; scale AR/AP with balance-sheet visibility.
