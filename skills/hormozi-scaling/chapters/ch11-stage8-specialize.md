# Chapter 11: Stage 8 — Specialize (headcount 100–249, role: President)

## Core Idea
No single person can know everything anymore. As **President** (four layers incl. a full executive team), the graduation move is to create **dedicated people and teams for specific tasks** — and to **prune** what's bloated. Fewer than 900K US companies get here.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Improve through deletion (product)**: the product has become a feature-bloated "Frankenstein." **Survey customers** to learn what they truly value, **prune** what they don't, and turn pruned pieces into **cross-sells / add-ons**.
- **Route best leads → best closers (sales)**: closing efficiency on the premium product drops → route the highest-value leads to the best closers and install **automated metrics tracking by closer and by product**.
- **Blended customer journey (CS)**: multiple product journeys now conflict/mis-time → merge them into **one blended journey** with proactive cross-recommendations.
- **Standardize selection + founder final-fit (recruiting)**: recruiters hold inconsistent standards and culture suffers → standardize the **selection process** with a **founder final fit check**.

## Key Concepts
- **Brand-building marketing**: broad creative brings lower-quality leads and dilutes the brand (CAC rises) → grow the brand with **standardized messaging, big free value, strong positive brand associations**; re-hone core messaging and keep *pouring into* the audience, not just extracting.
- **Specialized tech stacks + ticketing (IT)**: departments outgrow general software → per-department tech stacks, an **internal inquiry/ticketing process**, staffing, departmental processes and **SLAs**.
- **Group onboarding + performance management (HR)**: volume creates performance/compliance issues → group onboarding (software), documented performance management, employee liability insurance, and **reinforced cultural values** (same "broad messaging loses potency" problem as marketing, applied to culture).
- **Renegotiate + audit (finance)**: you're the big dog — **renegotiate rates on everything**, hire specialized finance roles (esp. tax), and run **yearly external audits** (most finance failures are internal).

## Mental Models
- **Specialization raises productivity**: dedicated people/teams per task beat generalists at scale.
- **Deletion is improvement**: at scale, subtracting features/products can raise value.

## Anti-patterns
- **Piling features on** an already-overwhelming product.
- **Letting broad messaging dilute** both brand and culture.

## Key Takeaways
1. Build **dedicated teams/roles per task**; specialize.
2. **Prune** the product (improve by deletion); convert cut pieces to cross-sells.
3. Route best leads to best closers; blend the customer journey.
4. Standardize hiring selection; reinforce brand & culture messaging; audit finances.
