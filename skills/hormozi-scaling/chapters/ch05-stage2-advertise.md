# Chapter 5: Stage 2 — Advertise (headcount 1 + freelancers, role: Doer)

## Core Idea
The last stage before your first full-time team. Lead flow and sales are inconsistent because *you* are inconsistent — so make advertising a **daily, non-negotiable input** (Rule of 100) and nail a repeatable **sales script**. Your role is **Doer**: be in the weeds, no job beneath you.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **The Rule of 100**: do **100 primary actions of advertising every single day** — 100 dials, 100 outreaches, 100 texts/emails, ~100 minutes of content, or ~100 minutes/$100 on paid ads. Consistency > cleverness. Never stop advertising just because you have enough customers.
- **CLOSER (sales script)**: **C**larify why they came · **L**abel them with the problem/plan to solve · **O**verview their past pains & experiences · **S**ell the vacation, not the plane flight · **E**xplain away concerns · **R**einforce the decision. Simple frame: *where are you now → where do you want to be → what's stopping you → here's how I help → reinforce.*
- **The Three-Pillar Pitch** (the "S" in CLOSER): boil success down to 3 things (e.g. fitness / nutrition / accountability — "three legs of the stool"). One-line claim + an analogy per pillar. Keep it 2–3 minutes; spend most time on *their* deprivation, not your pitch.
- **CLO + AAA (two-step / setting sale)**: when setting, run only **CLO** (don't sell — get them "hungry," then hand off). On the closing call use **AAA**: **A**cknowledge ("got it, so I'm hearing X, right?") → **A**sk/**A**nswer the next question, looping seamlessly.
- **The HERE framework + the Angry Boat (CS for upset customers)**: **H**ear them out (let them vent, short acknowledgments) · **E**mpathize & escalate beyond their upset · **R**esolve *with* them (offer options). The **Angry Boat**: only one person can be in it — get *more* upset on the customer's behalf and they de-escalate. Never cower; always confront hard conversations.
- **The Epiphany Bridge script (getting testimonials)**: walk a happy customer from **internal struggle** (rock bottom feelings) → **external struggle** (objective metrics: weight, size, revenue) → **skepticism** (concerns before buying) → **why they did it anyway** (why us / why now) → loop back to **internal & external victory**. People relate to the *pain* more than the solution.

## Key Concepts
- **Valuable over scalable**: fix the product in unscalable ways; new entrepreneurs waste time making offers scalable before making them valuable.
- **Deprivation drives action**: motivate by amplifying the felt gap ("when did you last have regular lead flow?").
- **Contractor → employee (HR)**: the **IRS 20-point test** — ~5 "yes" answers means they're really an employee; convert to W-2 to avoid back-taxes/penalties. Business is **risk management**, not risk-free.
- **Finance discipline**: QuickBooks equivalent, save for **quarterly taxes**, **check your bank account daily** (fast feedback loop beats precision now).

## Anti-patterns
- **Stopping advertising when busy** → the feast-famine cycle.
- **Trying to look like Alex/Leila** instead of being the Doer in the dirt.
- **Cowering from upset customers** because you lack the skill of hard conversations.

## Key Takeaways
1. **Rule of 100** daily via the **Core Four** — consistency is the whole game.
2. Run **CLOSER** (+ Three-Pillar Pitch; CLO+AAA if two-step).
3. Handle anger with **HERE / the Angry Boat**; harvest proof with the **Epiphany Bridge**.
4. Go legit: W-2 conversions, quarterly taxes, daily bank check.
