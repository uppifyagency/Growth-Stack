# Chapter 2: The 10 Stages Overview (Context)

## Core Idea
A 1,000-foot map of the whole roadmap: 10 stages from 0 to 500 employees, each with a one-word theme, a founder role, a leadership structure, and a bottom-line graduation move. The deeper the stage, the more complexity — early trainings are short (few people, few things), late ones are deep.

## The 10 Stages (theme · headcount · role · # companies · bottom line → graduate)

| Stage | Theme | Head | Role | Companies (US) | Bottom line → graduation move |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Improvise** | 0 | Researcher | — (not a company yet) | Nothing is happening → **make something to give away free** |
| 1 | **Monetize** | 1 | Starter | 30M | Business makes no money → **make your first sale** |
| 2 | **Advertise** | 1 (+freelancers) | Doer | 30M | New customers inconsistent → **let more people know (Rule of 100)** |
| 3 | **Stabilize** | 1–4 | Trainer | 24% have ≥1 employee | Too much for one person → **get your first hires / help** |
| 4 | **Prioritize** | 5–9 | Manager | 3M | Being everything to everyone → **niche down to one best customer** |
| 5 | **Productize** | 10–19 | Director | 2.4M | Not enough $ per customer (low LTV:CAC) → **make & sell a 2nd premium product** |
| 6 | **Optimize** | 20–49 | Leader | 2.1M | Everything inefficient → **grow by doing everything *better*, not more** |
| 7 | **Categorize** | 50–99 | Executive | 1.2M | Every system overwhelmed & disorganized → **triage/organize + fund a 2nd acquisition channel** |
| 8 | **Specialize** | 100–249 | President | 900K | No one is good enough to know everything → **dedicated people & teams per task** |
| 9 | **Capitalize** | 250–500 | Chairman | 300K | Unsure where the next big growth comes from → **make your big bet (M&A / R&D)** |

## Key Concepts
- **Role progression**: Researcher → Starter → Doer → Trainer → Manager → Director → Leader → Executive → President → Chairman. Your job shifts from *doing* to *allocating resources between divisions*.
- **Leadership layers** grow from "just you" → one layer → 1.5 layers → 2 full layers → 3 layers → 4 layers + executive team → division leaders/CEOs.
- **Function arcs across stages** (how a function matures): e.g. IT goes hardware → free tools → shared platforms → company-owned hardware → cloud/security → specialized tech stacks → ERP; Recruiting goes recruit-yourself → freelancers → convert to full-time → interviews → headhunting → ATS → founder-recruits-stars.

## Mental Models
- **The theme names the graduation move.** One word per stage tells you what you must *do* to advance (Improvise → Monetize → Advertise → …).
- **Complexity compounds with headcount** — expect later stages to demand systems, not hustle.

## Key Takeaways
1. Memorize the **10 themes in order** — they're the spine of the whole system.
2. Each stage has exactly **one bottom-line graduation move**; the table above is the cheat sheet.
3. Find your headcount band, work that stage (± one), ignore the rest.
