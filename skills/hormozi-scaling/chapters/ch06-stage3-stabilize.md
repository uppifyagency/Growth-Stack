# Chapter 6: Stage 3 — Stabilize (headcount 1–4, role: Trainer)

## Core Idea
There's too much for one person, so you make your **first full-time hires** and become a **Trainer**. Only 24% of businesses ever get one employee beyond the founder — crossing this line moves you up a big percentile. The job now is to stop everything from breaking as you add people.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **The RICE framework (prioritization)**: score what to fix/do by **R**each · **I**mpact · **C**onfidence · **E**xpense. Do the high-impact / low-investment items first; deprioritize low-impact / high-investment. Use it to pick "the one thing customers complain most about" when you can't fix everything.
- **Basic sales pipeline**: define how people come in → schedule → show → convert. Even on paper — just make customer flow predictable.
- **Long-term nurture (marketing)**: friends & family are used up; colder audiences don't buy fast, so add **email and/or content nurture**.
- **Qualification + outsource setting (sales)**: decide basic buyer qualifications; outsource setting/calendaring so you spend time with *good* prospects.

## Key Concepts
- **Consistent onboarding (CS)**: new customers feel lost because you're no longer the one onboarding them — build a repeatable onboarding.
- **Tool triage (IT)**: cut the free trials you don't use; keep and extend the useful ones (hunt discounts/credits).
- **Recruiting basics**: learn to write a job description and review résumés.
- **Payroll provider (HR)**: stop paying via Venmo/PayPal/checks; get payroll so state taxes/fees are withheld — avoid liens.
- **Bookkeeping (finance)**: set up processing, manage invoices & payments so cash doesn't stall your business.

## Mental Models
- **Fix the loudest thing first**: with no time to fix everything, RICE-rank and address the single biggest complaint.
- **Predictability over polish**: a paper pipeline you actually use beats a CRM you don't.

## Anti-patterns
- **Paying people informally** (liability + liens).
- **Onboarding inconsistently** because "it used to just be me."
- **Hoarding unused free-trial tools** that quietly start charging.

## Key Takeaways
1. **Get help** — your first 1–4 full-time hires.
2. Prioritize fixes with **RICE**.
3. Add **nurture**, **qualification**, **consistent onboarding**, and a **basic pipeline**.
4. Get **payroll + bookkeeping** legit.
