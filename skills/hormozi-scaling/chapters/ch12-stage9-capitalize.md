# Chapter 12: Stage 9 — Capitalize (headcount 250–500, role: Chairman)

## Core Idea
You run a conglomeration of mini-businesses (division leaders / CEOs) and your job is **allocating resources between divisions**. You can't touch the **cash cow** without making it worse, yet you still must grow — so the graduation move is to **make a big bet on the future** via M&A or R&D, funded by capital. Only ~300K companies (~0.001% of starters) reach here.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Cash cow vs Rising Star (product)**: changes to the successful core product (the **cash cow**) now only make it worse — but you still need new growth. Find the next **rising star**: **buy it (M&A)** or **build it (R&D)**, thinking 1–3 years out to capture a whole market/trend right as it needs you.
- **Brand-first, omnichannel marketing**: lead attribution by channel breaks down → shift to **brand-first advertising**, omnichannel, **blended ROAS across products**, plus a compliance process. Think top-of-funnel/awareness/sponsorships/collabs, not just arbitraging direct-response ROAS.
- **Sales specialization by product/lead/process/territory**: too many leads and products for any one seller → salespeople specialize by product, prospect, process, and sometimes territory (e.g. D2C vs wholesale).
- **Prep for Sale (finance)**: to fund the big bet, raise/borrow/sell equity. Run a **prep for sale**: get a **Quality of Earnings**, talk to investment bankers, audit your **capital structure** and entities, get legal review, and consider **hedging** big investments to smooth the profit curve. "Cleaning up the house always makes the house better" (e.g. a $20M credit line in your back pocket).

## Key Concepts
- **Strategic workforce planning (HR)**: at 250+ employees, even 5% churn = losing 12–25 people/month you must backfill. Forecast the people side of growth — capacity, skill gaps, redundancies, generalists → **hyper-specialization**.
- **Founder recruits the stars (recruiting)**: the best talent in the world only responds to the **founder/CEO** — expect 6–12+ months of courting to sell them on your vision.
- **Specialize CS reps (CS)**: support for new offerings breaks under volume → triage and specialize CS reps to shorten training.
- **New ERP / specialized tech (IT)**: the old stack is insufficient; hire specialized tech roles per major function and may need an **ERP** to tie disparate systems together.

## Mental Models
- **Allocate capital across divisions** like a portfolio, not operate them.
- **Big bets, long horizons**: growth now means capturing a market, not tweaking the cash cow.

## Anti-patterns
- **Tinkering with the cash cow** and degrading what made you.
- **Waiting until you need capital** to clean up your financials.

## Key Takeaways
1. Protect the **cash cow**; grow via a **rising star** (M&A or R&D).
2. Go **brand-first / omnichannel**; specialize sales by product/lead/territory.
3. **Prep for sale** (Quality of Earnings, banker review, capital audit) to fund the bet.
4. **Strategic workforce planning**; the **founder personally recruits stars**.
