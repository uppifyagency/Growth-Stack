# Chapter 13: Free Bonus (Call to Action — no framework)

## Core Idea
This module contains **no scaling content** — it's a closing call-to-action. After finishing *$100M Offers*, *$100M Leads*, or the *$100M Scaling Roadmap*, Hormozi asks viewers to fill out a short feedback form.

## What it is
- **The ask**: a ~60-second form describing where you started, results you've had, and where you are today.
- **Why**: feedback for the trainings, and social proof for the media side (Mosey Media) where formalized feedback is scarce.
- **The incentives ("cookies")**: (1) monthly featured stories on the site; (2) a **random monthly draw** to be invited to the Las Vegas headquarters for two days.

## Key Takeaway
Nothing to apply here — it's a testimonial/feedback capture. (Note the tactic itself, though: it's the **Epiphany Bridge / proof-gathering** motion from Ch 5, run at the end of a value-delivery sequence.)

## Connects To
- **Ch 5 (Advertise)**: getting testimonials via the Epiphany Bridge — this bonus *is* that motion in practice.
