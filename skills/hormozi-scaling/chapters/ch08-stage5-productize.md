# Chapter 8: Stage 5 — Productize (headcount 10–19, role: Director)

## Core Idea
You've niched down so well that customers have **nothing else to buy** — and you're not making enough money per customer (low **LTV:CAC**). The graduation move is a **second, premium product**, which raises LTV and lets you afford more expensive marketing. You're now a **Director** (~1.5 leadership layers).

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Connect CS → Product to build product #2**: mine customer success for what customers want next, then build a **second premium product** to sell to existing customers. Raising LTV:CAC is the whole point of the stage.
- **The Five A's — "Game Tape" review**: a weekly 60–90 min cross-departmental (sales, product, marketing, CS) film-study of real calls. **A**rrange the call → **A**ssemble (head of sales pulls the tape) → **A**ssess (each dept: what can I take from this call?) → **A**nalyze (the *one* thing each will do) → **A**ct (commit to it within 7 days, by next game tape). Aligns what you *promise* vs *deliver*.
- **LTV:CAC economics**: with a second product, LTV rises, so you can afford higher CAC → score leads, install **attribution**, and run a **CRO** (conversion-rate-optimization) process.
- **The Peter Principle (recruiting)**: people get promoted to their **point of incompetence** (usually out of convenience). Avoid it with **deliberate promotions from within** and incentivize referrals up to your **CAT (Cost to Acquire Talent)**.

## Key Concepts
- **Sales aids + feedback metrics**: build sales webinars/presentations; install **NPS, CSAT, refund rate**; consider "buy rep" (buying sales reps).
- **CS playbook + ascension path**: onboard/train the team to answer questions consistently; give customers a path to ascend.
- **Company-owned hardware (IT)**: stop employees using personal laptops/phones (they walk out with your leads/media); provide controlled hardware + a basic dashboard.
- **Termination policy (HR)**: you'll fire your first bad hires — create termination process to avoid exposure.
- **Budget & forecasting (finance)**: make a budget, save for big expenses, forecast simply, track write-offs to fund growth.

## Mental Models
- **Grow LTV before scaling spend**: a second product is the lever that makes expensive marketing affordable.
- **Promote for competence, not convenience** (anti-Peter-Principle).

## Anti-patterns
- **Improving only one product** while customers churn for lack of next purchase.
- **Promoting your best doer into a manager role they'll fail at.**

## Key Takeaways
1. Build & sell a **second premium product** (raise **LTV:CAC**).
2. Run weekly **Game Tape (Five A's)** across departments.
3. Add attribution, lead scoring, CRO; install **NPS/CSAT/refund** metrics.
4. Promote deliberately (beat the **Peter Principle**); spend to your **CAT**.
