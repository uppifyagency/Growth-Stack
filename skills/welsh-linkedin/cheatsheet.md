# The LinkedIn Playbook — Cheatsheet

## The Sequence (do in order)
`Niche → Character → Profile (Sales Page) → Tribe → Content → Algorithm/Consistency`
Serve a group → look the part → feed them → convert to an improved career.

## The Four Niche Types
| Niche | Target | Best for | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Downward** | 1–2 levels below | *start here* — easy content + influence | low buyer income |
| **Lateral** | peers | compounding peer respect | must prove success; crowded |
| **Dual** | below + peers | wide selling net | huge content load |
| **Upward** | above (founders/C-suite) | biggest payoff | needs rare skill, zero fluff |

## Character = 3 Parts
**Backstory** (true, elevator-pitch) · **Personality Traits** (2–4, consistent voice) · **Focused Topics** (3–5, niche-aligned).
Voice-fit test: *does this post meet ≥1 of my traits?*

## 8 Ways to Add Connections
Colleagues · Career Advice · "More suggestions" · Groups · **Profile-viewers (add <30 min)** · Similar profiles · Advanced Search · **2X Simple Ask** (tag exactly 2).

## Industry-Titan Sequence (~20K+ followers)
1. List 5–10 → 2. **Stay out of inbox** → 3. **10+** thoughtful comments (2–3 wks) → 4. **Solo Recognition Post** → 5. **Custom** connection request.

## 5 Audience-Boosting Interactions
1. Use **Recent** sort (be first comment) · 2. **5 thoughtful comments/day** · 3. **End with a question** · 4. **Tag** someone · 5. **Like + reply** to every interaction on your posts.

## 5 Idea Sources
Experience (notes app) · **Pocket** (save) · **Feedly** (discover) · HubSpot Blog Idea Generator · **Twitter + screenshot** (#1).

## Story = Hook + Format + Feel
- **5 viral traits**: Inspirational · Believable · Authentic · Relatable · Polarizing.
- **Hook line**: line **5** (text post) / ~line **3** (image/video) — 3 short lines + blank lines between.
- **Format**: White Space + Wave (blank line each line; sentence lengths rise/fall).
- **Feel**: polarizing (~60–70% agree / 30–40% disagree) **or** authentic overcoming-adversity.

## Content Format Menu (rotate on a calendar)
Story · Checklist + action steps · Clickbait + strong CTA · Recognition listicle · Topical/newsworthy · Vulnerability/mistakes · Free resource.

## 10 Algorithm Tips
1. Professional not boring · 2. Be diverse · 3. **Don't link out** (use 1st comment / About) · 4. Think mobile · 5. **≤3 relevant hashtags** · 6. Stay on-niche (human editors) · 7. Be consistent (30-day run) · 8. **End with a CTA** · 9. Be human · 10. **Be relevant + interact**.

## Weekly Batch-and-Schedule
Capture all week → **Sat 8–10am, 2 hrs → 3–5 posts** → **Buffer/Hootsuite** → schedule best time (Welsh ~6–6:15am PT; Tue/Wed strong) → monitor.
**120 minutes/week = the price of consistency.**
