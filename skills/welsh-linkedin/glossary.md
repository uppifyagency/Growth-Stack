# Glossary — The LinkedIn Playbook (Justin Welsh)

**2X Simple Ask** — a post tagging exactly two popular people asking "who else should I follow?"; their interaction lifts the post and warms future connections (Ch 4).
**About section (link hack)** — the profile section used to host links the post body can't (algorithm punishes outbound links); direct readers "to the first link in my profile" (Ch 3, 6).
**Audience-Boosting Interactions** — the 5 commenting tactics that convert others' audiences into your profile views/connections (Ch 4).
**Authentic** — viral-story trait: vulnerable/real, not made up (Ch 5).
**Backstory** — character component: your true, written origin story, used like an elevator pitch (Ch 2).
**Believable** — viral-story trait: reads as something that really happened (Ch 5).
**Buffer** — automated publishing tool Welsh uses to schedule batched posts; free plan (Ch 6).
**Career Advice section** — profile area where LinkedIn curates people you can help; a connection source (Ch 4).
**Character** — your true, consistent on-platform persona = backstory + personality traits + focused topics (Ch 2).
**Checklists with action steps** — a non-story content format; high-consumption (Ch 5).
**Clickbait + strong CTA** — content format: an enticing first line paired with a call to action (Ch 5).
**Consistency** — posting Mon–Fri without gaps; the biggest separator between large and small accounts (Ch 6).
**Content calendar** — assigning content formats to weekdays (Mon checklist, Tue story, etc.) and testing (Ch 5).
**CTA (Call to Action)** — an explicit ask at the end of a post (weigh in, give your top-3) (Ch 5, 6).
**Downward Niche** — targeting people one–two steps below you; easiest start, easiest influence (Ch 2).
**Dual Niche** — downward + lateral combined; wide net but high content load (Ch 2).
**Feedly** — RSS reader aggregating new blogs/podcasts for *discovering* fresh ideas (Ch 5).
**Feel** — third part of a story: either polarizing or an authentic overcoming-adversity story (Ch 5).
**Focused Topics** — character component: 3–5 niche-aligned topics you post about (Ch 2).
**Format (story)** — second part of a story: the White Space + Wave layout (Ch 5).
**Free resources** — content format: offer a free e-book/asset (often linked in first comment) (Ch 5).
**Gary Vaynerchuk content model** — spin one long-form piece into many micro-posts (Ch 5).
**Golden Rule #1 (titans)** — stay out of an industry titan's inbox until you've earned the relationship (Ch 4).
**Hook** — first part of a story: the last visible line before "see more"; must compel the click (Ch 5).
**HubSpot Blog Idea Generator** — keyword→title/topic tool for long-form ideas (Ch 5).
**Hootsuite** — automated publishing alternative to Buffer; nicer free plan (Ch 6).
**Hybrid algorithm** — LinkedIn ranking = machine + human editors; write for both (Ch 6).
**Industry Titan** — a creator with ~20K+ followers in your niche; needs an earned-recognition approach (Ch 4).
**Inspirational** — viral-story trait: motivational content performs well (Ch 5).
**Lateral Niche** — targeting peers at your level; peer respect, but must prove success; crowded (Ch 2).
**Link in first comment** — algorithm hack: put outbound links in a comment, not the post body (Ch 6).
**Niche** — the specific group you serve; the narrower, the stronger the followers (Ch 1, 2).
**Personality Traits** — character component: 2–4 consistent traits that are your voice (Ch 2).
**Pocket** — Chrome extension to save articles for later reaction/reframe (Ch 5).
**Polarizing** — viral-story trait between vanilla and crazy; targets ~60–70% agree / 30–40% disagree; tribe-forming (Ch 5).
**Profile (= Sales Page)** — your most important asset; conversion copy that turns viewers into followers (Ch 3).
**Recent sort** — feed sort (vs. Top) used to catch titans early and become the first comment (Ch 4).
**Recognition-based listicle** — content format: a recurring ranked list that tagged winners reshare (Ch 5).
**Relatable** — viral-story trait: resonates so readers finish and engage (Ch 5).
**Sales Page** — Welsh's frame for the profile: you selling yourself (Ch 3).
**Solo Recognition Post** — publicly crediting one titan's post and how it helped you; the warm-up before a connection request (Ch 4).
**The Four Niche Types** — Downward, Lateral, Dual, Upward (Ch 2).
**The Four Objectives** — define the group, build the appearance, create content, convert to career (Ch 1).
**The 8 Ways (connections)** — colleagues, career advice, suggestions, groups, profile-viewers, similar profiles, advanced search, 2X Simple Ask (Ch 4).
**The 10 Algorithm Tips** — the posting-and-algorithm checklist (Ch 6).
**Thought leader** — the status that converts attention into career outcomes; the course's destination (Ch 1).
**Topical / newsworthy content** — content format: react to current events the day they happen (Ch 5).
**Twitter + Screenshot** — Welsh's #1 idea source: niche-curated feed, screenshot + annotate + save (Ch 5).
**Upward Niche** — targeting people above you (founders, C-suite); biggest payoff, hardest (Ch 2).
**Vulnerability & mistakes** — content format: sharing failures/real days; highly relatable (Ch 5).
**Voice-fit test** — pre-publish check: "does this meet at least one of my personality traits?" (Ch 2).
**Weekly Batch-and-Schedule** — capture all week → 2-hour Saturday block → 3–5 posts → schedule (Ch 6).
**White Space + Wave** — story format: blank line between every line; sentence lengths rising/falling like a wave (Ch 5).
