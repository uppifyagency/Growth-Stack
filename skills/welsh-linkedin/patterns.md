# Patterns & Techniques — The LinkedIn Playbook

## Pick & Narrow Your Niche
**When to use**: at the very start, before profile or posting.
**How**: choose one of the four niche types — start with the **Downward Niche** (easiest content, easiest influence). Then narrow hard: not "sales" but "sales managers targeting salespeople in financial services." Depth = follower strength.
**Trade-offs**: Downward earns less if you sell to lower levels; Upward pays most but demands a hard-to-replicate skill and no-fluff content.

## Establish Your Character
**When to use**: right after picking the niche.
**How**: write a true **backstory** (elevator-pitch length), define **2–4 personality traits** that mirror real life, and pick **3–5 focused topics** aligned to the niche. Gate every future post with the **voice-fit test**.
**Trade-offs**: must be authentic — fabricated personas break under daily output.

## The Profile as Sales Page
**When to use**: before any tribe-building or posting.
**How**: read the profile module and *apply each step as you go*; render backstory/traits/topics into headline, banner, About, Featured so a stranger instantly gets who you serve and wants to follow. Host links in the About section.
**Trade-offs**: it's the most time-intensive module — but it's the destination for all later traffic, so front-load it.

## The 8-Way Connection Sweep
**When to use**: ongoing tribe-building.
**How**: work colleagues, Career Advice, "More suggestions," Groups, **profile-viewers (add within 30 min)**, similar profiles, Advanced Search (a peer's 2nd-degree by your cities), and the **2X Simple Ask**.
**Trade-offs**: proactive and manual; pairs with inbound from good content.

## The Industry-Titan Connection Sequence
**When to use**: to connect with ~20K+ follower creators who ignore their inbox.
**How**: list 5–10 titans → **stay out of their inbox** → meaningfully comment on **≥10** of their posts over 2–3 weeks → publish a **Solo Recognition Post** crediting one → after they react, send a **custom** connection request.
**Trade-offs**: slow (weeks per titan), but the highest-probability path; flattery beats the cold ask.

## Borrow Reach via Audience-Boosting Interactions
**When to use**: daily, to grow profile views/connections.
**How**: switch feed to **Recent** to be the *first* comment on a titan's post; leave **5 thoughtful comments/day**; **end each with a question**; **tag** a relevant person; **like + reply** to every interaction on your own posts.
**Trade-offs**: thoughtful comments take time; generic ones are wasted effort.

## The 5-Source Idea Capture System
**When to use**: continuously, so creation day is selection not invention.
**How**: log work lessons in a phone **notes** folder; **Pocket** for articles you find; **Feedly** to discover new blogs; **HubSpot Blog Idea Generator** for long-form seeds; **Twitter + screenshot** (curated niche feed) saved to a content folder.
**Trade-offs**: requires browser/phone setup and a daily habit; pays off as a permanent backlog.

## Build a Viral-Ready Story (Hook → Format → Feel)
**When to use**: every story post.
**How**: make it **Inspirational, Believable, Authentic, Relatable, Polarizing**. **Hook**: 3 short lines + blank lines so the hook lands on line 5 (text) or ~line 3 (image/video). **Format**: White Space + Wave (blank line between lines; sentence lengths rise/fall). **Feel**: polarizing *or* an authentic overcoming-adversity story.
**Trade-offs**: storytelling is hard early — write daily to improve; save your best personal story until your craft can do it justice.

## Rotate the Content-Format Menu
**When to use**: to keep the feed fresh beyond stories.
**How**: cycle checklists with action steps, clickbait + strong CTA, recognition-based listicles, topical/newsworthy reactions, vulnerability/mistakes, and free resources — assigned to weekdays on a content calendar; test what wins.
**Trade-offs**: more formats to master; prevents staleness.

## Beat the Hybrid Algorithm
**When to use**: every post.
**How**: be interesting (not vanilla); be diverse (text/video/image/checklist/Live); **never link out** (use first comment or About hack); think mobile; **≤3 relevant hashtags**; stay on-niche for the human editors; **end with a CTA**; be human; and when unsure, **be relevant + interact**.
**Trade-offs**: the link rule constrains traffic routing; the workaround is the profile/comment hack.

## Weekly Batch-and-Schedule
**When to use**: to sustain daily posting around a full-time job.
**How**: capture all week → carve a **2-hour Saturday block (8–10am)** → produce **3–5 posts** → load into **Buffer/Hootsuite** → schedule auto-publish at your tested best time (Welsh: ~6–6:15am PT; Tue/Wed strong). Monitor and adjust.
**Trade-offs**: separates capture/create/publish so no single day is overloaded; requires committing to a 30-day run.
