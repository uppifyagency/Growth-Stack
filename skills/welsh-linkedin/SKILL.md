## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

**The thesis.** LinkedIn is a career-conversion machine, not a résumé. Serve one specific group consistently until you become their **thought leader**, then convert that following into an improved professional career. The whole playbook runs in sequence: **Niche → Character → Profile → Tribe → Content → Algorithm/Consistency**.

**The Four Objectives** (the funnel the course optimizes): (1) define the group you'll serve; (2) build a robust, professional appearance attractive to that group; (3) create relevant content that brings followers from that group; (4) **turn the following into an improved career** (promotion, job, stage, podcasts, consulting) — "the most important."

**The Four Niche Types** — pick by goal; *start with the Downward Niche*:
- **Downward** (1–2 levels below) — easiest content + influence; con: lower buyer income.
- **Lateral** (peers) — compounding respect; con: must prove success, crowded.
- **Dual** (below + peers) — wide net; con: huge content load.
- **Upward** (founders/C-suite) — biggest payoff; con: needs a hard-to-replicate skill and zero fluff.
Rule: **the narrower the niche, the stronger the followers.**

**Character = 3 parts**: a true written **Backstory** (elevator-pitch), 2–4 consistent **Personality Traits** (your voice), 3–5 niche-aligned **Focused Topics**. Gate every post with the **voice-fit test**: *does this meet ≥1 of my traits?*

**Profile = Sales Page.** Your most important asset — conversion copy, not biography. Build it *before* posting because all tribe/content traffic lands here. Host links in the **About section** (the post body can't link out).

**Building the Tribe.** The **8 Ways to Add Connections** (colleagues, Career Advice, suggestions, groups, **profile-viewers added within 30 min**, similar profiles, Advanced Search, the **2X Simple Ask** = tag exactly two). The **Industry-Titan Sequence** (≥20K followers): list 5–10 → **stay out of their inbox** → 10+ thoughtful comments over 2–3 weeks → a **Solo Recognition Post** → a custom connection request ("flattery is the fastest way to connection"). The **5 Audience-Boosting Interactions**: use the **Recent** sort to be first comment, 5 thoughtful comments/day, **end with a question**, **tag** someone, like + reply to every interaction.

**Creating Content.** The **5 Idea Sources**: experience (notes app), **Pocket** (save), **Feedly** (discover), HubSpot Blog Idea Generator, **Twitter + screenshot** (#1). The **5 Viral-Story Traits**: Inspirational, Believable, Authentic, Relatable, **Polarizing** (between vanilla and crazy; ~60–70% agree / 30–40% disagree). A story has **3 parts — Hook, Format, Feel**: the **Hook** is the last line before "see more" (lands on line **5** for text, ~line **3** for image/video); the **Format** is **White Space + Wave** (blank line between every line; sentence lengths rise and fall); the **Feel** is polarizing *or* an authentic overcoming-adversity story. Rotate the non-story **format menu**: checklists, clickbait + strong CTA, recognition listicles, topical/newsworthy, vulnerability/mistakes, free resources.

**The Algorithm is hybrid** (machine + human editors) — write for both. The **10 Algorithm Tips**: professional not boring · be diverse · **don't link out** · think mobile · **≤3 relevant hashtags** · stay on-niche (human editors) · be consistent · **end with a CTA** · be human · **be relevant + interact** (the master lever).

**Consistency is the differentiator.** The **Weekly Batch-and-Schedule**: capture ideas all week → a **2-hour Saturday block** → 3–5 posts → schedule via **Buffer/Hootsuite** → test your own best time. Commit to a **30-day Mon–Fri run**. "120 minutes/week is the best investment you can make."

## Scope & Limits

This skill covers the course video transcripts only (5 modules / 12 lessons). The profile module ("Your LinkedIn Profile") was text/PDF-based in the original course, so its element-by-element instructions (exact headline/banner/About templates) are synthesized from the transcript's framing rather than reproduced verbatim — apply the Sales Page principle and check current LinkedIn UI. Named tools (Pocket, Feedly, Buffer, Hootsuite, HubSpot Blog Idea Generator) and platform mechanics (line counts, hashtag limits, the "career advice" dashboard, feed sorts) reflect ~2019–2020 LinkedIn and change over time — verify current behavior before relying on a specific tactic. For real results, pair this with hands-on posting and your own time/day testing.
