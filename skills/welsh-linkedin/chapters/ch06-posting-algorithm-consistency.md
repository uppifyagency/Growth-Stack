# Chapter 6: Posting, the Algorithm & Consistency

## Core Idea
Getting seen is part craft, part discipline. The LinkedIn algorithm is a *hybrid* — machine ranking plus human editors — so write for both. Then make it *systematic*: batch a week of content in one Saturday session and schedule it, because consistency is the single biggest separator between 30K–50K-follower accounts and 1K-follower accounts.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **The 10 Algorithm Tips**:
  1. **Be professional, but not boring** — vanilla content never goes viral; human editors reward interesting/humanizing/humorous.
  2. **Be diverse** — text, native video, images, graphics, checklists, LinkedIn Live, tips, opinions.
  3. **Don't link out** — outbound links (sites, YouTube, other socials) get crushed. Hacks: **link in first comment**, or host links in your **About section** and point readers "to the first link in my profile."
  4. **Think mobile** — high-quality images, lots of white space; long paragraphs and dull photos get scrolled past.
  5. **Use hashtags, never more than three** — >3 cut engagement; irrelevant high-volume hashtags get caught and pushed down by human editors. Use 1–3 *relevant* ones.
  6. **Play nice with the human element** — editors check that your posts are relevant to *your* industry (why niche + character matter); topic-hopping reads as noise.
  7. **Be good and consistent** — consistent schedules get rewarded most; post Mon–Fri, and run a **30-consecutive-day** test to watch engagement soar.
  8. **End posts with a question (strong CTA)** — ask for opinions/their top-3; as engagement grows you can drop CTAs and people interact naturally.
  9. **Be a human being** — candid, vulnerable, personal, relatable stories over mundane facts.
  10. **When in doubt: be relevant + interact** — the two highest-leverage moves; post often *and* engage with relevant people's content (comment, tag, drive them to yours).
- **The Weekly Batch-and-Schedule System** (how Welsh stayed consistent 18 months while holding a full-time SVP role):
  - Capture ideas all week via the Ch 5 five sources (Pocket, Feedly, Twitter screenshots, notes, HubSpot generator).
  - **Saturday 8–10am: a 2-hour batch block** → produce 3–5 high-quality posts.
  - Load into an **automated publishing tool — Buffer** (Welsh's pick) **or Hootsuite** (nicer free plan); both have free plans, no affiliate.
  - Schedule auto-publish at your best time (Welsh: ~6:00–6:15am Pacific; common study advice is 7:45am Eastern; Tue/Wed near-always best). **Test and monitor** days/times yourself.

## Key Concepts
- **Hybrid algorithm** — because humans review content, "interesting" and "relevant to your stated niche" are ranking factors, not just engagement math.
- **Outbound links are the cardinal sin** — keep readers on-platform; route link traffic through the first comment or the profile/About hack (ties to Ch 3).
- **Consistency compounds** — the benefits (revenue, better jobs, promotions, thought-leader status, speaking, podcasts, guest blogs) accrue only to those who keep showing up.
- **Batching beats daily writing** — separate idea-capture (all week, passive) from creation (one focused block) from publishing (automated), so a full-time job is no excuse.

## Mental Models
- **Write for two audiences at once** — the ranking machine *and* the human editor; satisfy both.
- **Relevance + interaction is the master lever** — when unsure what to do, do more of these two.
- **120 minutes is the best investment** — a single weekly 2-hour block is the whole price of consistency.

## Anti-patterns
- **Linking out in the post body** — guarantees suppression.
- **More than 3 hashtags, or irrelevant trophy hashtags** — drops engagement and trips the human editors.
- **Boring/vanilla, fact-regurgitating posts** — never reach virality.
- **Topic-hopping across industries** — human editors can't place you and demote you.
- **Writing post-by-post daily** — unsustainable; leads to gaps. Batch instead.
- **Quitting before the 30-day run** — consistency is the hard part and the differentiator.

## Key Takeaways
1. Write for the hybrid algorithm: interesting + diverse + relevant + human, never linking out, ≤3 relevant hashtags, ending with a CTA.
2. The two highest-leverage moves are *be relevant* and *interact*.
3. Capture all week, batch 3–5 posts in a 2-hour Saturday block, schedule via Buffer/Hootsuite, and test your own best times.
4. Commit to a 30-day Mon–Fri run; consistency is what separates large accounts from small ones.
