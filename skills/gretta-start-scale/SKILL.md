## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

**The thesis.** Build a *brand*, not a store, the cheap/low-risk way — and **build the market before the product**. "The world doesn't need more products, it needs better solutions to existing problems." A brand only exists in someone's mind (the gut feeling people have about you); it's the asset you can sell. AI hasn't changed how e-commerce works — it just speeds up the work.

**Idea (problem-first).** Ask "what problem can I solve?" not "what product?" Use **P.S.S.P.** — Problem → Stat → Solution → Product. You don't need to reinvent the wheel: find a **UVP** by changing **one of four dimensions** — **Design, Function, Price, Time** (The Fifth = "exclusive by time"). Screen ideas on market / demand / **shippability** (free shipping is the gate). Seek **painkillers, not vitamins**.

**Customer & validation.** Build 2–3 **Customer Personas** (demographics + psychographics, written as narratives). Plan it on a **Lean Canvas**. **Validate before spending** — 20 scripted interviews; the truest validation is a sale. "Everyone is my market" is a trap.

**Brand (MVB).** Ship a **Minimum Viable Brand** via Love & Money's **Brand Pyramid** (audience → benefits → values/RTBs → aphorism → vision → personality). Own one word in the mind (positioning). Prefer **associative/suggestive names**; generate 3–5. Default to **Shopify** on a free theme.

**Develop (Start Local, Scale Global).** Prototype locally, then scale to a global manufacturer on **Alibaba** post-validation. Shortlist 5 on Verified + Trade Assurance + ISO/BSCI + 5yrs/revenue + relevance + transparency; search **Manufacturers, not Products**. Open with a **leverage-building message** + spec sheet. Packaging is the first physical touchpoint.

**Profitable.** Keep **gross margin 30–60%** ("your profit margin is your mistakes margin"); sell at **≥2.5× cost**. Order at **MOQ**, inspect (2–5% defects normal), trademark only your name (skip patents), default to *not* breaking the mould.

**Audience (Market-Product Fit).** Grow the audience first, reverse-engineer the product, then monetize: followers → email waitlist → customers → advocates. Run the **3 C's** (Content, Collaboration, Consistency). Pick **3–5 content territories**; weight toward **Top Performing Content**; mix **BGC/UGC/IGC**. Map content across the **funnel** (Awareness → Evaluation → Purchase). Convert with **post-purchase micro-moments**, **giveaways** (tag-to-win), and **marketing psychology** (Reciprocity, Social Proof, Scarcity — never faked). The **waitlist** is the #1 pre-launch asset (7-component landing page; ≥100 signups).

**Million Dollar Influencer Marketing Blueprint.** Reverse-engineer from a goal (**4 C's**: Conversions, Community, Content, Conversation). **Influence = Reach × Relevancy × Relationship.** An influencer ≠ their audience — do audience analysis. Mix **macro vs micro** by goal; find via clusters and platform search; personalize outreach + give creative freedom; **leverage assets** and re-collaborate; judge ROI as a compounding system over months.

**Launch & Ops.** Three phases (plan → pre-launch → launch) with a Monday date, a **pre-mortem**, and a "why buy now" offer. **Free shipping** baked in beats lower price + paid shipping; add PayPal + Afterpay/Zip. Know stock-on-hand / avg sales / lead time. Founder runs **customer support** until it hurts. **Reiterate** from real feedback. Scale via **SOPs** and the **Entrepreneurship Scorecard** ($1,000/$100/$5 per hour). "Just launch — no launch is perfect."
