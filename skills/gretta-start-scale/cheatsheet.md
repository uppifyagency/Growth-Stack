# Start & Scale 3.0 — Cheatsheet

## The Journey (course arc)
`Idea → Customer & Validation → Brand & Store → Develop/Manufacture → Profitable → Audience → Conversion/Waitlist → Influencers → Launch & Ops → Iterate`

## Ideation Frameworks
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Problem-First** | ask "what problem can I solve?", not "what product?" |
| **P.S.S.P.** | Problem → Stat → Solution → Product |
| **4 dimensions** | Design · Function · Price · Time → change one → UVP |
| **Choose-right (screen)** | Market? Demand/trending? Shippable? → cut to 3 |

## Numbers to Memorize
| Metric | Rule |
|---|---|
| Gross profit margin | **30–60%** (never <30%) |
| Markup | **≥ 2.5× cost** (retail) |
| Defect rate | **2–5%** is normal |
| Designer (render) | **$250–$500** on Upwork |
| 3PL minimum | ~300–500 units/mo (US/UK/AU); ~100 (Asia) |
| First order | at the **MOQ**, keep it small |
| Waitlist before launch | **≥ 100** subscribers |
| Help desk software | at ~**$10K/mo** revenue |

## Brand Pyramid (MVB, Love & Money)
Target Audience → Benefits (rational) → Values/RTBs (emotional) → Aphorism → Vision → Personality (1 core + 2 sub archetypes).

## Name Categories (pick associative/suggestive)
Literal (avoid) · Descriptive (clear but boxes you in) · **Associative/Suggestive** (best) · Coined/Abstract (hard recognition). Generate **3–5**; secure the domain.

## Alibaba Supplier 6 Criteria
Verified · Trade Assurance · ISO + BSCI · 5+ yrs & revenue · Relevance · Transparency. **Search "Manufacturers," not "Products."**

## Content
- **3 C's**: Content · Collaboration · Consistency. *"Content is king, context is queen, engagement is the kingdom."*
- **3 content types**: BGC (you own) · UGC (most genuine) · IGC (platform-native).
- **Territories**: 3–5 (2 universal: Products & Promotions, Celebrate Community).
- **Weighting**: post more of your Top Performing Content (~20%+ engagement).
- **Funnel**: Awareness (cold) → Evaluation (warm, social proof) → Purchase (hot, offer).

## Marketing Psychology (Cialdini → Gretta's 3)
**Reciprocity** (give first) · **Social Proof** (popularity / praise / experts / demand) · **Scarcity** (limited time/stock — never fake it).

## Waitlist Landing Page (7 parts)
Headline · Subhead · Hero (render in use) · Benefits copy · Social proof · Clickable CTA · Scarcity.

## Influencer Blueprint
- **Goal first (4 C's)**: Conversions · Community · Content · Conversation.
- **Influence = Reach × Relevancy × Relationship.**
- **Macro** (≥100K, $1–20K+/post, 1–3% eng, ROI trackable) vs **Micro** (<100K, <$3K, 5–10% eng, will gift).
- Find via: Suggestions-for-you · clusters · customer-follows · platform search (People/Hashtags/Places).
- Audience ≠ influencer — always do **audience analysis**.

## Launch & Conversion Levers
- Launch **Monday**; do a **pre-mortem**; answer **"why buy now?"** (newness > discount).
- **Free shipping** (baked in) > lower price + paid shipping (proven in split tests).
- Add **PayPal** + **Afterpay/Zip** (one only) to lift conversion.
- Faster delivery (1–5 days) drastically lifts conversion.

## Scaling People & Process
- **SOPs** for every recurring task → delegate.
- **Scorecard**: $1,000/hr (founder: SOPs, new channels/markets/products) · $100/hr (mid) · $5/hr (junior).
- **Hire when it hurts** (~6 figures). Founder does support first.

## Operating Maxims
- **Market before product** (Market-Product Fit).
- **Don't reinvent the wheel** — change one dimension / convergence.
- **Your profit margin is your mistakes margin.**
- **Sell the sizzle, not the sausage** (benefits > features).
- **Turn accidents into UVPs** (The Fifth).
- **Just launch — no launch is perfect.**
- **Version one > version none.**
