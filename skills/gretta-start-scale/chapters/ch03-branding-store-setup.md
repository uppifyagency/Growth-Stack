# Chapter 3: Branding & Store Setup

## Core Idea
Build a **brand, not a store**. A store needs products to be worth anything; a brand has value on its own — it's the asset you can one day sell (Native→P&G $100M, Movement→Movado $200M, Dollar Shave Club→Unilever $1B). "A brand only exists in someone's mind" — it's the gut feeling people develop about your business (Marty Neumeier, *The Brand Gap*). Customers are far less rational than you think; make them *feel* something, then give them a reason to believe. Then ship a **Minimum Viable Brand** — the fewest consistent assets needed to differentiate — and stand up a Shopify store without a developer.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Minimum Viable Brand (MVB)** — the fewest consistent assets and tools needed to define and differentiate your brand. "Short enough to be easily understood, sharp enough to clearly differentiate" (Charle, Love & Money). Most startups don't need a full brand strategy at the outset.
- **The Pyramid Scheme / Brand Pyramid** (Love & Money) — six elements that build the MVB: (1) **Target Audience** (from your personas), (2) **Benefits** (rational; the brand features, e.g. Dropbottle = functional/fashionable/sustainable), (3) **Values / Reasons To Believe (RTBs)** (emotional common ground — e.g. pragmatic, altruistic, transparent, conscious), (4) **Aphorism / Brand Value Proposition** (a pithy general truth, e.g. "every drop counts"), (5) **Vision** (what you aspire to beyond the bottom line), (6) **Brand Personality** (via **Millward Brown brand archetypes** — pick one core + two sub-archetypes; e.g. Dropbottle = Wise core + Dreamer + Friend).
- **Brand Positioning** — the distinct place you own in the customer's mind; aim for **top-of-mind, single-word ownership** (Volvo=safety, Mercedes=prestige, BMW=driving; SkinnyMe=detox/teatox).
- **Name-Storming Process** — (1) 5-minute **word dump** (≥15 words) drawn from PSSP, Lean Canvas, brand pyramid; (2) take ~5–10 favorites down a thesaurus.com rabbit hole; (3) come up with **3–5 candidate names** (your first choices are often taken). Four name categories: **literal/generic** (avoid unless you own the domain+trademark), **descriptive** (SkinnyMe Tea — clear but boxes you in), **associative/suggestive** (Gretta's favorite — The Fifth, Dropbottle, Apple, Nike), **coined/abstract** (Kodak, Google — blank slate but hard to build recognition). Good names: memorable, distinct, pique interest, make sense, open to change. Bonus: works as adjective+noun (Uber, Frank Body's "Let's Be Frank") or has a double meaning (Taylor = tailored swim shorts).
- **Brand Look & Feel / Brand Kit** — colourways, typography (headers/subtext), logo. Choose colors *strategically* tied to brand values. A logo can just be the brand name in a stylized font — no icon required. Give designers real-life mockups (packaging, thank-you cards, billboards) and mood boards (often 2 directions). Distinguish **competitors** (industry research) from **Brand Spo** (brands you aspire to resemble).

## Key Concepts
- **Brand features vs product features** — articulate *why* you made the product (the rational benefits) before the emotional values.
- **"In the factory we make cosmetics; in the store we sell hope"** (Charles Revson) — emotion sells.
- **AI branding tools** (examples, change constantly): Looka (logos), Khroma/Huemint (palettes), Photor/DALL·E/Midjourney (mockups). For image generators, give *maximum* detail; **Concept Art / 3D modes give AI more creative permission** than Photography when ideating early concepts. AI gets a freelancer "ahead of the game," it doesn't replace creativity.
- **Store platforms** — the **big three**: **Shopify** (Gretta's default, 1M+ stores, easiest, most apps/themes, best for beginners; $29–$299/mo), **BigCommerce** (no transaction fee; good if migrating from a physical store), **Magento** (open-source, enterprise/high-volume, needs a developer — not for beginners).
- **Shopify setup essentials** — set currency/units; enable **Shopify Payments (Stripe) + PayPal** (PayPal reliably lifts conversion); pick a **free theme** (don't overspend); add core pages (shipping, returns/refunds, terms, privacy — Shopify pre-supplies legal copy, FAQ, contact); connect sales channels (Instagram tagging, Facebook Shop, buy buttons, POS). **The store name/URL cannot be changed** after creation. Use plugins/apps for "bells and whistles" — e.g. **Loox** for photo reviews collected on autopilot (social proof + reusable marketing assets).

## Mental Models
- **Renting vs buying a house** — running a store selling others' products (dropshipping) = renting; building a brand = owning an appreciating asset.
- **People won't remember what you said or did — they'll remember how you made them feel.**
- **We believe in reasons because we've already made the decision** (Kahneman, *Thinking Fast and Slow*) — RTBs come after the emotional pull.

## Anti-patterns
- Confusing "brand" with "branding" (logo/name/colors) — the brand is the perception in the mind.
- Building a full, complex brand strategy too early (overcomplicates; an MVB suffices).
- Fixating on the "perfect" name and letting it stall progress; getting attached before checking availability.
- Over-engineering a logo (paying for a custom icon) when stylized type works.
- Buying an expensive theme or hiring a developer for a first store.

## Key Takeaways
1. Build a brand (an asset), not just a store; win on emotion + reasons to believe.
2. Use the Love & Money Brand Pyramid to ship a Minimum Viable Brand and own one word in the mind.
3. Prefer associative/suggestive names; generate 3–5 candidates, validate availability.
4. Default to Shopify on a free theme with Shopify Payments + PayPal; you don't need a developer.
