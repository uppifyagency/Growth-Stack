# Glossary — Start & Scale 3.0 (Gretta van Riel)

**2.5× Markup** — minimum rule: sell at ≥2.5× per-unit cost; protects margin and leaves room for a 50% wholesale cut (Ch 5).
**3 C's of Community** — Content, Collaboration, Consistency — the foundation of social growth on any platform (Ch 6).
**3 R's Formula** — Influence = Reach × Relevancy × Relationship (Ch 8).
**4 C's (campaign goals)** — Conversions, Community, Content, Conversation — set the goal, then the campaign type (Ch 8).
**Afterpay / Zip** — buy-now-pay-later (AU/NZ); raises conversion; use only one (Ch 9).
**Alibaba** — world's largest B2B marketplace; where you find a global manufacturer post-validation (Ch 4).
**AOV (Average Order Value)** — total sales ÷ number of orders (Ch 5, 6).
**Aphorism** — the brand pyramid's pithy value proposition (e.g. "every drop counts") (Ch 3).
**BGC** — Brand-Generated Content (in-house/pro; you own it; mainly product photography) (Ch 6).
**Brand** — the gut feeling/perception in someone's mind, not the logo/name ("branding") (Ch 3).
**Brand Pyramid / Pyramid Scheme** — Love & Money's 6-part MVB tool: audience, benefits, values/RTBs, aphorism, vision, personality (Ch 3).
**Brand Spo** — brands you aspire to resemble (vs competitors for research) (Ch 3, 6).
**Breaking the Mould** — making a fully custom product vs modifying an existing design (Ch 5).
**BSCI** — Business Social Compliance Initiative; an Alibaba supplier certification (Ch 4).
**COGS** — Cost of Goods Sold; all production-related costs (Ch 5).
**Content Curation** — gathering and re-sharing existing content (vs creation) (Ch 6).
**Content Funnel** — Awareness → Evaluation → Purchase (+ post-purchase) (Ch 6).
**Content Territories / Pillars** — 3–5 topics your brand has authority/interest in (Ch 6).
**Content Weighting** — posting more of what works (Top Performing Content) (Ch 6).
**Convergence** — combining two existing ideas into one (Ch 10).
**CLV (Customer Lifetime Value)** — total value of a customer over time; retention raises it (Ch 7).
**Customer Persona Canvas** — semi-fictional ideal-customer profile (demographics + psychographics) (Ch 2).
**Dropshipping** — selling products you don't stock; supplier ships to customer, you keep the margin (Ch 10).
**ESOP** — Employee Share Options Program for vesting equity to staff (Ch 10).
**Entrepreneurship Scorecard** — bucket tasks by leverage ($1,000/$100/$5 per hour) (Ch 10).
**Evergreen categories** — stable, low-seasonality niches (pet, baby, home, comfort, car care, gardening) (Ch 1).
**FOMO** — fear of missing out; the emotional response scarcity triggers (Ch 7).
**Free Gift With Purchase** — reciprocity tactic shown at $0 (app: Ultimate Special Offers) (Ch 7).
**Free Shipping (baked-in)** — price includes shipping; higher price + free shipping beats lower price + paid shipping (Ch 9).
**GM structure** — promoting a trusted right-hand to General Manager with vesting equity (vs co-founding) (Ch 10).
**Gretta's E-Commerce Stack** — her evergreen, externally-maintained list of software/AI tools (Ch 9).
**Gross Profit Margin** — (Revenue − COGS) ÷ Revenue × 100; target 30–60% (Ch 5).
**Hey Influencers** — Gretta's influencer agency; uses an IRM database and a 3-month approach (Ch 8).
**IGC** — Influencer-Generated Content (higher quality, platform-native) (Ch 6).
**Influencer Cluster Campaign** — interconnected influencers (same show/group/niche) with overlapping audiences (Ch 8).
**IRM (Influencer Relationship Management)** — database of proven-to-convert influencers (Ch 8).
**KOL** — Key Opinion Leader (influencer term used with manufacturers) (Ch 4).
**Lean Canvas** — Ash Maurya's one-page idea-planning sheet (10 blocks) (Ch 2).
**Loop Giveaway** — multi-account/brand co-hosted giveaway for reach (Ch 7).
**Market-Product Fit** — build the market/audience first, then the product (Gretta's inversion of product-market fit) (Ch 6).
**Micro-influencers at scale** — 10–20+ micros, the go-to Conversions campaign (Ch 8).
**Micro-Moments** — 4 shareable customer-experience moments: after purchase, unboxing, in action, results (Ch 7).
**Millward Brown Brand Archetypes** — human-trait archetypes; pick 1 core + 2 sub (Ch 3).
**MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity)** — smallest run a manufacturer will produce; start here (Ch 4, 5).
**MVB (Minimum Viable Brand)** — fewest consistent assets needed to define/differentiate (Ch 3).
**MVP / Validation** — proving demand before spending (20-person interviews; waitlist) (Ch 2, 4).
**Painkiller vs Vitamin** — need-to-have vs nice-to-have product (seek painkillers) (Ch 1).
**Perceptual Set Theory** — why "next logical step" CTAs work (Ch 6).
**Pre-mortem** — brainstorming what could go wrong *before* launch (Ch 9).
**Private Label** — putting your logo on a ready-made product (fast to market, weak brand) (Ch 5).
**Problem-First Ideation** — start from a problem, not a product (Ch 1).
**Product-for-Content campaign** — influencer ships content to you (doesn't post) (Ch 8).
**Product Innovation Framework** — change one dimension (Design/Function/Price/Time) to find a UVP (Ch 1).
**Product Hybridization** — combining two products' functions (Ch 1).
**Product Seeding** — free product to influencers, no strings ("spray and pray") (Ch 8).
**P.S.S.P. Framework** — Problem → Stat → Solution → Product (Ch 1).
**Reasons To Believe (RTBs)** — emotionally charged brand values (Ch 3, 10).
**Reiterate & Optimize** — improving the product from real customer feedback/surveys (Ch 9).
**Render vs Prototype** — render = digital 3D visual; prototype = physical model (Ch 4).
**Reciprocity / Social Proof / Scarcity** — Gretta's 3 top Cialdini persuasion triggers (Ch 7).
**SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures)** — task/role checklists that enable delegation (Ch 10).
**Start Local, Scale Global** — prototype locally, scale to global manufacturing post-validation (Ch 4).
**Tag-to-Win Giveaway** — follow + tag a friend per comment; fastest social growth (Ch 7).
**TikTok Creative Center** — free product-research tool (top products by region, CPA, top ads) (Ch 2).
**Top Performing Content (TPC)** — posts with ~20%+ more engagement; weight toward these (Ch 6).
**Trade Assurance** — Alibaba refund protection if the order doesn't match (Ch 4).
**UVP (Unique Value Proposition)** — clear message of why you're different and worth buying (Ch 1).
**UGC** — User-Generated Content (customer-made; strongest social proof) (Ch 6).
**Vesting Equity** — equity earned over time (e.g. % per year) (Ch 10).
**Waitlist Landing Page** — pre-launch email-capture page (7 components); aim ≥100 signups (Ch 7).
**White Space** — underserved market segment / gap identification (Ch 1).
