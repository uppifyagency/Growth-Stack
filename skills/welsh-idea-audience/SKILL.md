## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

**The thesis & the Five-Step Plan.** The internet gives access to ~4.66B people; building online is *less* risky than one employer. The whole course is one plan: **build an audience → use it to stand up a service business → serve clients and learn → package those learnings into digital products → shift income from time-for-money to scalable products.** This maps to the four sections/pillars: **Idea → Audience → Proof → Product (IAPP).**

**Mindset first.** *Play the Long Game* (Welsh: ~11 months / 140 posts before traction — no overnight success). *Knowledge Blindness* — you don't need to be the expert, only "one chapter ahead"; the **market decides**, no permission needed. *Overcome fear* via the **Comfort → Fear → Learning → Growth** zones (growth requires crossing the painful fear zone). The **Six New Rules of Internet Money** are "______ yourself" prompts: **scale, diversify, invest, replicate, increase, compound.**

**Ideation — A Niche of One.** Reject product-led ideation (most can't build) and "solve your own problem" (needs distribution you don't have). Instead combine **skill × interest → a unique idea you alone own** → service business → product. Neither input is unique; the *combination* is, so competition ≈ zero and distribution is built in. Wrap it in a story: **The Background** (honest snapshot of struggles, inward + outward, before transformation) + **The Transformation** (the change/decision, told as a memorized 5-step talk track; be authentically polarizing, never vanilla).

**Audience — Play Moneyball.** Growing an audience *to sell* ≠ growing for dopamine. Get on base daily with singles/doubles; ignore virality. 5 steps: one platform → daily relevant content → a social ecosystem (adjacent + contrarian voices) → notifications (the **LinkedIn activity-page hack**) → tools (SparkToro) to find where they play. **Audience siphoning** = first thoughtful comment on bigger accounts pulls their audience to you.

**The Profile Funnel = profile as a sales page.** Scan order each must pass: **banner** (what/who in 2s) → **tagline** (who · what · how) → **headshot** (clean) → **about** (crisp + proof) → **featured** (website → email) → **pinned tweet** (popular + informative).

**Content — the Content Formula + Content Matrix.** Gut-check every post on **Outcome / Focus / Structure / Opinions** (be a *sniper*, not a *sprayer*). Generate volume with the matrix (5 layers): **Mantra → Taglines → Pillars → Core Concepts (strong opinions) → Structures**; combine a core concept × a structure to say one thing 15 ways. **The Scroll Test** (4 levels) gets people to stop: hook line 1 / crave line 2; anti-stock-photo images; short video; own a distinctive style. Polarization must be authentic.

**Capture & distribute.** **The Art of the Capture** — email is the only channel you own; a ≤3-second landing page: bold statement + secondary header + social proof + **one** CTA (less is more). **The Distribution Network / Content Web** — after mastering one platform, stitch many into a web that all funnels to the subscriber page, with format + CTA tailored per platform (long-form + ask / visual snippets + subtle CTA / direct link; LinkedIn via the Featured section).

**Proof — the MVP Service Business + F.I.T.I.** Stand up the **7 components** (site, service description, FAQs, inquiry form, booking, rate sheet, payments) with no-code (Carrd + Airtable + Stripe + Zapier); qualify/disqualify up front; automate anything routine above your aspirational rate (35-45 min/client → ~4 min). Climb the **Three Levels** (low-cost → industry-average → high-price micro-niche) with **F.I.T.I — Feedback, Iteration, Testimonials, Increase** (Welsh: $250 → $2,500/hr; doubling price while halving win-rate keeps revenue flat at half the time).

**Product — Move Fast & Cheap + the 14-day launch.** Narrow to one specific problem you know cold, **time-box to 2 weeks**, outline (sections → chapters, 1-2 outcomes), build with the **Four-Pass Process** (brain-dump → notes + bullet summaries → media → resources), get honest feedback throughout. Host on **Gumroad** (pre-order, versions, workflow email triggers). Launch via the **14-Day Pre-Sell**: Day 1 set up + announce (discount); Days 2-9 build during the window + day-8 teaser; Days 10-13 review, screencasts, write-up, testimonials, buyer-flow check; Day 14 **blitz**; post-launch collect testimonials + raise price. Mantra: **low production, high access, high affordability.**
