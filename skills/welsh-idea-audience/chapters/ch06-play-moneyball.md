# Chapter 6: Play Moneyball

## Core Idea
Growing an audience *to make income* is different from growing an audience for dopamine, career, or thought leadership. If your goal is to sell, play **Moneyball**: ignore virality, "get on base" every single day with singles and doubles, do the little things right consistently — and it compounds. All good ideas need distribution; this is where the Audience section begins.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Play Moneyball** — modeled on the Oakland A's: a roster with no superstars and little money that won by getting on base, not swinging for home runs. Translate to content: a systematized daily process that reliably hits singles/doubles with your *intended* audience. Home runs happen, but with your audience, not random viral crowds.
- **The 5-Step Get-On-Base Strategy**:
  1. **Focus on one platform** (LinkedIn, Twitter, YouTube, IG, FB groups) — master it before layering a second.
  2. **Create rapid relevant content every day** — aimed specifically at your audience (Welsh: 1×/day LinkedIn, 2×/day Twitter).
  3. **Build a social ecosystem** — ask two questions: *Who has good influence on my target audience but serves a slightly different solution?* and *Who reaches my same audience from a very different perspective?*
  4. **Set up notifications** — Twitter/IG/YouTube have native alerts; for LinkedIn, the **LinkedIn ecosystem hack** (below).
  5. **Use tools to find where your audience plays** — e.g., SparkToro for podcasts, YouTube channels, newsletters they consume (covered later); eventually share content there.
- **The LinkedIn Ecosystem Hack** — bookmark people's *activity* pages (not profile pages), note the exact time each posts, and be the first thoughtful commenter. (Welsh: Austin Belsack posts 8:10 CT; Welsh posts 8:15 ET / 5:15 PT.)
- **Audience Siphoning** — craft a thoughtful early reply on a larger account's post so their audience clicks your profile, researches you, and follows. You siphon attention *toward* you (not away from them).

## Key Concepts
- **Participation + creation accelerates growth** — you can grow sizeably through participation alone, but combining it with creation compounds far faster.
- **Reasons to grow ≠ income reasons** — dopamine, career advancement, and thought leadership are all valid, but require a *different* strategy than selling.

## Mental Models
- **Singles compound, home runs don't bank** — daily consistency beats chasing one viral hit (Welsh got 1.25M views once — an outlier that happened twice).
- **Borrow bigger audiences** — engage upward on larger accounts to generate impressions and follows.

## Anti-patterns
- **Chasing virality / engagement-bait** (sweet viral reposts, reaction polls) — gets dopamine-driven clicks that never convert to business.
- **Spreading across many platforms at the start** — master one first.
- **Following accounts but never engaging early/thoughtfully** — siphoning requires being first with value.

## Key Takeaways
1. Pick one platform; aim to be great there before expanding.
2. Build a social ecosystem of adjacent and contrarian voices; set notifications.
3. Use the activity-page bookmark hack to be the first thoughtful commenter and siphon audience.
4. Get on base daily — ignore virality.
