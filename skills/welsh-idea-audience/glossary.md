# Glossary — Idea to Audience (Justin Welsh)

**4.66B New Prospects** - the internet's ~4.66 billion connected people; your customer base (Ch 1).
**A Niche of One** - signature framework: one skill × one interest = a unique idea you alone own; foundation of IAPP (Ch 2, 3).
**About section** - profile block expanding on what you do, with social proof; step 4 of the Profile Funnel (Ch 7).
**Airtable** - no-code CRM for pipeline, clients, inquiry forms, automation triggers (Ch 11).
**Aspirational rate** - a target hourly value (e.g. $100/hr) for deciding what to automate or delegate (Ch 11).
**Audience siphoning** - drawing another account's audience to you via early, thoughtful comments (Ch 6).
**Banner image** - top profile graphic answering "what/who do you help?" in <2s; step 1 of the Profile Funnel (Ch 7).
**Behind the Scenes** - live walkthrough of the automated service business (Ch 11).
**Build in Public** - launch option: share everything (process, metrics, revenue); powerful but time-consuming (Ch 13).
**Calendly** - scheduling tool for discovery-call booking (Ch 11).
**Canva** - design tool for cover art and graphics; has templates (Ch 12).
**Carrd (carrd.co)** - cheap no-code website builder (~$18-37/yr) for landing pages and the service site (Ch 11).
**Comfort/Fear/Learning/Growth Zones** - growth requires crossing the painful fear zone (Ch 1).
**Content Formula** - per-post gut check on Outcome, Focus, Structure, Opinions (each bad→average→great) (Ch 8).
**Content Matrix** - content engine combining five layers (Mantra → Taglines → Pillars → Core Concepts → Structures) to say one thing many ways (Ch 8).
**Content Web** - a network of platforms all funneling to one email subscriber page (Ch 10).
**Core Concepts** - a strong/polarizing opinion assigned to each pillar (Ch 8).
**Discovery call** - 30-min recorded call, 6-8 pre-written questions on client challenges; notes in CRM (Ch 11).
**Distribution** - getting your product/idea in front of people via selling, audience, marketing, ads (Ch 2).
**Featured section** - LinkedIn block holding resources and the email-capture link; step 5 of the Profile Funnel (Ch 7).
**Feedback (F.I.T.I)** - constantly asking customers their wants and tracking what you enjoy delivering (Ch 12).
**Five-Step Plan** - Audience → Service business → Serve & learn → Productize → Shift to scalable products (Ch 1).
**F.I.T.I** - Feedback, Iteration, Testimonials, Increase — method for climbing the three service levels and raising rates (Ch 12).
**Four Goals** - ideate; grow an audience; stand up a service business; package learnings into products (Ch 1).
**Four-Pass Process** - product build: (1) brain-dump, (2) notes + summaries, (3) media, (4) resources (Ch 12).
**Get on base** - Moneyball principle: daily singles/doubles that compound, not home runs (Ch 1, 6).
**Gumroad** - cheap platform for selling digital products; supports pre-order/membership, versions, workflows (Ch 13).
**Hidden payments page** - a private (unlinked) URL with Stripe buttons, sent only to qualified buyers (Ch 11).
**Headshot** - clean professional profile photo; step 3 of the Profile Funnel (Ch 7).
**IAPP** - Idea Audience Proof Product: the course's full name and its four pillars/sections (Ch 1, 8).
**Increase (F.I.T.I)** - raising prices as demand rises; double price + half win-rate = same revenue, half the time (Ch 12).
**Inward vs. outward struggles** - internal states vs. visible events; share both in the backstory (Ch 4).
**Iteration (F.I.T.I)** - growing the overlap of what you enjoy × what customers want; cut the rest (Ch 12).
**Knowledge Blindness** - false belief you have nothing to teach; cured by being "one chapter ahead" (Ch 1).
**LinkedIn ecosystem hack** - bookmark activity pages (not profiles), note post times, comment first (Ch 6).
**Mantra** - your single highest-level rallying statement (Welsh: "the future belongs to those who build online") (Ch 8).
**Move Fast & Cheap** - build a minimum-viable product in ~2 weeks; low production, high access (Ch 12).
**New Rules of Internet Money** - six "_ yourself" rules: scale, diversify, invest, replicate, increase, compound (Ch 1).
**No-code tools** - Zapier, Airtable, Monday.com, Notion, etc.; build without code (Ch 2, 3, 11).
**Niche of One, Part 2** - extending a skill×interest idea into a service business (Ch 3).
**One chapter ahead** - the Catch Me If You Can lesson: know only slightly more than your students (Ch 1).
**Outcome / Focus / Structure / Opinions** - the Content Formula's four basics (Ch 8).
**Pattern interruption** - stopping the scroll via impactful openings and distinctive style (Ch 8).
**Pillars** - fundamental topics supporting the mantra (Welsh: Idea, Audience, Proof, Product) (Ch 8).
**Pinned tweet** - Twitter element that must be both popular and informative; step 6 of the Profile Funnel (Ch 7).
**Play Moneyball** - audience strategy of daily singles/doubles, ignoring virality (Ch 6).
**Play the Long Game** - 2-5 year mindset; ~11 months/140 posts before traction (Ch 1).
**Polarization (authentic)** - natural strong opinions that form a tribe; never mean or political (Ch 5, 8).
**Pre-sell** - announcing weeks ahead to build excitement; the recommended launch (Ch 13).
**Profile Funnel** - treating a social profile as a sales page with a six-step scan order (Ch 7).
**Rate sheet** - clear pricing document; one of the 7 service components (Ch 11).
**Scroll Test** - getting people to stop scrolling; four levels, consume-only to auto-consumed (Ch 8).
**SendGrid** - Gumroad's email engine, high deliverability (Ch 13).
**Service business** - trading time for money at an hourly rate (Ch 3, 11).
**Seven Components (service business)** - website, service description, FAQs, inquiry form, booking, rate sheet, payments (Ch 11).
**Shield** - LinkedIn analytics tool (Ch 11).
**Skills Document** - a ~100-skill worksheet to surface skills you underestimate (Ch 2, 3).
**Sniper vs. sprayer** - sniper = every post drives one outcome; sprayer = scattered (Ch 8).
**SparkToro** - audience-research tool to find where your audience hangs out (podcasts, channels, etc.) (Ch 6).
**Stripe** - payment processor; subscriptions auto-charge every 30 days (Ch 11).
**Surprise launch** - the weak launch ("I have a product!"); not recommended (Ch 13).
**Tagline** - (1) profile line: who you help · what · how, step 2 of the Profile Funnel (Ch 7); (2) the 2nd Content Matrix layer — short branded statements under the mantra (Ch 8).
**Testimonials (F.I.T.I)** - social proof collected ruthlessly at every level; guide customers to a result (Ch 12, 13).
**Three Levels (service)** - L1 low-cost/learn, L2 industry-average, L3 high-price micro-niche (Ch 11, 12).
**Three Ways to Ideate** - product-led (worst), solve-own-problem (needs distribution), Niche of One (best) (Ch 2).
**Time-box** - a fixed build deadline (first product = 2 weeks) (Ch 12).
**Transformation** - the story half moving the audience from past struggle to present change (Ch 5).
**Versions (Gumroad)** - product tiers (e.g. course vs. course + coaching) (Ch 13).
**Workflows (Gumroad)** - email triggers from buyer actions (Ch 13).
**Zapier** - integration "glue" tying Stripe → Drive → email automations (Ch 11).
**14-Day Launch Plan** - day-by-day pre-sell schedule, build sprint, and launch-day blitz (Ch 13).
