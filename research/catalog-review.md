## 7. Data layer

Data-dependent work is wide: 156 of 264 units have `needs_data = yes` and 23 are `data` kind (measured from `units.tsv`; `needs_data` is my reading of each `data` field, so it is a judgment count).

**Recommendation: one shared data step, not owned units.** The 16 dataforseo units plus A-043 (visualization) become a data layer that domain playbooks call; P7 Raccolta prove is the mode that runs it and labels each number measured, documented or estimate.

Why, from the inventory:
- The dataforseo slice says it outright: "None is a finished marketing artifact on its own: each feeds a book deliverable named in its downstream" (C:1217).
- All 16 share one quality bar (the data-hygiene criteria, C:1012 to C:1018: balance check, explicit location/language, per-task status, bulk endpoints, cost reported). One shared bar is exactly what a shared step is.
- Their callers spread over 9 v1 playbooks (G1, G2, G4, G5, A1, A5, C4, C6, H4). Owning them would pad G2 and G4 with tool calls and repeat the hygiene rules in each.

How it attaches: each domain playbook's procedure names the data units it calls at a "pull data" step (G2 calls DF1, DF2, DF3; G4 calls DF5, DF6, DF10; G5 calls DF4, DF8; G1 calls DF7; A1 calls DF9, DF13; A5 calls DF12, DF16; C4 calls DF14; C6 calls DF15; H4 calls DF11). The gtm-internet-pipes `data` units (A-035 to A-042) **stay owned** by A1 and A5: they carry judgment (Sales > Search > Survey tiers, two-source rule, 3 x 8 matrix), not just pulls, and their two-source rule (A:690) should become the data layer's default bar. The alternative, owning each DF unit in its main caller (the mapping `units.tsv` records for v1), is workable but leaves G2 and G4 half tool-catalog.
