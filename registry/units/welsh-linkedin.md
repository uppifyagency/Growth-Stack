# welsh-linkedin

## D-001 1. Niche and character brief
- ask: "Help me pick my LinkedIn niche and write my backstory, voice traits and topics."
- inputs: the user's role and level, who sits below, beside and above them, what they sell or want (job, consulting, stage), and their true career story.
- data: none.
- upstream: none upstream
- downstream: feeds profile rewrite (2), story post (3), weekly batch (4).
- depth: full

### steps
1. State the goal the following must serve (promotion, job, consulting, stage). [chapters/ch01-course-introduction.md]
2. Pick one of the four niche types (Downward, Lateral, Dual, Upward) by goal. Default to Downward. [chapters/ch02-laying-the-foundation.md]
3. Narrow the niche past the comfortable point (for example "sales managers targeting salespeople in financial services", not "sales"). [chapters/ch02-laying-the-foundation.md]
4. Write a true backstory at elevator-pitch length. [chapters/ch02-laying-the-foundation.md]
5. Define 2-4 personality traits that mirror real life. [chapters/ch02-laying-the-foundation.md]
6. Pick 3-5 focused topics aligned to the niche. [chapters/ch02-laying-the-foundation.md]
7. Write the voice-fit test ("does this post meet ≥1 of my traits?") as the gate for future posts. [chapters/ch02-laying-the-foundation.md]
### criteria
- Exactly one niche type is named, with its stated con acknowledged. [chapters/ch02-laying-the-foundation.md]
- The niche names a specific role or level and an industry or segment, not a bare function. [chapters/ch02-laying-the-foundation.md]
- If Upward is chosen, a hard-to-replicate skill is named. [chapters/ch02-laying-the-foundation.md]
- The backstory fits elevator-pitch length and contains no invented events (the user confirms). [chapters/ch02-laying-the-foundation.md]
- There are 2-4 traits and 3-5 topics, and every topic maps to the niche. [chapters/ch02-laying-the-foundation.md]

## D-002 2. LinkedIn profile as sales page
- ask: "Rewrite my LinkedIn profile so it sells me to my niche."
- inputs: the niche and character brief, the current profile, and the links to host.
- data: the current profile and the profile-view dashboard.
- upstream: niche and character brief (1)
- downstream: the destination of tribe and content traffic (3-6).
- depth: partial

### steps
1. Finish the profile before any tribe-building or posting. [chapters/ch03-your-linkedin-profile.md]
2. Render backstory, traits and topics into the headline, banner, About and Featured sections. [chapters/ch03-your-linkedin-profile.md]
3. Write each element as conversion copy aimed at a relevant viewer deciding whether to connect or follow, not as a résumé. [chapters/ch03-your-linkedin-profile.md]
4. Host outbound links in the About section, so posts can say "first link in my profile". [chapters/ch03-your-linkedin-profile.md, chapters/ch06-posting-algorithm-consistency.md]
5. Use profile views as the scoreboard. [chapters/ch03-your-linkedin-profile.md]
### criteria
- A stranger can tell from the profile who it serves. [chapters/ch03-your-linkedin-profile.md]
- The profile is not a job-history dump. [chapters/ch03-your-linkedin-profile.md]
- The About section hosts the links the user wants traffic sent to. [chapters/ch03-your-linkedin-profile.md]
- The backstory, traits and topics from the brief all appear somewhere in the profile. [chapters/ch03-your-linkedin-profile.md]

## D-003 3. Story post
- ask: "Turn this experience into a LinkedIn story post."
- inputs: a true experience or opinion, the character brief (traits, topics), and the post type (text, or image/video).
- data: none.
- upstream: character brief (1), idea backlog (7)
- downstream: feeds the weekly batch (4).
- depth: full

### steps
1. Check the idea against the focused topics and the voice-fit test. [chapters/ch02-laying-the-foundation.md, chapters/ch05-creating-content.md]
2. Shape it to the 5 viral traits: Inspirational, Believable, Authentic, Relatable, Polarizing. [chapters/ch05-creating-content.md]
3. Write the hook as 3 short lines with blank lines between, so it lands on line 5 for text or about line 3 for image/video. [chapters/ch05-creating-content.md]
4. Format the body as White Space + Wave: a blank line between every line, and sentence lengths that rise and fall. [chapters/ch05-creating-content.md]
5. Pick the Feel: polarizing (~60-70% agree), or an authentic story of overcoming adversity. [chapters/ch05-creating-content.md]
6. Finish for the algorithm: no outbound link in the body, ≤3 relevant hashtags, and a closing question as the CTA. [chapters/ch06-posting-algorithm-consistency.md]
### criteria
- The hook sits on the last visible line (line 5 for text, ~line 3 for image/video). [chapters/ch05-creating-content.md]
- Every line is separated by a blank line, with no paragraph of more than one sentence. [chapters/ch05-creating-content.md]
- Sentence lengths visibly alternate long and short. [chapters/ch05-creating-content.md]
- The post takes a position some readers would disagree with (not vanilla), or it tells an overcoming-adversity arc. [chapters/ch05-creating-content.md]
- There is no URL in the post body. [chapters/ch06-posting-algorithm-consistency.md]
- There are 0-3 hashtags, all relevant to the niche. [chapters/ch06-posting-algorithm-consistency.md]
- The post ends with a question or a CTA. [chapters/ch06-posting-algorithm-consistency.md]
- The post matches at least one of the stated traits. [chapters/ch02-laying-the-foundation.md]

## D-004 4. Weekly content batch
- ask: "Write and schedule my LinkedIn posts for next week."
- inputs: the idea backlog, the character brief, the user's best posting time (or a plan to test it), and a scheduling tool.
- data: a scheduling tool (Buffer/Hootsuite) and post engagement analytics.
- upstream: idea backlog (7), story post (3), character brief (1)
- downstream: feeds tribe growth (5, 6).
- depth: full

### steps
1. Pull ideas from the week's captured backlog. [chapters/ch05-creating-content.md, chapters/ch06-posting-algorithm-consistency.md]
2. Assign formats to weekdays from the menu: story, checklist + action steps, clickbait + strong CTA, recognition listicle, topical/newsworthy, vulnerability/mistakes, free resource. [chapters/ch05-creating-content.md]
3. Write 3-5 posts in one 2-hour block. [chapters/ch06-posting-algorithm-consistency.md]
4. Apply the 10 algorithm tips to each post. [chapters/ch06-posting-algorithm-consistency.md]
5. Schedule Mon-Fri in Buffer or Hootsuite at the tested best time. [chapters/ch06-posting-algorithm-consistency.md]
6. Commit to a 30-consecutive-day run, then monitor and adjust times. [chapters/ch06-posting-algorithm-consistency.md]
### criteria
- The batch has 3-5 posts. [chapters/ch06-posting-algorithm-consistency.md]
- At least 2 different formats from the menu are used, so the posts are not all stories. [chapters/ch05-creating-content.md]
- No post links out in the body. [chapters/ch06-posting-algorithm-consistency.md]
- Each post has ≤3 relevant hashtags and ends with a CTA or question. [chapters/ch06-posting-algorithm-consistency.md]
- Every post sits on one of the 3-5 focused topics (no topic-hopping). [chapters/ch06-posting-algorithm-consistency.md]
- Each post has a scheduled weekday and time. [chapters/ch06-posting-algorithm-consistency.md]

## D-005 5. Industry-titan connection plan
- ask: "Plan how I get on the radar of the 5-10 biggest voices in my niche."
- inputs: the niche, and candidate creators with ~20K+ followers.
- data: LinkedIn (titans' follower counts and posts).
- upstream: niche brief (1), profile (2)
- downstream: the Recognition Post enters the weekly batch (4).
- depth: full

### steps
1. List 5-10 titans. [chapters/ch04-building-your-tribe.md]
2. Rule: stay out of their inbox. [chapters/ch04-building-your-tribe.md]
3. Leave 10+ meaningful comments with specific takeaways on their posts over 2-3 weeks. [chapters/ch04-building-your-tribe.md]
4. Publish a Solo Recognition Post that credits one titan's post and says how it helped. [chapters/ch04-building-your-tribe.md]
5. After they react, send a custom connection request that references the relationship. [chapters/ch04-building-your-tribe.md]
### criteria
- The list has 5-10 names, each with ≥20K followers. [chapters/ch04-building-your-tribe.md]
- The plan contains no DM or cold ask before the request. [chapters/ch04-building-your-tribe.md]
- The comment drafts are specific (none read "great job", "100%" or a bare emoji). [chapters/ch04-building-your-tribe.md]
- The Recognition Post credits exactly one person and does not mass-tag. [chapters/ch04-building-your-tribe.md]
- The connection request is personalized and references the prior interaction. [chapters/ch04-building-your-tribe.md]

## D-006 6. Daily tribe-building routine
- ask: "Give me a daily routine to grow relevant connections on LinkedIn."
- inputs: the niche, target cities and companies, and peers' profiles.
- data: LinkedIn (profile-view list, Advanced Search).
- upstream: profile (2)
- downstream: grows reach for (3, 4).
- depth: full

### steps
1. Run the 8 connection sources: colleagues, Career Advice, "More suggestions", Groups, profile viewers (added within ~30 min), similar profiles, Advanced Search, and the 2X Simple Ask. [chapters/ch04-building-your-tribe.md]
2. Switch the feed to Recent to be the first comment on big posts. [chapters/ch04-building-your-tribe.md]
3. Leave 5 thoughtful comments a day, each ending with a question, tagging one relevant person where it fits. [chapters/ch04-building-your-tribe.md]
4. Like and reply to every interaction on your own posts. [chapters/ch04-building-your-tribe.md]
### criteria
- The routine covers all 8 sources, or names which ones are skipped and why. [chapters/ch04-building-your-tribe.md]
- Any 2X Simple Ask post tags exactly two people. [chapters/ch04-building-your-tribe.md]
- The daily quota is 5 comments, and each sample comment ends in a question. [chapters/ch04-building-your-tribe.md]
- No step mass-tags 15-30 people. [chapters/ch04-building-your-tribe.md]

## D-007 7. Idea capture backlog
- ask: "Set me up so I never run out of LinkedIn post ideas."
- inputs: the focused topics and the tools the user uses.
- data: Pocket, Feedly, HubSpot generator, Twitter. These tools are dated (`SKILL.md` Scope).
- upstream: character brief (1)
- downstream: feeds (3, 4).
- depth: full

### steps
1. Experience: a notes-app folder for daily work lessons. [chapters/ch05-creating-content.md]
2. Save articles you read (Pocket). [chapters/ch05-creating-content.md]
3. Discover new sources (Feedly RSS). [chapters/ch05-creating-content.md]
4. Use a blog idea generator to seed long-form posts, then split them into micro-posts. [chapters/ch05-creating-content.md]
5. Keep a curated niche Twitter feed, screenshot good ideas, annotate them and file them to a Drive content folder. [chapters/ch05-creating-content.md]
### criteria
- Each of the 5 sources has a named place where captures land. [chapters/ch05-creating-content.md]
- The feed sources are limited to the niche. [chapters/ch05-creating-content.md]
- The weekly batch draws from ideas captured all week across the five sources. [chapters/ch06-posting-algorithm-consistency.md]
