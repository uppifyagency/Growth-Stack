# fazio-vsl

## B-031 F1. One-Secret VSL script
- ask: "Write a VSL script for my [service] to book calls."
- inputs: the "secret" (what you do differently), backstory and rep volume, bad vs good examples, quantified results, component list, one-sentence case study, pricing model.
- data: client results/case studies.
- upstream: offer, H1 optional.
- downstream: F3, F4.
- depth: full

### steps
1. Hook/reframe: name the desire and disarm the objection. [chapters/ch03-vsl-example-1.md]
2. Backstory: authority through volume of reps. [chapters/ch02-vsl-structures.md]
3. Reveal the secret: what, how, why it beats the norm. [chapters/ch02-vsl-structures.md]
4. Bad-vs-good examples, then quantifiable results. [chapters/ch03-vsl-example-1.md]
5. How to do it (over-exaggerate difficulty). [chapters/ch02-vsl-structures.md]
6. Logical breakdown (the numbers, e.g. 100 emails = 1 client). [chapters/ch03-vsl-example-1.md]
7. Component breakdown (all pieces required). [chapters/ch03-vsl-example-1.md]
8. CTA + risk reversal; decide price display. [chapters/ch03-vsl-example-1.md, chapters/ch02-vsl-structures.md]
### criteria
- Beats appear in the prescribed order. [chapters/ch02-vsl-structures.md]
- The 8 prospect questions (who, what, how, true, trust, results, fit, next step) are answered in order. [chapters/ch02-vsl-structures.md]
- One secret only. [chapters/ch03-vsl-example-1.md]
- Contains a side-by-side bad vs good example. [chapters/ch03-vsl-example-1.md]
- Results are numeric. [chapters/ch02-vsl-structures.md]
- Logical breakdown shows explicit math. [chapters/ch03-vsl-example-1.md]
- High-ticket/complex: no exact price (or price on a separate end slide); productized: pay-now price. [chapters/ch02-vsl-structures.md]

## B-032 F2. Three-Secrets (three-pain-point) VSL script
- ask: "Rewrite my VSL in the three-secrets format."
- inputs: same as F1 plus three prospect pain points and their resolutions, a bold quantified promise.
- data: client results.
- upstream: F1 (book says start with one-secret).
- downstream: F3, F4.
- depth: full

### steps
1. Big promise hook (quantified). [chapters/ch04-vsl-example-2.md]
2. Who am I (relatable origin). [chapters/ch04-vsl-example-2.md]
3. Pain 1, 2, 3, each with a resolution. [chapters/ch04-vsl-example-2.md]
4. Results, logical breakdown, component breakdown. [chapters/ch04-vsl-example-2.md]
5. "I will do this for you" CTA; done-for-you breakdown removes all client effort. [chapters/ch04-vsl-example-2.md]
### criteria
- Exactly three pain-to-resolution beats. [chapters/ch02-vsl-structures.md]
- Never says "three secrets" (uses "three things I do"). [chapters/ch02-vsl-structures.md]
- Hook contains a number. [chapters/ch04-vsl-example-2.md]
- DFY breakdown leaves the client no operational task. [chapters/ch04-vsl-example-2.md]
- Prospect question-order respected. [chapters/ch02-vsl-structures.md]

## B-033 F3. VSL recording and production plan
- ask: "How do I film and publish this VSL cheaply?"
- inputs: final script and slides, equipment, recording space.
- data: Loom, Camtasia, YouTube.
- upstream: F1/F2.
- downstream: F4.
- depth: full

### steps
1. Meet the filming bar: daylight on face (source behind camera), presentable, face visible, fast-enough pace, expect 3-10 takes. [chapters/ch05-filming-vsls.md]
2. Record in Loom (screen + camera, presenter view). [chapters/ch05-filming-vsls.md]
3. Stitch in Camtasia at 1920x1080; export local file. [chapters/ch05-filming-vsls.md]
4. Upload to YouTube unlisted, descriptive name, not-for-kids. [chapters/ch05-filming-vsls.md]
5. Embed on the landing page; optionally link channel to Google Ads for view retargeting. [chapters/ch05-filming-vsls.md]
### criteria
- Lighting is daylight from in front of the speaker. [chapters/ch05-filming-vsls.md]
- Face visible on camera. [chapters/ch05-filming-vsls.md]
- Interest set in the first 3 seconds (no slow intro). [chapters/ch05-filming-vsls.md]
- Hosted unlisted, not public or private. [chapters/ch05-filming-vsls.md]
- Pricing kept on a swappable end slide when shown. [chapters/ch02-vsl-structures.md]

## B-034 F4. VSL landing page (single scroll)
- ask: "Build the landing page for my VSL."
- inputs: VSL embed, client interview video, results images, how-it-works, disqualifiers, booking link, offer type/price.
- data: Carrd/ClickFunnels, Calendly, domain DNS.
- upstream: F1-F3.
- downstream: F6.
- depth: full

### steps
1. Pick builder by offer: Carrd (high-ticket), ClickFunnels/Kajabi (productized checkout), Webflow (needs polish). [chapters/ch06-landing-pages.md]
2. Lay out in order: Headline, VSL, client interview, results, how it works, disqualification, Calendly. [chapters/ch06-landing-pages.md]
3. Pure white/black background, one column, no nav. [chapters/ch06-landing-pages.md]
4. Booking only for high-ticket; pay-now checkout for productized. [chapters/ch06-landing-pages.md]
5. Publish to custom domain (A records + CNAME). [chapters/ch06-landing-pages.md]
### criteria
- Sections appear in the prescribed order. [chapters/ch06-landing-pages.md]
- No navigation menu; single page. [chapters/ch06-landing-pages.md]
- Contains an explicit "who this is not for" section. [chapters/ch06-landing-pages.md]
- Conversion path matches offer type (call vs pay-now). [chapters/ch06-landing-pages.md]
- Ends in an embedded scheduler (high-ticket). [chapters/ch06-landing-pages.md]

## B-035 F5. Personalized cold email + Big Win follow-up sequence
- ask: "Write my cold email and follow-ups for [service]."
- inputs: qualified prospect list (can afford you), per-prospect research, one-sentence case study, a pool of client wins.
- data: prospect list, cold-email tooling (sending domain, SPF/DKIM per DFY breakdown in `chapters/ch04-vsl-example-2.md`).
- upstream: case studies.
- downstream: F4 (CTA destination).
- depth: full

### steps
1. Pre-qualify prospects who can afford you. [chapters/ch03-vsl-example-1.md]
2. ~5 min research per prospect; write a concrete compliment that passes the say-it-out-loud test. [chapters/ch03-vsl-example-1.md]
3. Follow with a one-sentence case study. [chapters/ch04-vsl-example-2.md]
4. Day-3 bump ("just bumping this to the top of your inbox"). [chapters/ch04-vsl-example-2.md]
5. Big Win #1, #2, #3..., each a fresh client result. [chapters/ch04-vsl-example-2.md]
6. Set volume from the math (4% book, 25% close = 100 emails per client). [chapters/ch03-vsl-example-1.md]
### criteria
- First line is specific to one prospect and could not be sent to anyone else. [chapters/ch03-vsl-example-1.md]
- No merge-field-only personalization. [chapters/ch04-vsl-example-2.md]
- Not "we-we-we" self-centered. [chapters/ch03-vsl-example-1.md]
- Contains a one-sentence case study (brand, result, tool). [chapters/ch03-vsl-example-1.md]
- Has a bump email at day 3. [chapters/ch04-vsl-example-2.md]
- Each follow-up after the bump cites a different client result. [chapters/ch04-vsl-example-2.md]

## B-036 F6. Live chat and retargeting setup for the VSL page
- ask: "Add live chat and retargeting to my VSL page."
- inputs: Facebook page, Business Manager access, Calendly, Zapier, landing page head access.
- data: ManyChat, Meta Business Manager, Zapier, Calendly.
- upstream: F4.
- downstream: I8 (deeper retargeting).
- depth: full

### steps
1. ManyChat customer chat: install snippet in page head, greeting, open on first view; replies via Business Suite app. [chapters/ch07-retargeting-live-chat.md]
2. Install Pixel in page head. [chapters/ch07-retargeting-live-chat.md]
3. Zapier: Calendly booking fires a Conversions API lead event. [chapters/ch07-retargeting-live-chat.md]
4. Zapier: add bookers to a "call scheduled" exclusion audience. [chapters/ch07-retargeting-live-chat.md]
5. Visitor custom audience (e.g. 30 days); ~$1/day Story ads, manual placements, optimized for lead event. [chapters/ch07-retargeting-live-chat.md]
### criteria
- Live chat present before retargeting is built. [chapters/ch07-retargeting-live-chat.md]
- Chat greeting written and set to open on first view. [chapters/ch07-retargeting-live-chat.md]
- Lead event is server-side (Conversions API), not pixel-only. [chapters/ch07-retargeting-live-chat.md]
- Bookers excluded from retargeting. [chapters/ch07-retargeting-live-chat.md]
- Placements manual. [chapters/ch07-retargeting-live-chat.md]
- Story ad script is casual face-to-camera follow-up. [chapters/ch07-retargeting-live-chat.md]
