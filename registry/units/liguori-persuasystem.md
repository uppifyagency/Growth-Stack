## B-024 L1. Sales meeting script (Le 7 fasi di vendita)
- ask: "Scrivimi lo script per l'appuntamento di vendita di [prodotto]." / "Write my sales call script."
- inputs: product, client interests/passions (researched), the 3 advantages, known weakness, frequent objections.
- data: none (CRM notes on the client help).
- upstream: L2.
- downstream: L4, L5.
- depth: full

### steps
1. Fase 1 Preparazione: state (positivo, sicuro, rilassato, energico, determinato) + mantra di attivazione. [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
2. Fase 2 Rapport: smile, name, energy slightly above client, prepared interests. [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
3. Fase 3 Analisi dei bisogni: strategic questions, summary + double verification, create the need. [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
4. Fase 4 Proposta: entusiasmo, mission, tre vantaggi, tecnica del sandwich. [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
5. Fase 5 Attivazione emotiva: emotional gate, Go first, reciprocity. [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
6. Fase 6 Gestione obiezioni (see L4). [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
7. Fase 7 Chiusura (5 momenti) and fidelizzazione. [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
### criteria
- All 7 phases present in order; none skipped. [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
- Proposal lists exactly 3 advantages (not 6-10). [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
- Needs-analysis questions use "come", not "perché". [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
- Needs summary is confirmed twice. [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
- Contains an emotional-gate line ("Riesci a immaginare...", "Ti è mai capitato di..."). [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
- Closing covers the 5 momenti. [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
- No "ma / però / tuttavia". [chapters/ch07-tecniche-verbali.md]

## B-025 L2. Needs-analysis / calibration question sheet (Calibrazione strategica)
- ask: "Prepara le domande per capire il cliente prima di proporre."
- inputs: product category, client type (B2B/B2C), meeting context.
- data: none.
- upstream: none.
- downstream: L1, L3.
- depth: full

### steps
1. Ask permission to take notes ("contesto cornice"). [chapters/ch06-calibrazione-strategica.md]
2. Domande: opening questions (e.g. "Se dovesse comprare un prodotto del genere, perché lo acquisterebbe?") to profile verso a / via da. [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md, chapters/ch03-principi-comunicazione.md]
3. Ascolto/Osservazione: record the exact words. [chapters/ch06-calibrazione-strategica.md]
4. Verifica: second round reusing the client's words to create need. [chapters/ch06-calibrazione-strategica.md]
5. Ricalco (emotions) then Guida (tailored solution). [chapters/ch06-calibrazione-strategica.md]
### criteria
- Starts with a note-taking permission line. [chapters/ch06-calibrazione-strategica.md]
- Has a field to capture the client's exact words. [chapters/ch06-calibrazione-strategica.md]
- Has a verification round that reuses those words. [chapters/ch06-calibrazione-strategica.md]
- Contains at least one question that classifies verso a / via da. [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
- Contains a gain AND a loss question (cosa guadagna / cosa perde). [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]

## B-026 L3. Persuasive pitch (flusso persuasivo in 9 passaggi)
- ask: "Scrivi il pitch del mio servizio." / "Write a persuasive pitch."
- inputs: client problem in their words, 3 advantages, one weakness, stats/success stories, mission.
- data: none.
- upstream: L2.
- downstream: F1/F2 (script beats), L4.
- depth: full

### steps
1. Ascolto, then Riformulazione in the client's lexicon. [chapters/ch10-conclusione-sintesi.md]
2. Ricalco emotivo: describe how the problem feels. [chapters/ch10-conclusione-sintesi.md]
3. Proiezione negativa sul futuro: the cost of not acting. [chapters/ch10-conclusione-sintesi.md]
4. 3 vantaggi + svantaggio (sandwich: 3 advantages, weakness justified, 4th recovery advantage). [chapters/ch10-conclusione-sintesi.md, chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
5. Preselezione: stats, success stories, uniqueness. [chapters/ch10-conclusione-sintesi.md]
6. Mission e valori. [chapters/ch10-conclusione-sintesi.md]
7. Gestione obiezioni, then Semplificazione (clear purchase steps). [chapters/ch10-conclusione-sintesi.md]
8. Phrase features as sensory experiences (linguaggio evocativo); yes-set before CTA with the client's name. [chapters/ch07-tecniche-verbali.md]
### criteria
- Follows the 9 steps in order. [chapters/ch10-conclusione-sintesi.md]
- Exactly one disclosed weakness, framed as an effect of an advantage, followed by a recovery advantage. [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
- Contains a negative future projection. [chapters/ch10-conclusione-sintesi.md]
- At least one feature rewritten as an experience. [chapters/ch07-tecniche-verbali.md]
- Ends with explicit purchase steps. [chapters/ch10-conclusione-sintesi.md]
- No "ma / però"; no "non le voglio far perdere tempo". [chapters/ch07-tecniche-verbali.md]

## B-027 L4. Objection-handling sheet
- ask: "Prepara le risposte alle obiezioni più frequenti."
- inputs: frequent objections, product proof points (volumes sold, guarantees).
- data: none.
- upstream: L1.
- downstream: F4 (disqualification), sales training.
- depth: full

### steps
1. Sort objections into 5 categories: venditore, prodotto, prezzo, tempo, concorrenza. [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
2. Write one script per frequent objection. [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
3. Choose a technique per script: Ristrutturare, Equivalenza complessa, Ricalco e guida, Disqualifier, Domande aperte/chiuse, Rottura del comfort (last resort). [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
4. Keep answers short ("less is more"); replace ma/però with "è proprio per questo che". [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md, chapters/ch07-tecniche-verbali.md]
### criteria
- Every objection tagged with one of the 5 categories. [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
- Every answer names its technique. [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
- Each objection reply is short and incisive ("less is more"). [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
- Rottura del comfort, if used, is marked last resort. [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
- Ricalco answers first agree with the client's view, then guide. [chapters/ch06-calibrazione-strategica.md]
- No "ma / però / tuttavia". [chapters/ch07-tecniche-verbali.md]

## B-028 L5. Closing script (Chiusura in 5 momenti)
- ask: "Come chiudo la vendita? Scrivimi la chiusura."
- inputs: post-sale steps, add-on product, client's stated advantages.
- data: none.
- upstream: L1, L4.
- downstream: none.
- depth: full

### steps
1. Definizione passaggi post-vendita. [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
2. Presupposizione + proiezione sul futuro (double embedded choice). [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md, chapters/ch07-tecniche-verbali.md]
3. Primo tentativo di chiusura, handle last weak objection. [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
4. Riproporre i SUOI vantaggi. [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
5. Rassicurazione (quality, compliments, post-purchase availability). [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
### criteria
- All 5 moments present in order. [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
- Uses a temporal presupposition ("mentre...", "dopo che..."). [chapters/ch07-tecniche-verbali.md]
- Restated advantages use the client's words. [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]
- Ends with reassurance. [chapters/ch09-7-fasi-vendita.md]

## B-029 L6. Persuasive language rewrite (linguaggio strategico)
- ask: "Riscrivi questo testo di vendita in linguaggio persuasivo."
- inputs: existing copy or script, client desires.
- data: none.
- upstream: any copy (H6, I4, F1).
- downstream: same.
- depth: partial

### steps
1. Turn features into sensory experiences. [chapters/ch07-tecniche-verbali.md]
2. Add future projections and temporal presuppositions. [chapters/ch07-tecniche-verbali.md]
3. Replace negations with positive commands; remove ma/però/tuttavia. [chapters/ch07-tecniche-verbali.md]
4. Build a yes-set before the call-to-action. [chapters/ch07-tecniche-verbali.md]
5. Add hypnotic kit elements sparingly (repetitions, power words, metaphors). [chapters/ch07-tecniche-verbali.md]
### criteria
- Zero "ma / però / tuttavia". [chapters/ch07-tecniche-verbali.md]
- No negated commands ("non pensare...", "non le voglio far perdere tempo"). [chapters/ch07-tecniche-verbali.md]
- At least one future projection. [chapters/ch07-tecniche-verbali.md]
- Yes-set precedes the CTA. [chapters/ch07-tecniche-verbali.md]
- Lists at most 3 advantages. [chapters/ch03-principi-comunicazione.md]

## B-030 L7. Cold in-person approach opener
- ask: "Come approccio a freddo i passanti allo stand/fiera?"
- inputs: venue, product, opening line.
- data: none.
- upstream: none.
- downstream: L1.
- depth: partial

### steps
1. Over the shoulder: approach from the side, a step ahead. [chapters/ch04-presentarsi.md]
2. Indirect opener (banal remark or curious question); move to product only if they smile/respond. [chapters/ch04-presentarsi.md]
3. Name procedure: shake hands without your name, wait for theirs, repeat it, then say yours. [chapters/ch04-presentarsi.md]
4. Shock-and-command recovery if attention drops. [chapters/ch04-presentarsi.md]
### criteria
- Opener does not mention the product. [chapters/ch04-presentarsi.md]
- Script has the name sequence in the right order. [chapters/ch04-presentarsi.md]
- Name reused only at key moments (product, CTA). [chapters/ch04-presentarsi.md]
