# shleyner-landing-pages

## C-019 L1. Transformational landing page (CDCA)
- ask: "Build a landing page for my course/ebook/webinar" / "Rewrite this page to double conversions."
- inputs: the content being sold (worth it), one real prospect ("person"), proof assets, the incentive, the current page (control) if any.
- data: A/B testing and analytics (L9).
- upstream: L7
- downstream: composes L2–L6 → feeds L8, L9.
- depth: full

### steps
1. Confirm the content is "worthy of a transformational landing page". [chapters/ch01-foundations-cdca.md]
2. Run the personal interview and pick the **person** (L7). [chapters/ch02-clarity-hero-congruence.md]
3. **Clarity**: write the 6-element Hero (L2). [chapters/ch02-clarity-hero-congruence.md]
4. **Desire**: write the fascination wall (L3). [chapters/ch03-desire-fascinations-rules.md, chapters/ch04-desire-process-18-filters.md]
5. **Credibility**: logos, guarantee, statistics, testimonials (L4). [chapters/ch05-credibility.md]
6. **Action**: answer "Why now?" (L6). [chapters/ch06-compelling-action.md]
7. Build the page first, then derive the ads from the Hero (L8). [chapters/ch02-clarity-hero-congruence.md, patterns.md]
8. Test it as a big test against the control (L9). [chapters/ch07-testing.md]
### criteria
- Page has a section doing each CDCA job, in order. [chapters/ch01-foundations-cdca.md, cheatsheet.md]
- One CTA per divider, in a color used nowhere else on the page. [chapters/ch06-compelling-action.md]
- Proof shown high on the page. [chapters/ch05-credibility.md]
- Conversion form has the fewest possible fields. [chapters/ch06-compelling-action.md]
- Incentive is time- or supply-limited. [chapters/ch06-compelling-action.md]
- If replacing a control: looks/reads nothing like it. [chapters/ch01-foundations-cdca.md, chapters/ch07-testing.md]
- Clear, not clever: no exclamation-mark hype. [chapters/ch02-clarity-hero-congruence.md]

## C-020 L2. Hero divider (above the fold)
- ask: "Write the hero section for this landing page."
- inputs: what the page advertises, the one big promise, the incentive, art concept.
- data: none.
- upstream: L7
- downstream: part of L1, source for L8; reused as the rewrite target in L9.
- depth: full

### steps
1. **Primer**: say plainly what it is; end with a colon. [chapters/ch02-clarity-hero-congruence.md, cheatsheet.md]
2. **Headline**: one big, beautiful promise; clear, not clever. [chapters/ch02-clarity-hero-congruence.md]
3. **Subhead**: **Future Pacing** with "imagine / picture this / stop / start"; periods, not exclamation marks. [chapters/ch02-clarity-hero-congruence.md]
4. **Body**: answer "how?" in ~20–30 words. [chapters/ch02-clarity-hero-congruence.md, cheatsheet.md]
5. **CTA**: an incentive creating urgency (optional downsell). [chapters/ch02-clarity-hero-congruence.md, chapters/ch06-compelling-action.md]
6. **Art**: visualize what they get and reinforce the incentive. [chapters/ch02-clarity-hero-congruence.md]
### criteria
- Six elements present in this order. [chapters/ch02-clarity-hero-congruence.md, cheatsheet.md]
- Primer ends with a colon. [chapters/ch02-clarity-hero-congruence.md]
- Headline is a single promise, no wordplay. [chapters/ch02-clarity-hero-congruence.md]
- Subhead uses a future-pacing trigger word and no "!". [chapters/ch02-clarity-hero-congruence.md]
- Body is ~20–30 words and explains how. [chapters/ch02-clarity-hero-congruence.md]
- CTA states an incentive. [chapters/ch02-clarity-hero-congruence.md]

## C-021 L3. Fascination wall
- ask: "Write the bullets that make people want this" / "Write fascinations for my course."
- inputs: the content itself (text, or transcripts of audio/video).
- data: transcripts of the content.
- upstream: the content
- downstream: part of L1; filters also generate headlines/subject lines (overlaps N1).
- depth: full

### steps
1. Transcribe audio/video; highlight every excerpt with valuable info + the benefit of knowing it (**find the nugget**, hunt benefits first). [chapters/ch04-desire-process-18-filters.md, patterns.md]
2. Validate each nugget with an **If/Then** ("If you know X, then you get Y"). [chapters/ch04-desire-process-18-filters.md]
3. **Tease the value and state the benefit** (allude, don't reveal). [chapters/ch04-desire-process-18-filters.md]
4. **Apply a filter** from the 18 (Mechanisms, Statement+Interest+Benefit, What, What never, Right?-Wrong, Why, When, Specific question, If-then, Quickest-easiest, Number, Big promise, How, Caution, Truth, Best, Surprising, Are you). [chapters/ch04-desire-process-18-filters.md, cheatsheet.md]
5. Write ~7× more than needed and pare down. [chapters/ch03-desire-fascinations-rules.md]
6. Create variance in layout (checkerboard) and filters. [chapters/ch03-desire-fascinations-rules.md]
### criteria
- Each fascination is 1–3 sentences. [chapters/ch03-desire-fascinations-rules.md]
- Each promises a benefit and withholds the how. [chapters/ch03-desire-fascinations-rules.md]
- Each delivers on its promise in the content (not clickbait). [chapters/ch03-desire-fascinations-rules.md]
- No "buy/purchase" (use "get"), no "learn/study" (use "discover"). [chapters/ch03-desire-fascinations-rules.md, cheatsheet.md]
- Simple words (≤2 syllables) except insider terms for expert audiences. [chapters/ch03-desire-fascinations-rules.md]
- Several different filters used; fear filters (#4, #14) not dominant. [chapters/ch04-desire-process-18-filters.md, patterns.md]
- Benefits are specific and backed by a specific or a credible source. [chapters/ch03-desire-fascinations-rules.md]

## C-022 L4. Credibility section (confidence signals and testimonials)
- ask: "Add a trust section" / "How should I show proof on this page?"
- inputs: customer logos (with permission), guarantee terms, market-relative statistics, testimonials.
- data: testimonial store/embed tool.
- upstream: L5, D3
- downstream: part of L1.
- depth: full

### steps
1. **Logos**: well-known customers willing to vouch, plus awards; place near the top ("show your proof first"). [chapters/ch05-credibility.md]
2. **Guarantee**: transfer risk (paid: lifetime/100% money-back/60-day; free: unsubscribe anytime/one email a week/never share). [chapters/ch05-credibility.md]
3. **Statistics**: numbers substantial relative to your market (time on market, copies sold, reach). [chapters/ch05-credibility.md]
4. Choose **Whale** (one highly credible name) or **School** (wall of many customers); prefer School without a credible whale. [chapters/ch05-credibility.md, patterns.md]
5. Place proof near the claim it backs. [chapters/ch05-credibility.md]
### criteria
- Proof appears high on the page. [chapters/ch05-credibility.md]
- A guarantee is stated in plain terms. [chapters/ch05-credibility.md]
- Each statistic is large relative to competitors. [chapters/ch05-credibility.md]
- Any standalone whale is a recognizably influential person. [chapters/ch05-credibility.md]
- School wall has visible volume. [chapters/ch05-credibility.md]

## C-023 L5. Testimonial collection campaign
- ask: "I have no testimonials, help me get some."
- inputs: audience/list, an incentive to offer.
- data: collection tool, email list.
- upstream: none
- downstream: feeds L4, D3, N10 (cool angle).
- depth: full

### steps
1. Set up a collection/embed tool for video + text (source uses Testimonial.to). [chapters/ch05-credibility.md, patterns.md]
2. Email a clear, easy ask with an incentive (gift card, backlink, free content). [chapters/ch05-credibility.md]
3. Bake the ask into the content itself (at every intermission) so testimonials accrue. [chapters/ch05-credibility.md]
### criteria
- Ask offers an explicit incentive. [chapters/ch05-credibility.md]
- Ask is one clear step. [chapters/ch05-credibility.md]
- Collection point exists inside the content. [chapters/ch05-credibility.md]

## C-024 L6. "Why now?" action block
- ask: "Make people act now instead of later."
- inputs: incentive options, form fields, guarantee, possible downsell.
- data: bounce rate for the downsell decision.
- upstream: part of L1
- downstream: the incentive is the favorite small-test variable in L9.
- depth: full

### steps
1. **FOMO**: an economic incentive limited by time or supply (flash sale, % off, first-N bonuses). [chapters/ch06-compelling-action.md]
2. **Easy**: cut the form to the fewest lines. [chapters/ch06-compelling-action.md]
3. **Nothing to lose**: guarantee; add a free **downsell** only if bounce is high. [chapters/ch06-compelling-action.md, patterns.md]
4. **Because you're asking**: one CTA per divider, anchored to the form, stand-out color. [chapters/ch06-compelling-action.md]
### criteria
- Incentive has a stated time or quantity limit. [chapters/ch06-compelling-action.md]
- No form field beyond what conversion needs. [chapters/ch06-compelling-action.md]
- Guarantee stated near the CTA. [chapters/ch06-compelling-action.md]
- Every divider has a CTA. [chapters/ch06-compelling-action.md]
- Downsell is absent at launch or justified by bounce data. [chapters/ch06-compelling-action.md]

## C-025 L7. Prospect interview → "person" and hook
- ask: "Help me understand who I'm writing this page for."
- inputs: access to one real prospect.
- data: interview recording/transcript.
- upstream: none
- downstream: feeds L1, L2, and L9's recovery loop.
- depth: partial

### steps
1. Interview one real prospect with open-ended questions. [chapters/ch02-clarity-hero-congruence.md, patterns.md]
2. **Mirror**: repeat their last words back as a question. [chapters/ch02-clarity-hero-congruence.md]
3. Record it instead of taking notes. [chapters/ch02-clarity-hero-congruence.md]
4. Mine the rawest, most honest line for the hook; write the page to that person, not a persona. [chapters/ch02-clarity-hero-congruence.md, patterns.md]
### criteria
- Based on a named real interviewee, not a composite persona. [chapters/ch02-clarity-hero-congruence.md]
- Hook line traces to a quote from the recording. [patterns.md]

## C-026 L8. Congruent ad from the Hero
- ask: "Write the Facebook/email ad that sends traffic to this page."
- inputs: the finished Hero (L2).
- data: none.
- upstream: L2
- downstream: traffic to L1. Same principle as Neville's ad scent (N7).
- depth: full

### steps
1. Build the landing page first. [chapters/ch02-clarity-hero-congruence.md, patterns.md]
2. Derive ad copy, art and layout from the Hero; often screenshot the Hero into the post. [chapters/ch02-clarity-hero-congruence.md]
3. If space is tight, use the headline + part of the art. [patterns.md]
### criteria
- Ad headline matches the Hero headline. [chapters/ch02-clarity-hero-congruence.md]
- Ad art matches the Hero art. [chapters/ch02-clarity-hero-congruence.md]

## C-027 L9. Landing-page test plan (big test, then small tests)
- ask: "How should I test this new page against the old one?"
- inputs: control page, contender page, traffic volume, testing tool.
- data: A/B tool, analytics, traffic.
- upstream: L1
- downstream: loops back to L2/L7.
- depth: full

### steps
1. **Big test**: CDCA contender vs bare control. [chapters/ch07-testing.md]
2. Run to statistical significance (enough traffic) before calling it. [chapters/ch07-testing.md]
3. Win → promote the contender to control; run **small tests** cloning it with one variable changed (art, button color, downsell, incentive; incentive is the favorite). [chapters/ch07-testing.md]
4. Lose → return to the person: right angle, problem, person? Rewrite only the Hero hook (headline, subhead, body) and retest. [chapters/ch07-testing.md]
### criteria
- Control and contender named. [chapters/ch07-testing.md]
- Significance condition stated before launch. [chapters/ch07-testing.md]
- Each small test changes exactly one variable. [chapters/ch07-testing.md]
- Loss branch rewrites only the hook; content, fascinations and testimonials stay. [chapters/ch07-testing.md]
