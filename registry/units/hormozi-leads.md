# hormozi-leads

## B-001 H1. Lead magnet
- ask: "Design a lead magnet for my [offer] that gets more engaged leads."
- inputs: core offer and price, target customer, the problem the core offer solves, delivery budget.
- data: none (optional polls for name testing).
- upstream: core offer (out of slice: hormozi-offers).
- downstream: H2, H3, H5, H6, H10 (affiliates give it away), F1/F2 CTA, E14.
- depth: full

### steps
1. Pick a narrow, meaningful problem and who has it; confirm the next problem it reveals is solved by the core offer (Problem-Solution Cycle). [chapters/ch03-lead-magnets.md]
2. Pick the solution type: Reveal Their Problem, Samples & Trials, or One Step of a Multi-Step Process. [chapters/ch03-lead-magnets.md]
3. Pick the delivery method (software, information, services, physical); up to 12 versions (3 types x 4 methods). [chapters/ch03-lead-magnets.md]
4. Test the name: headline first, then image, then subheadline. [chapters/ch03-lead-magnets.md]
5. Make it easy to consume (every format, every device). [chapters/ch03-lead-magnets.md]
6. Make it darn good: "give away the secrets, sell the implementation". [chapters/ch03-lead-magnets.md]
7. End with a CTA = what to do + a reason to do it now (scarcity, urgency, or any "because"). [chapters/ch03-lead-magnets.md]
### criteria
- Solves one narrow problem completely (not a teaser). [chapters/ch03-lead-magnets.md]
- The next problem it reveals is named and is solved by the paid core offer. [chapters/ch03-lead-magnets.md]
- Declares exactly one of the 3 types. [chapters/ch03-lead-magnets.md]
- Ships in more than one format. [chapters/ch03-lead-magnets.md]
- Headline, image and subheadline exist and at least 2 headline variants are listed for testing. [chapters/ch03-lead-magnets.md]
- CTA states the action and a reason to act now. [chapters/ch03-lead-magnets.md]
- Uses clear wording over clever wording ("call now" over "don't delay"). [chapters/ch03-lead-magnets.md]

## B-002 H2. Warm outreach campaign (script pack)
- ask: "Write my warm outreach messages to get my first 5 clients."
- inputs: contact list sources (phone, email, social), offer, dream outcome, a result to name-drop.
- data: contact exports (phone, email, social).
- upstream: offer; optionally H1.
- downstream: H13 (Rule of 100 tracking), referrals (H9).
- depth: full

### steps
1. Build the list from every contact source; pick the platform with the most contacts. [chapters/ch05-warm-outreach.md]
2. Write a personalized greeting with no ask. [chapters/ch05-warm-outreach.md]
3. Plan volume: 100 reach outs/day, up to 3 attempts each. [chapters/ch05-warm-outreach.md]
4. Reply with A-C-A (Acknowledge, Compliment, Ask) for 3-4 exchanges. [chapters/ch05-warm-outreach.md]
5. Make the "Do you know anybody?" offer built on the Value Equation, first five free in exchange for use, feedback, review. [chapters/ch05-warm-outreach.md]
6. Price ramp after referrals start: 80% off next five, then 60%, 40%. [chapters/ch05-warm-outreach.md]
7. Keep the list warm with the 9-Word Email: "Are you still looking to [4-word desire]?" [chapters/ch05-warm-outreach.md]
### criteria
- Opening message contains a personal detail and no ask. [chapters/ch05-warm-outreach.md]
- Reply templates follow Acknowledge, Compliment, Ask in that order. [chapters/ch05-warm-outreach.md]
- Offer asks "do you know anyone", not "will you buy". [chapters/ch05-warm-outreach.md]
- Offer names a result from someone similar and the free-for-five terms. [chapters/ch05-warm-outreach.md]
- Plan states 100/day and up to 3 touches. [chapters/ch05-warm-outreach.md]
- Includes a 9-word re-engagement email with no links or images. [chapters/ch05-warm-outreach.md]
- Includes a "why not / what would make it worth it" reply for a no. [chapters/ch05-warm-outreach.md]

- data: none (platform analytics for growth tracking).
- upstream: none.

### steps
1. Pick a topic from the 5 categories (Far Past, Recent Past, Present, Trending, Manufactured). [chapters/ch06-post-free-content-1.md]
2. Write the headline with at least 2 of the 7 components (Recency, Relevancy, Celebrity, Proximity, Conflict, Unusual, Ongoing). [chapters/ch06-post-free-content-1.md]
3. Match the format to the platform. [chapters/ch06-post-free-content-1.md]
4. Retain with lists, steps or stories (state the count up front for lists). [chapters/ch06-post-free-content-1.md]

- Headline contains 2+ of the 7 components. [chapters/ch06-post-free-content-1.md]
- Uses at least one retention device (list, steps, story). [chapters/ch06-post-free-content-1.md]
- Delivers exactly what the hook promised (e.g. "7 ways" delivers 7). [chapters/ch06-post-free-content-1.md]
- Framed as "How I" rather than "How to". / [patterns.md, chapters/ch07-post-free-content-2.md]

## B-004 H4. Content ask plan (Give:Ask calendar)
- ask: "Plan how often I should pitch in my content without killing growth."
- inputs: platform (short or long form), offer and lead magnet, current posting volume.
- data: platform follower counts.
- upstream: H3, H1.
- downstream: none.
- depth: partial

### steps
1. Keep give:ask well above 3:1. [chapters/ch07-post-free-content-2.md]
2. Choose Integrated asks (long form) or Intermittent asks (short form). [chapters/ch07-post-free-content-2.md]
3. Choose the ask target: core offer or lead magnet (default lead magnet). [chapters/ch07-post-free-content-2.md]
4. Start with one CTA after a valuable moment, confirm growth does not slow, then add. [chapters/ch07-post-free-content-2.md]
5. Pick a scaling path: Depth-then-Width or Width-then-Depth; measure absolute and % growth monthly. [chapters/ch07-post-free-content-2.md]
### criteria
- Plan shows a give:ask ratio above 3:1. [chapters/ch07-post-free-content-2.md]
- Ask type matches format (integrated for long, intermittent for short). [chapters/ch07-post-free-content-2.md]
- Every ask is followed by give pieces. [chapters/ch07-post-free-content-2.md]
- Defines both an absolute and a % monthly growth metric. [chapters/ch07-post-free-content-2.md]
- Posts are not pre-scheduled. [chapters/ch07-post-free-content-2.md]

## B-005 H5. Cold outreach campaign
- ask: "Build a cold outreach campaign (list, scripts, follow-ups) for [ICP]."
- inputs: ICP criteria, offer or lead magnet, channels (phone, email, DM), LTGP.
- data: lead-list software or brokers, outreach/automation tool, CRM.
- upstream: H1 (Big Fast Value).
- downstream: H11 (reps), F5 overlaps.
- depth: full

### steps
1. Build the list: software/scraping, then brokers, then elbow grease; test a few hundred per source. [chapters/ch08-cold-outreach.md]
2. Personalize: 1-3 facts a friend would know + a compliment. [chapters/ch08-cold-outreach.md]
3. Lead with Big Fast Value: something people actually pay for, mind-blowing in under 30 seconds. [chapters/ch08-cold-outreach.md]
4. Use A-C-A once someone replies. [chapters/ch08-cold-outreach.md]
5. Follow up more times, more ways (channel switching); re-run the list after 3-6 months. [chapters/ch08-cold-outreach.md]
6. "Solve for X": back into daily volume from the customer goal using channel benchmarks. [chapters/ch08-cold-outreach.md]
### criteria
- First message opens with personalization, not an ask. [chapters/ch08-cold-outreach.md]
- The free value is something the market pays for. [chapters/ch08-cold-outreach.md]
- Sequence has more than one touch and more than one channel. [chapters/ch08-cold-outreach.md]
- States daily volume derived from a customer target. [chapters/ch08-cold-outreach.md]
- Defines tracked metrics per step (open, reply, engaged). [chapters/ch08-cold-outreach.md]
- Unit economics show LTGP at least 3x CAC. [chapters/ch08-cold-outreach.md]

## B-007 H7. Paid ads budget and unit-economics plan (CFA)
- ask: "How much should I spend on ads and when can I scale?"
- inputs: price, delivery cost, avg months retained, CAC, 30-day cash per customer, upsells, customer capacity.
- data: ad account spend and leads, sales/CRM revenue per customer.
- upstream: H6.
- downstream: I7, H10 (max CAC for payouts).
- depth: full

### steps
1. Set up tracking before spending. [chapters/ch10-run-paid-ads-2.md]
2. Compute LTGP and LTGP:CAC; target 3:1. [chapters/ch10-run-paid-ads-2.md]
3. Test budget: up to 2x 30-day cash per new ad; kill before 1x if zero leads. [chapters/ch10-run-paid-ads-2.md]
4. Engineer Client-Financed Acquisition: 30-day cash collected covers cost to get + fulfill (e.g. upsell). [chapters/ch10-run-paid-ads-2.md]
5. Diagnose: CAC below 3x industry average means fix LTGP; above means fix ads; qualified-not-buying means sales problem. [chapters/ch10-run-paid-ads-2.md]
6. Print Money: budget = customers you can handle x CAC + 20%. [chapters/ch10-run-paid-ads-2.md]
### criteria
- Shows LTGP, CAC and the ratio with formulas. [chapters/ch10-run-paid-ads-2.md]
- States a per-ad test cap and a kill threshold in money. [chapters/ch10-run-paid-ads-2.md]
- Shows 30-day cash vs CAC and the gap, with a named lever to close it. [chapters/ch10-run-paid-ads-2.md]
- Scale budget is reverse-computed from a customer goal with ~20% pad. [chapters/ch10-run-paid-ads-2.md]
- Names tracking as a precondition. [chapters/ch10-run-paid-ads-2.md]

## B-008 H8. Constraint test plan (More / Better / New)
- ask: "I'm stuck at X leads. What do I test next?"
- inputs: funnel step conversion rates, current volume, platforms used.
- data: funnel metrics from ad account / CRM.
- upstream: any running channel (H2, H5, H6).
- downstream: I9, I10.
- depth: full

### steps
1. More first: Rule of 100 for the current method. [chapters/ch11-core-four-on-steroids.md]
2. Map each step's conversion %; the largest drop-off is the constraint. [chapters/ch11-core-four-on-steroids.md]
3. One split test per platform per week, on the constraint; log results. [chapters/ch11-core-four-on-steroids.md]
4. If 4 tries cannot beat the best, move to the next constraint. [chapters/ch11-core-four-on-steroids.md]
5. New only after More+Better: new placement, then platform, then Core Four activity. [chapters/ch11-core-four-on-steroids.md]
### criteria
- Lists every funnel step with a conversion rate. [chapters/ch11-core-four-on-steroids.md]
- Names one constraint and the test targets it. [chapters/ch11-core-four-on-steroids.md]
- Each test changes one thing. [chapters/ch11-core-four-on-steroids.md]
- Has a results log and a 4-try exit rule. [chapters/ch11-core-four-on-steroids.md]
- Any "New" item follows placement, platform, activity order. [chapters/ch11-core-four-on-steroids.md]

## B-009 H9. Referral program
- ask: "Design a referral program for my customers."
- inputs: churn %, referral %, CAC, best-customer traits, product weak spots.
- data: CRM churn and referral source data.
- upstream: H7 (CAC).
- downstream: H13.
- depth: full

### steps
1. Compute Referral Growth Equation (referrals vs churn). [chapters/ch13-customer-referrals.md]
2. Raise goodwill with the six value levers (sell better customers, set better expectations, more people better results, faster wins, keep improving, tell them what to buy next). [chapters/ch13-customer-referrals.md]
3. Choose ask mechanics from the Seven Ways (one-sided, two-sided, at purchase, negotiation chip, events, ongoing, unlockable bonuses). [chapters/ch13-customer-referrals.md]

### criteria
- States baseline referral % and churn %. [chapters/ch13-customer-referrals.md]
- Incentive value is at or below CAC. [chapters/ch13-customer-referrals.md]
- Names at least one of the Seven Ways explicitly. [chapters/ch13-customer-referrals.md]
- Includes an ask at the moment of purchase. [chapters/ch13-customer-referrals.md]
- Includes a product-fix loop (top complaint monthly). [chapters/ch13-customer-referrals.md]
- Target of 25%+ of leads from referrals is stated. / [cheatsheet.md, chapters/ch17-get-started.md]
